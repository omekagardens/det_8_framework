**Recurring follow-through**

Use an hourly follow-up in this coordination task. At each run:

1. Read this plan, the progress record and QR handoff; inspect the current
   working tree and task statuses before editing or sending a new assignment.
2. Review new QR results when present. If QR is still working, preserve its
   reservations and advance an independent coordinator item. Do not restart,
   duplicate or interrupt an unchanged running assignment.
3. Complete a bounded implementation/proof-review item with appropriate checks.
   Reconcile findings into the progress record and update actual states here.
   At a reviewed source-stable boundary, make a scoped commit and push the
   update to the verified upstream; record its identity and remote result.
4. Send a concrete next QR assignment only when its predecessor is reviewed or
   a new result changes the needed direction. Keep the full record/residual,
   lawful sequencing, precursor and nontermination requirements explicit.
5. Surface decisions requiring missing user information, external data or
   authority with a precise reason. Continue other unblocked work. Do not
   promote a theorem, release, calibration, ontology or physical claim merely
   to close a row.

The cadence is a coordination interval, not an execution-time estimate or a
promise of continuous computation. Local scheduled work requires the computer,
application and checkout to remain available. Existing acceptance thresholds,

subject to their actual project-specific prerequisites. No broad staging,
