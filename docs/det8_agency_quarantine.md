# DET v8.0-P0.4 — Agency Quarantine Appendix

**Status:** M (metaphysical/interpretational)
**Date:** August 9, 2026
**Purpose:** Enforce the strict separation of agency from the physical core. Document what agency is, what it is not, and what would be required to promote it to physical status.

---

## 1. Quarantine Statement

Agency is status M throughout DET v8.0-P0.4. It shall not enter physical equations, serve as an explanatory resource, or be used to adjust predictions. Its consequences (actions, choices, orientations) may leave record-side traces; agency itself is not stored, measured, or modeled as a physical quantity.

---

## 2. What Agency Is (in DET)

Agency is proposed (M) as:

> A selfhood-bearing regime's present participation in actualizing a lawful coarse-grained orientation or act.

Key properties (all M):
- Agency belongs to the present (\(\mathfrak P_e\)), not to the record.
- Agency is not a force, energy, scalar capacity, random variable, or update coefficient.
- Agency is expressed in an event, not stored in matter.
- Agency is distinct from identity, coherence, consciousness, and complexity.

---

## 3. What Agency Is NOT

Agency must not be confused with:

| Concept | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Persistent relation across change. Can be modeled as record structure. | P |
| Coherence | Integration of a regime's components; measurable via \(C\). | P |
| Consciousness | Experiential dimension. Not modeled in DET physics. | M |
| Complexity | Structural richness of a regime. | P |
| Free will (philosophical) | The metaphysical capacity for libertarian choice. | M (philosophy, not physics) |
| Randomness | Stochastic variation. Modelable via kernel \(K_e\). | P/C |
| Indeterminacy | Non-singleton support \(|\Omega_e| > 1\). | P/C |
| Unpredictability | Epistemic limitation on prediction. | Epistemic, not ontic |
| Agency (stored) | `a_i`, agency energy, agency charge, choice field. | **FORBIDDEN** |

## 4. Forbidden Variables

The following must NOT appear in any DET physical equation or state vector:

- `a_i` (agency scalar)
- `will_coefficient`
- `agency_strength`
- `choice_field`
- `actualization_bias`
- `spirit_force`
- `intention_energy`
- `free_will_parameter`
- `consciousness_field`
- Any variable whose sole purpose is to represent "what the agent chose"

---

## 5. What May Appear in the Record

The following are records and may be modeled:

- Intentions previously formed (declared plans, commitments).
- Habits (learned behavioral patterns).
- Memories (stored experiential traces).
- Preferences (declared or inferred value orderings).
- Motor readiness (preparatory neural/muscular state).
- Attention state (focus allocation).
- Biological viability (health, energy, structural integrity).
- Learned policy (reinforcement history, skill).
- Relational commitments (promises, covenants).
- Recorded assent or refusal (past decisions).
- Structural openness (capacity for novel response, modeled as high \(|\Omega|\) in certain regimes).

These strongly constrain and help predict an act. **None is identical to the present act itself.**

## 7. Promotion Criteria

For agency to be promoted from M to H (research hypothesis):

1. **Operational definition:** An operational criterion for identifying "agent-involving" systems that does not presuppose the very agency it aims to detect.
2. **Causal interface:** A specification of how agency couples to the record — what it changes, when, and under what lawful constraints.
3. **Distinctive prediction:** A measurable record-side signature that would be absent in a non-agentic system with the same record-side structure.
4. **Pre-registered test:** Null model, statistic, threshold, and failure condition declared prospectively.
5. **Compatibility:** The proposal must not violate causal locality, conservation, or the No Pre-Existing Future Facts invariant.

For promotion from H to P (proposed core):

- The distinctive prediction must survive at least one pre-registered experimental test.
- The causal interface must be integrated with the formal core without ad hoc exceptions.

## 8. Simulation Rule

DET simulations simulate records, lawful possibility structures, predicted outcome distributions, and explicit computational actualizers. They do NOT simulate agency.

The computational actualizer (pseudorandom sampler) is a numerical gauge, not a claim about what ontological becoming or agency actually is. This distinction is explicit in code and documentation:

- `Actualizer` (MAM-0): docstring states "seed is for reproducibility only."
- `QubitActualizer` (MAM-Q): docstring states "must not be interpreted as a physical hidden variable."

## 9. Anti-Smuggling Checklist

Before any DET equation or model is accepted, verify:

- [ ] No variable named `a_i`, `will`, `choice`, `agency`, `intention`, `spirit`, `consciousness`.
- [ ] No variable whose operational definition is "what the agent contributes."
- [ ] No term that cannot be measured, computed from the record, or clearly labeled M.
- [ ] No Boundary operator that directly writes an agentic outcome.
- [ ] No hidden random seed interpreted as physical ontology.
- [ ] No "agency did it" explanation for any physical result.
