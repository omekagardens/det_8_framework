## 2. Four Deadlocks Resolved

### 2.1 The Time Deadlock (Block Universe vs. Open Future)

**Standard physics deadlock:** Relativity implies a "block universe" where past, present, and future all exist equally in a static four-dimensional manifold. This leads to fatalism: the future is as fixed as the past. The experienced present is an illusion.

**DET resolution:** The past is determinate record (\(\mathcal R^-\)). The future is open lawful support (\(\Omega\)). The present is the commit event (\(X_e \sim K_e\), interpreted as \(\mathfrak P_e\) at status M). Time is not a dimension we move through; it is the asymmetric, irreversible growth of the determinate record through commit events.

**Why this matters:** DET restores the reality of the present and the openness of the future without violating relativistic causal structure. No other interpretation does this without either denying relativity or denying becoming.

---

### 2.2 The Quantum Deadlock (Reification vs. Ignorance)

**Standard physics deadlock:** Everett (Many-Worlds) reifies unactualized possibilities into parallel physical universes — a massive ontological inflation. Bohmian mechanics hides them in pilot waves. Copenhagen refuses to talk about them, leaving the measurement problem unresolved.

**DET resolution:** A quantum superposition is an actual, phase-bearing relational constraint on future possibilities. It is a real, open relation that does not contain its own outcome. Amplitudes are present structural features (P/C), not pre-existing degrees of belief in hidden outcomes. The quantum state is an "actual relational record" — determinate as a relation but open as to its pointer outcome.

**Why this matters:** DET allows quantum states to be physically real without multiplying universes or hiding outcomes in inaccessible variables. NPF-C (the construction invariant) is code-auditable: no hidden outcome register exists in the toy models. This is a clean ontological position that neither Copenhagen nor Many-Worlds achieves.

---

### 2.3 The Agency Deadlock (Physicalism vs. Dualism)

**Standard physics deadlock:** If matter is deterministic or purely stochastic, agency is an illusion (epiphenomenalism). If agency is real, it must be a "ghost force" that injects energy or violates conservation laws (dualism). There is no third option in standard ontology.

**DET resolution:** Agency is not a stored physical variable, nor is it a force, energy source, stochastic disturbance, or hidden control register. It is a selfhood-bearing regime's present participation in actualizing a lawful coarse-grained orientation. Disposition (intentions, habits, memories, preferences) is record — modelable, predictable, but not identical to agency. Agency is present enactment (M).

**Why this matters:** DET provides the first rigorous ontological framework where agency can be real and causally decisive without requiring it to inject energy, violate conservation, or appear as a term in a differential equation. This resolves the physicalism/dualism deadlock without either denying agency or smuggling it into physics.

---

### 2.4 The History/Trauma Deadlock (Fixed Past vs. Healing)

**Standard physics deadlock:** The past is fixed. You cannot change what happened. Therefore, "healing," "forgiveness," or "recovery" are subjective psychological overlays on an unchanging objective reality. There is no physical basis for the past losing its grip.

**DET resolution:** The past event is fixed as an occurrence — it happened and cannot be retroactively undone. But its present structural carrying (mutable κ) can change. κ-recovery does not retroactively edit history; it releases the structural drag of the past in the present. The event remains real; its current constraint is reduced.

**Why this matters:** DET provides a rigorous ontological basis for recovery, forgiveness, and healing without resorting to retrocausality or denying the reality of past events. The past is real, but how heavily it weighs on the present can change. This is a genuine ontological innovation with implications far beyond physics.

## 3. Track B Is Not "Pre-Physics"

A common dismissal: ontology is "just philosophy" — pre-physics that will eventually be replaced by equations.

DET's response: **Ontology is the conceptual scaffolding that makes sense of the physics.** The Copenhagen interpretation, Many-Worlds, and QBism are all ontological frameworks built on the same physical calculus (the Schrödinger equation). None of them produces a unique falsifiable equation. They are all interpretations. And yet:

- Copenhagen shaped 80 years of how physicists think about measurement.
- Many-Worlds reshaped cosmology and quantum computing.
- QBism reframed quantum probabilities as personalist Bayesian degrees of belief.

DET's ontological grammar is comparably ambitious and comparably rigorous. It replaces:
- Block universe → record-growth time
- Many worlds / hidden variables → open relational constraints
- Epiphenomenalism / dualism → present-enactment agency
- Retrocausality → mutable structural carrying (κ)

**We reclaim this classification.** In the philosophy of physics, an interpretive framework is a vital, major category:

DET belongs in this company. It proposes a disciplined ontological grammar that is:
