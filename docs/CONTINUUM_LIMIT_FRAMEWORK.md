# DET8 Continuum-Limit Legacy Quarantine

**Status:** RETIRED historical program

**Active replacement:** T7 order-and-count correspondence, with continuum emergence open

**Executable ledger:** `det8/models/legacy_gravity_quarantine.py`

## Why this document was replaced

The former continuum-limit document mixed four distinct levels:

1. coordinates and observables supplied by a Minkowski/de Sitter generator;
2. empirical quantities computed from those generated coordinates;
3. formal estimators acting on an order or point sample;
4. interpretations in which Π fixed a conformal factor and κ supplied matter
   or gravitational stress-energy.

Finite recovery of a geometry deliberately inserted by a generator is a
synthetic correspondence check. It is not evidence that DET dynamics generate
a manifold, that Π is physical volume, that κ is a continuum field, or that an
Einstein equation emerges. The earlier document repeatedly crossed those
boundaries, so its theorem and gravity claims are quarantined rather than
hardened.

## Quarantined chain

| Historical step | What the code directly does | Why it is retired |
|---|---|---|
| Π-weighted measure | Samples coordinates from a supplied continuum region and reweights them | Π-to-volume identification is assumed |
| Π-weighted metric | Fits metric components using supplied coordinates and target geometry | The target metric/embedding enters the construction |
| κ-field limit | Smooths samples of a supplied continuum scalar | Interpolation does not establish a native field or its dynamics |
| curvature/LGH | Evaluates finite proxies against supplied geometries | No analytic convergence theorem is proved |
| κ Poisson equation | Maps κ or γ to a Newtonian source | The κ-gravity identification was withdrawn |
| κ-weighted BD action | Modifies a known causal-set action by κ weights | The weighting and Einstein limit are not derived |
| Bianchi/Einstein claims | Checks local arithmetic or stationarity in constructed examples | These checks do not prove Bianchi conservation or field-equation emergence |

All `continuum_limit_*.py` files from this chain, plus the old `o7_*.py`
derivation chain, carry a machine-readable `QUARANTINE` record. Their permitted
use is historical reproduction and regression testing only.

## Rewritten, revivable content

Two narrow components remain useful after reclassification:

- `order_count_geometry.py` is a **synthetic correspondence test**. It samples
  points from an explicitly supplied Minkowski diamond and tests causal-order
  dimension, null-structure, and scale estimators. It does not establish
  background-free emergence.
- `lorentz_derivation.py` is a **known-answer special-relativity check**. It
  supplies Minkowski coordinates, the interval, invariant speed, and Lorentz
  boosts, then verifies the standard identities. Its historical `derive_*`
  names are compatibility names, not epistemic claims.

The active T7 question is therefore deliberately smaller:

> Given a finite causal record and declared sampling assumptions, which
> geometric quantities are identifiable from order and count, with what bias,
> variance, degeneracies, and failure diagnostics?

That question can strengthen causal-set estimators without claiming that the
record was generated by DET or that gravity has emerged.

## Re-entry gate

A quarantined gravity or continuum claim can return only as a new module that:

1. names its direct instrument record or explicitly declares a synthetic
   generator;
2. separates derived empirical quantities from formal representations;
3. states every borrowed geometric and physical assumption;
4. provides a result not fixed by calibration to the target theory;
5. survives equivalence-principle, conservation, covariance, and identifiability
   checks appropriate to the claim; and
6. registers a discriminator before comparison with data.

Passing historical regression tests is not a re-entry criterion.

## Current verification

Run `python3 run_tests.py`. The integrated suite checks the executable
quarantine boundary and internal arithmetic. It does not validate a continuum
limit, the physical existence of κ or Π, or any DET theory of gravity.
