## The exact remaining comparison and its improvement

Set D=89+72theta, so Z=9/D, and retain all positive same-law coefficients.
Equation(11) becomes exactly

    I(Z)=-1+972lambda*G_*/[D^2*q(Z)]+B_Z/v,
    B_Z=108lambda*j_0/(80+72theta)
       =27lambda*j_0/(20+18theta).                   (13)

Positive clearing gives the complete remaining condition

    I(Z)<=0 iff
      v>B_Z and q(Z)>=972lambda*G_*v/[D^2*(v-B_Z)].   (14)

The strict v>B_Z branch is required before division. At v=B_Z the
positive q term makes I(Z)>0; v<B_Z also cannot pass. Equality in the
q comparison is retained and gives the unattained-zero case above.

B_Z<12lambda*j_0*R/(1-R). If I(R)<=0, then I(Z)<0. The converse is not
asserted. On the strict-capacity branch v>12lambda*j_0*R/(1-R), both
positive contributions to I are strictly smaller at Z than at R.

endpoint test succeeds. No actual endpoint value is computed.
