companion masses and strictly lowers the primary objective. The move

carrying Q row is slack. Thus a tight Q row is a genuine new obstruction,
not an omitted cost or an assumed free capacity.

## The requested mass preserving signed move

All other original rows are unchanged, including P,Y,V5 and the other

For a positive step it is necessary and sufficient that

    xi_0>0, xi_1>0, and S_Q(j,m,k)>0 for every Q record. (R5)

Necessity follows from coordinate nonnegativity and the strictly

    0<u<=min { n xi_0/h, n xi_1/h,
                       min_(j,m,k) S_Q(j,m,k)/v }.     (R6)

## Compensate only where the companion is tight

Choose any subset M of {0,1}, and change

    Delta eta=u>0;
    Delta xi_m=-(h/n)u for m in M;
    Delta xi_m=0 for m outside M.                      (R9)

For m in M the companion mass is unchanged. For m outside M its
mass increases by h u. All Q rows increase by v u, and only P_nu
rows whose m lies in M decrease. No other row changes.

This chosen direction has a positive feasible step iff

    xi_m>0 for every m in M;
    S_m>0 for every m outside M;
    all carrying S_Q>0.                               (R10)

In addition to the Q bounds, retain n xi_m/h for all m in M and
S_m/h for every m outside M. The finite minimum is positive exactly
under (R10). Empty subsets entail no fictitious coordinate bound;
the nonempty finite Q family supplies a positive finite upper bound.

Its exact primary change is

    Delta Phi=v h u(2|M|-5)/160<0,                    (R11)

since |M| is zero, one or two. No zero-cost endpoint is hidden.

There exists a feasible strict descent in this subset family iff

    all Q rows are slack AND every xi_m with m in T_l
    is strictly positive.                             (R12)

For sufficiency choose M=T_l; every uncompensated row is then slack.
For necessity, any feasible M must contain T_l and its xi allocations
must be positive. A zero xi on a slack companion row does not block
the choice M=T_l, whereas a zero xi on a tight row does.

Thus the canonical point, already a primary optimum, must obey the
localized support/capacity obstruction

    some Q row is tight
       OR some m in T_l has xi_m=0.                    (R13)

If T_l is empty, the second alternative is false and Q must bind:
otherwise eta alone could increase at strict primary gain. No positive
eta assumption is needed for this conclusion.
