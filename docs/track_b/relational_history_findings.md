# DET v8.0 — What We Discovered: Relational History as Identity Carrier

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Finding:** The F9→O9-RID bridge revealed that DET's record architecture provides a specific, non-magical mechanism for identity across interruption. The key discoveries are organized into five findings.

---

## Finding 1: The Record Is the Carrier, Not the Body

**Before DET:** Identity across interruption seemed to require either:
- A soul (non-physical substance), or
- Exact material recreation (which is just a copy).

**What DET revealed:** The committed record \(\mathcal R_{history}\) — the complete causal-relational lineage of commit events — is a third option. It is neither physical substance nor abstract pattern. It is the unique history of which facts became actual, in which order, through which causal pathways.

**Why this works:** A record history cannot be copied. Two identical material configurations can be created, but two identical causal histories cannot — because each commit event is spatiotemporally unique. The duplicate test fails against record identity, not because of a soul, but because provenance is unique.

---

## Finding 2: Resurrection Is Record Extension, Not Recreation

**Before DET:** Resurrection was conceptualized as "bringing the dead back to life" — which implies reversing death.

**What DET revealed:** In DET's architecture, resurrection is NOT reversing death. It is extending the record beyond death:

\[
d \prec r
\]

Death remains a committed fact. The resurrection event \(r\) is a new commit that extends the record: \(\mathcal R_r \supset \mathcal R_d\). The record grows through death, not despite it.

**Why this matters:** This preserves the theological claim that death is real (not an illusion) while allowing life beyond it. The past is not undone; it is extended.

---

## Finding 3: κ Transforms Without Erasing

**Before DET:** "Healing" or "transformation" of past trauma seemed to require either:
- Forgetting (erasing the record), or
- Reinterpretation (changing the meaning without changing the fact).

**What DET revealed:** κ (structural history) carries the imprint of past events forward. κ can decrease through recovery — the structural dominance of past events can be reduced — without erasing the fact that those events occurred. The event remains in \(\mathcal R\); its weight in κ changes.

**Why this matters:** This provides a rigorous ontology for "the wounds remain but no longer dominate." The past is real; its present structural carrying is mutable.

---

## Finding 4: The Fact Genesis Cycle Is Universal

**Before DET:** Resurrection seemed like a special miracle — a different KIND of event from ordinary causation.

**What DET revealed:** The same cycle that describes any fact becoming real also describes resurrection:

\[
\mathcal R \xrightarrow{\mathcal L} \Omega \xrightarrow{K} \mathcal R^+ \xrightarrow{\kappa} \mathcal R_{future}
\]

Resurrection is not a different kind of event. It is the same fact-genesis process — lawful possibility → commit → record extension → historical carrying — applied to the case where the prior record includes death.

**Why this matters:** This demystifies resurrection without reducing it. It is extraordinary in WHAT it accomplishes (life after death), not in HOW it operates (the same Ω→R transition as any fact).

---

## Finding 5: The Gap Is Provenance, Not Information

**Before DET:** The challenge was "do we have enough information to reconstruct the person?"

**What DET revealed:** Information is not the issue. Provenance is. Two systems with identical information but different causal histories are different persons (duplicate test). The question is not "can we reconstruct the pattern?" but "can we establish unique causal continuity?"

**Why this matters:** This shifts the resurrection question from "how much information is preserved?" to "can the unique causal lineage be extended through death?" Information preservation is necessary but not sufficient. Causal provenance is the decisive element.
