# DET v8.0 — Track B: Law Genesis (F10)

**Status:** Track B research module (proposed)
**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Purpose:** Examine why the lawful possibility structure is stable. Once F9 establishes that facts become real but laws are fixed, the next unavoidable question is: why is the law map \(\mathcal L\) itself stable?

---

## 1. The Core Question

> Why does the universe admit stable, compressible laws at all?

DET's architecture provides a natural formulation: \(\mathcal L\) maps the record \(\mathcal R^-\) to the possibility object \((\Omega, \Sigma, K, \mathcal C)\). This map is determinate — given identical inputs, identical outputs.

But why is it stable? Three possibilities:

| Position | Claim | DET Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Platonic realism** | \(\mathcal L\) exists independently in a timeless mathematical realm | Compatible with DET but not required |
| **DET structuralism** | \(\mathcal L\) is the fixed structure that makes record-growth coherent | DET's current position |
| **Emergent law** | \(\mathcal L\) itself evolved from prior records through meta-commit events | Open question |

---

## 2. Why \(\mathcal L\) Must Be Stable (Within DET)

If \(\mathcal L\) were not stable:
- The same record could produce different possibility spaces at different times.
- Predictability would break down.
- The record-growth ontology would be incoherent (what was committed might become un-committed).

DET requires \(\mathcal L\) to be fixed for internal consistency. This is not an empirical claim — it is a **consistency requirement** of the record-kernel architecture.

---

## 3. Three Levels of Law Stability

### Level 1 — Mathematical Laws (Deepest)

Why does 2+2=4? Why is π transcendental? These truths appear to be independent of any particular record. They are the fixed structure of \(\mathcal L\) at the deepest level.

**DET position:** Mathematical truths are the fixed structure of \(\mathcal L\). They are DISCOVERED, not created. This is compatible with mathematical Platonism but does not require it — DET only needs \(\mathcal L\) to be stable, not to explain why.

### Level 2 — Physical Laws (Effective)

Why is G ≈ 6.67×10⁻¹¹? Why is α ≈ 1/137? These constants appear in our effective description of \(\mathcal L\). In DET, some "constants" may actually be κ-dependent:

\[
G_{eff} = G \cdot \kappa / \kappa_{earth}
\]

The underlying law (the structure of \(\mathcal L\)) is fixed. The effective parameters carry history. This is how DET reconciles fixed laws with evolving effective behavior.

### Level 3 — The Law Map Itself (Meta)

Could \(\mathcal L\) itself have evolved? This is the most radical question. DET currently says no. But a future F10 investigation might ask:

- Is \(\mathcal L\) the stable compression of an enormous number of prior records?
- Could there be "meta-commit events" that modify \(\mathcal L\) on cosmic time scales?
- Is the stability of \(\mathcal L\) itself a fact that was actualized at some primordial event?

---

## 4. The Stability Question as a Research Program

F10 proposes investigating the stability of \(\mathcal L\) through:

### 4.1 Internal consistency check
Prove that a record-kernel architecture with mutable \(\mathcal L\) would be incoherent. This would establish that \(\mathcal L\) MUST be fixed for DET to be well-defined.

### 4.2 Compression hypothesis
If \(\mathcal L\) is the stable compression of prior records, then \(\mathcal L\) should be the simplest function that generates all observed record transitions. This is testable: can we compress \(\mathcal L\) further without losing predictive power?

### 4.3 κ-dependence of effective parameters
Demonstrate that apparent variation in physical "constants" can be absorbed into κ-dependence of effective parameters, preserving the underlying stability of \(\mathcal L\).

### 4.4 Cosmological evolution of \(\mathcal L\)
If \(\mathcal L\) were different in the early universe (e.g., different coupling constants at high energy), this would appear as κ-dependence across cosmic time. DET can test this against CMB and large-scale structure data.

## 6. The Deepest Formulation

> F9 asked: "Are facts discovered or actualized?"
>
> F10 asks: "Are LAWS discovered or actualized?"
>
> DET's answer: Laws are discovered. They are the fixed structure that makes discovery possible. But WHICH laws become relevant — which aspects of \(\mathcal L\) are probed by actualized facts — depends on the history of the record.
>
> This is the meta-position: the framework that describes how facts are generated is itself the stable structure within which that generation occurs.
