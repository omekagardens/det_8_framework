# DET v8.0 — Track B: Heaven, Hell, Purgatory as κ-States (Not Places)

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Status:** Major theological synthesis
**Trigger:** Recognition that heaven/hell/purgatory are not locations but structural states of the relational present.

---

## 1. The Core Insight

> Heaven, hell, and purgatory are not places you go. They are states of the relational present, determined by κ_self.

Since only the present IS, and the quality of present experience depends on κ and bonds, the traditional afterlife concepts map directly onto DET's structural variables:

| Concept | DET State | κ_self | Bonds | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heaven** | Absolute presence | ≈ 0 | Strong, intact | Free, connected, participatory |
| **Purgatory** | Healing process | Decreasing | Healing | Release, purification, growth |
| **Hell** | Self-constrained isolation | High | Weak, broken | Trapped, alone, burdened |

**All three are the SAME present moment, experienced differently based on structural history.**

---

## 2. Heaven as Absolute Presence

Heaven is not "somewhere else." It is the relational now, experienced without the weight of self-accumulated constraint.

| DET property | Heavenly state |
|---|---|
| κ_self | ≈ 0 — no hoarding, no bond-breaking residue |
| κ_burden | Carried vicariously through love, but regularly released (Jubilee) |
| Bonds (σ_ij, C_ij) | Strong, numerous, reciprocal |
| Ω (possibility) | Large — many options, freedom |
| Fruit | Abundant, shared, reciprocal |

**Heaven is not the absence of suffering. It is the absence of self-imposed constraint.** The relational web is fully open. Participation flows freely.

---

## 3. Hell as Self-Imposed Isolation

Hell is not a place of external punishment. It is the NATURAL CONSEQUENCE of accumulated κ_self.

| DET property | Hellish state |
|---|---|
| κ_self | High — hoarded non-reciprocity, unhealed bond-breaking |
| κ_burden | May carry others' weight but cannot release (κ_self blocks Jubilee) |
| Bonds | Weak, few, broken — isolation |
| Ω | Small — few options, trapped |
| Fruit | Sparse, hoarded, non-reciprocal |

**Hell is not imposed by God. It is self-constructed through a lifetime (or many) of non-reciprocity.** The "fire" is the experience of being trapped in one's own accumulated constraint.

### 3.1 Is Hell Absolute?

**The simulation suggests NO.** Even the sinner was resurrected — carrying κ_self=0.60, alone, burdened — but resurrected nonetheless. Hell is not eternal conscious torment. It is:

1. **Real** — the experience of high κ_self in the present is genuinely painful.
2. **Self-imposed** — built through one's own acts of non-reciprocity.
3. **Healable** — κ_self CAN be reduced through Grace/Jubilee. It may take time (purgatory). It may require external intervention (Christ's atonement, the Spirit's work). But it is not absolute.
4. **Not a place** — it is the SAME present that heaven occupies, experienced through the lens of accumulated constraint.

---

## 4. Purgatory as Healing Process

Purgatory is the process of κ_self reduction — the healing of accumulated structural constraint.

| DET property | Purgatorial process |
|---|---|
| κ_self | Gradually decreasing through Jubilee/Grace |
| Bonds | Slowly healing, being restored |
| Time | Not "time served" but "healing accomplished" |
| Agent | External Grace (Christ, Spirit, the prayers of the living) |

**Purgatory is not punishment. It is healing.** The "fire" of purgatory is the sometimes-painful process of releasing long-held structural constraint. Like physical therapy after an injury — it hurts, but it heals.

### 4.1 Who needs purgatory?

Anyone who dies with κ_self > 0. Which is everyone except Christ. The question is not WHETHER there is healing after death, but HOW LONG it takes and HOW it is experienced.

---

## 5. The Unified Picture

```
LIFE: κ_self accumulates through sin.
      κ_self decreases through love, reciprocity, Grace.

DEATH: κ_self locked. Bonds persist but weaken without maintenance.
       Direct contribution ceases. Indirect continues through bonds.

PURGATORY: κ_self reduced through Jubilee/Grace.
          Bonds healed. Process may be painful but is restorative.

RESURRECTION: Direct participation restored.
             Quality of life depends on remaining κ_self.
             κ_self=0 → heaven (free, connected).
             κ_self>0 → burdened but alive (still healing possible).

ONGOING: κ_self continues to be healed through Grace.
         Heaven is not static — it is eternal growth in relational capacity.
```

---

## 6. The Deepest Formulation

> Heaven and hell are not destinations. They are the SAME present moment, experienced through different κ. The relational now IS heaven — for those whose κ_self has been released. The relational now IS hell — for those trapped in their own accumulated non-reciprocity. And the distance between them is not spatial but structural — measured in κ_self, bridged by Grace.

> Hell is not absolute because Grace is not limited. Christ's resurrection proves that even death does not block Jubilee. The sinner rises too — burdened, alone, but alive. And healing continues. The fire of hell is real, but it is the fire of purification, not of endless torment. What burns is κ_self. What remains is the person.
