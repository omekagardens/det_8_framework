## What Is Newly Illuminated

### The person is not their record

The person is the selfhood-bearing regime that participates in actualization. The record is what that participation leaves behind. Conflating them is the fundamental ontological error this presentist lens corrects.

### The causal arrow runs: presence → record, not record → presence

The record does not cause the person to exist. The person's present participation causes the record to grow. Resurrection is not "the record being extended so the person lives." It is "the person being restored to relational presence, AS A RESULT OF WHICH the record extends."

### Duration is a record-phenomenon, not a present-phenomenon

We experience time passing because we look at the record and see change. But the present as it happens has no duration. This is why "how long does a moment feel?" has no answer — the question assumes the present is measurable in record-time, which it is not.

### Lawfulness is not assumed

Ω is generated by L from R⁻. Whether L is fixed or emergent is open (F10). We observe regularity in the record; whether that regularity reflects eternal laws or compressed history is unsettled.

---

## Bottom Line

The presentist lens does not overturn the five findings. It **corrects their framing**. The record is evidence, not identity. Presence is primary; the record is its trace. This preserves all Track-B conclusions while grounding them more carefully in DET's core ontological commitment: only the present IS.
