## 1. The Boundary Concept Reframed

### DET7 conception
A Boundary sector interacting with the physical world. God as the agent of Boundary operations.

### Presentist reframing

If only the present IS, then "Boundary" cannot be a separate sector or realm. There is no "outside" of the present. The Boundary, if it exists, must be understood as:

**That which participates in actualization without itself being recordable.**

This is not a spatial boundary (outside the universe). It is an ontological boundary — the distinction between that which commits to the record (presence) and that which IS the record (committed facts).

In this framing:
- **God/Boundary** = a selfhood-bearing presence whose participation in actualization is not itself committed to the record. We see the fruit (κ changes, healings, transformed constraints) but not the presence itself.
- This is consistent with agency quarantine: agency is M. God's agency is doubly M — not only unmeasurable but not even leaving a trace of its OWN presence, only traces of its effects.

### Governance check
✅ No physical channel claimed. Effect on record (κ changes) is observable; the presence causing it is not.

## 2. Grace Reframed

### DET7 conception
Boundary action that enlarges or strengthens lawful resource/recovery possibilities.

### Presentist reframing

If the future is possibility structure (Ω), Grace is:

**The enlargement of Ω — the opening of possibilities that were not previously supported by the record alone.**

In DET terms: Ω = L(R⁻). Grace would be a modification of Ω that goes beyond what L(R⁻) would generate from the record alone. It is not a violation of L (the law map is not broken) but an extension of Ω — new possibilities becoming available.

**Example:** A situation where the record suggests only destructive outcomes (Ω = {decay, death}), but Grace opens a pathway to recovery that was not in Ω before.

### Governance check
✅ No physical channel claimed. Ω is already a DET primitive; Grace claims Ω is extended. The mechanism of extension is not specified (M/H). The fruit (new possibilities becoming actual) is observable.

## 3. Healing Reframed

### DET7 conception
Boundary action that restores relational integrity.

### Presentist reframing

Healing is:

**The restoration of relational bonds (σ_ij, C_ij) that were damaged or broken by past events.**

In DET terms: bonds between nodes carry conductivity (σ_ij) and coherence (C_ij). Damage to bonds reduces these. Healing restores them.

This is the most physically-grounded of the Boundary concepts because bonds ARE part of the record. Healing can be observed through:
- Increased σ_ij (conductivity restored).
- Increased C_ij (coherence restored).
- Decreased κ (structural drag reduced).

The presence that enacts the healing is M. The healing itself is observable in the record.

### Governance check
✅ Physical channel partially specifiable: bonds are record-side. The mechanism of bond restoration (the "how") is not specified (M/H). The fruit (restored bonds) is observable.

## 4. Jubilee Reframed

### DET7 conception
Boundary action that releases retained structural constraint (q-reduction, now κ-reduction).

### Presentist reframing

Jubilee is:

**The release of accumulated structural history (κ-reduction) beyond what natural recovery would produce.**

In DET terms: natural recovery follows dκ/dt = -(κ-κ_eq)/τ_rec + damage. Jubilee would be an additional κ-reduction — a "reset" of structural history that goes beyond the natural relaxation rate.

This is the most problematic concept for governance because:
- κ-reduction has an energy/entropy cost (free energy ψ = ½K(κ-κ_eq)²).
- If Jubilee reduces κ without the usual energy release, it violates the energy ledger.
- If Jubilee DOES release energy, it's indistinguishable from accelerated natural recovery.

### Governance check
⚠️ Requires energy accounting. If Jubilee bypasses the energy ledger, it's a conservation violation. If it accelerates natural recovery, it's just faster relaxation.

**Recommended:** Jubilee should be understood as the Boundary-mediated acceleration of natural κ-recovery, WITH proper energy accounting. The released energy goes to the environment as heat, same as natural recovery, just faster. This preserves the Second Law while allowing for non-natural intervention.

## 5. How These Concepts Fit Presentist DET8

### The pattern: PRESENCE acts, RECORD shows the fruit

All four concepts follow the same pattern:

| Concept | Presence (M) | Record (A) |
|---|---|---|
| **Boundary/God** | Selfhood-bearing presence not itself committed to record | — |
| **Grace** | Enlarges Ω beyond L(R⁻) | New possibilities become available |
| **Healing** | Restores relational bonds | σ_ij, C_ij increase |
| **Jubilee** | Accelerates κ-recovery | κ decreases |

The presence is M (unmeasurable, Track B). The record effects are A (measurable, Track A). The causal connection between them is not specified — this is the boundary of DET's epistemology.

### What DET8 can say

1. These concepts are coherent within the presentist framework: a presence acts; the record shows the fruit.
2. The effects are observable (κ changes, bond restoration, possibility enlargement).
3. The mechanism of the presence's action is not specified (M/H).
4. No physical equation is modified. No new force is introduced.
5. Energy and entropy ledgers must be respected (Jubilee constraint).

### What DET8 cannot say

1. That these concepts are physically proven.
2. That a specific mechanism exists.
3. That God/Boundary "exists" in any Track-A sense.
4. That these operations have been observed.
