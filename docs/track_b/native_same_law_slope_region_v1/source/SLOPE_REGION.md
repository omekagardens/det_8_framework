## 3. Exact normalized slope equivalence

## 4. A monotone threshold valid on the entire x interval

Abbreviate A_s(x)=(1+eta)(j_0+G_*x)>0 and define

    F(x)=x(1-x)A_s(x)/U30(x).                           (12)

This F is only a slope-comparison function, not one of the earlier F_i
complete-parent cap sums. It is strictly increasing on 0<x<=R. Indeed

    [x(1-x)(j_0+G_*x)]'
       =j_0(1-2x)+G_*x(2-3x)>0,
    U30'(x)=-1-(1-2x)V_0<0.                           (13)

The numerator and denominator are positive; a strictly increasing positive
numerator divided by a strictly decreasing positive denominator increases.
No derivative is taken with respect to an actual selectable scale or law.

By definition T_R=lambda j_0/F(R). If v<=T_R, then for every x<=R

    lambda j_0/v >= F(R) >= F(x),
    lambda j_0 U30(x)/[(1-x)v] >= x A_s(x).             (14)

The last negative term in (11) alone dominates its positive term. The
remaining strictly negative q term therefore proves b(x)<0. This holds
even at v=T_R and x=R, where (14) can be equality. For x<R the monotonicity
already makes the relevant endpoint comparison strict. The proof does
not constrain q to a hypothetical free value or assume an extra q floor.

## 6. Precisely what remains beyond the threshold

If v>T_R, strict monotonicity and F(x)->0 as x tends to zero give exactly
one x_c in (0,R) satisfying F(x_c)=lambda j_0/v. This is a symbolic
existence and uniqueness statement; no root is computed or declared to
equal the actual rho. Put

    Psi(x)=x A_s(x)-lambda j_0 U30(x)/[(1-x)v].         (20)

Its sign is the sign of F(x)-lambda j_0/v. Thus Psi<=0 on (0,x_c]
and Psi>0 on (x_c,R]. Formula (11) proves b<0 on the first interval.
On the second, positive clearing gives the exact remaining comparison

    b(x)<=0  iff
    q(x)<=lambda G_*x U20(x)/Psi(x).                  (21)

Strict inequality in (21) gives b<0; equality gives b=0; reversal gives
b>0. This is an **upper** comparison on the complete canonical-corrected
q in (9), not another lower-floor argument. It must be justified using
the actual same-law terms (9),(10); no sign is assigned to their separate
contrasts. Merely knowing q>0 does not settle (21).

The simpler gap v>8B does not itself put the law in this final regime:
the exact theorem still covers 8B<v<=T_R. At v=T_R the possible crossing
is x=R and strict negativity still follows from the q term. No actual
branch among these alternatives is selected in this manuscript.
