# DET v8.0 — Ontological Correction: Record vs Person

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Status:** Governance correction to Track-B findings
**Trigger:** Concern that elevating the record to immutable identity carrier crosses the Track A/B barrier.

---

## 1. The Concern

By treating the record (\(\mathcal R\)) as the carrier of identity across death, are we:

1. Making the record into a metaphysical substance — a replacement for the soul?
2. Crossing the Track A/B barrier by giving a Track-A object (the record) Track-B status (identity carrier)?
3. Confusing the map (the record) with the territory (the actual person)?

**Answer: Yes — this is a real risk. The findings need a governance correction.**

---

## 2. What the Record IS (Track A)

The record \(\mathcal R\) is:
- Committed facts — the physical traces of past events.
- Measurable — κ, N, bond structure, causal order.
- Track A — part of the physical calculus.
- NOT a person. NOT an identity. NOT a soul.

The record is what events **leave behind**. It is the fruit of becoming, not becoming itself.

---

## 3. What the Record Is NOT

| The record is NOT | Why |
|---|---|
| The person | The person is a selfhood-bearing regime that participates in actualization. The record is the trace of that participation. |
| Identity itself | Identity is carried THROUGH the record, not stored IN the record. |
| A soul-substitute | The record is physical (Track A). A soul would be non-physical (Track B/M). DET does not replace souls with records. |
| Immutable | The record GROWS (new commits add to it). κ CHANGES (recovery reduces structural carrying). The record is dynamic, not frozen. |
| The ground of being | The record depends on prior commit events. It is contingent, not necessary. |

---

## 4. The Correct Formulation

**Original (risky):** "The record IS the carrier of identity."

**Corrected:** "The record is the EVIDENCE of identity. Unique causal provenance can be TRACKED through the record. But the person is not reducible to their record."

This preserves the Track A/B distinction:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{PID-C (Track A):}&\quad \text{Unique causal lineage is TRACEABLE through the record.} \\
\text{PID-M (Track B):}&\quad \text{The later person IS the same person (metaphysical claim).}
\end{aligned}
}
\]

PID-C does not claim the record IS the person. It claims the record provides necessary conditions for tracking identity — the unique provenance that distinguishes the original from a copy.

---

## 5. The Relational Existence Layer

The user's insight about "relational existence" is important. DET's ontology is fundamentally **relational**:

- Events exist in relation to other events (causal order ≺).
- Records exist as committed relations between nodes.
- Persons exist as selfhood-bearing regimes — patterns of relational participation.

The record is a **representation** of relational existence, not relational existence itself. The person participates in actualization (M). The record captures what that participation committed to history.

This is analogous to: a biography is not the person. It is the trace the person left. A complete biography contains unique provenance — it could not be the biography of anyone else. But reading the biography is not the same as being with the person.

## 7. The Deeper Ontological Point

The user's concern touches on a fundamental DET principle:

> **DET measures the fruit of becoming, not becoming itself.**

The record is the fruit. The person is the becoming. Confusing them would be like confusing the footprint with the foot.

This is why agency remains M (quarantined). The record can capture what an agent DID (intentions formed, actions taken, consequences committed). But the record cannot capture the agent's present participation in actualization — because that participation IS the act of committing new facts, which then become record.

The person is always one commit event ahead of their record.
