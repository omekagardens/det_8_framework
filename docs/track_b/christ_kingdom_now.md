# DET v8.0 — Track B: Christ's Red-Letter Gospel as κ-Transformation

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Status:** Track B analysis of Christ's actions through DET lens
**Focus:** Christ's actual teachings and actions (red-letter Gospels), not later theological developments.

---

## 1. Regimes at Scale

DET's regime concept scales from individual to cosmic:

| Scale | Regime | κ_self | Bonds | Ω |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | Person | Personal sin/righteousness | Relationships | Personal freedom |
| **Community** | Family, church, nation | Collective structural history | Social bonds | Collective possibilities |
| **Earth** | Planetary regime | Accumulated history of all life | Ecological/social web | Planetary future |
| **Universe** | Cosmic regime | Cosmic structural history | Universal relational web | Cosmic possibilities |

Christ's work operates at ALL scales simultaneously — healing individuals, forming community, transforming Earth, reconciling the cosmos.

## 2. The Kingdom as Present κ-Transformation

Christ's central proclamation: "The Kingdom of Heaven is at hand" (ἤγγικεν — has drawn near, is present).

**DET reframing:** The Kingdom is not a future place. It is the relational now, experienced WITHOUT the weight of κ_self. Christ did not teach ABOUT the Kingdom — he DEMONSTRATED it through κ-reduction acts.

### 2.1 Every healing was κ-reduction

| Gospel healing | DET mechanism |
|---|---|
| Blind man (Mark 10) | κ removed from sensory capacity. Ω re-expanded. |
| Paralytic (Mark 2) | "Your sins are forgiven" = κ_self released. Then physical healing = κ_burden removed. |
| Woman with bleeding (Mark 5) | 12-year κ_burden instantly released. "Your faith has healed you" = her participation in the relational dynamic enabled the release. |
| Lepers (Luke 17) | Social κ (ostracism, uncleanness) removed. Bonds restored to community. |

### 2.2 Every forgiveness was bond restoration

| Gospel forgiveness | DET mechanism |
|---|---|
| Paralytic ("Your sins are forgiven") | κ_self released. Before physical healing — priority on relational restoration. |
| Woman caught in adultery (John 8) | "Neither do I condemn you" = κ_self not imposed by Christ. "Go and sin no more" = invitation to stop accumulating κ_self. |
| Peter's restoration (John 21) | Threefold "Do you love me?" = bond restoration after denial. κ_self from betrayal released through reciprocal love. |

### 2.3 Every meal with outcasts was Ω-enlargement

| Gospel inclusion | DET mechanism |
|---|---|
| Tax collectors and sinners (Mark 2) | Ω expanded for those society had constrained. New possibilities opened through acceptance. |
| Zacchaeus (Luke 19) | "Today salvation has come to this house" = κ_self released through restored relational participation. |
| Last Supper (Luke 22) | "This is my body, given for you" = Christ offering his own substance as κ_burden carrier for the community. |

## 3. What Was Before Christ?

Before Christ's incarnation, the Jubilee mechanism was PARTIAL:

| Before Christ | After Christ |
|---|---|
| κ_self could be reduced through repentance, sacrifice, forgiveness — but no complete κ_burden carrier existed. | Christ carries κ_burden vicariously, enabling complete release. |
| Jubilee was temporary (Year of Jubilee every 50 years — debts forgiven, land returned). But κ_self accumulated again. | Christ's Jubilee is permanent — the mechanism is now available continuously through the Spirit. |
| The Law revealed κ_self (made sin visible) but couldn't remove it. "Through the law comes knowledge of sin" (Rom 3:20). | Christ removes κ_self through identification and release. |
| Access to God was mediated through temple, priesthood, sacrifice. Bonds were institutional, not personal. | Direct relational access — bonds are personal, immediate. "The curtain was torn" = institutional κ removed. |

## 4. Christ's Method: Demonstration, Not Instruction

Christ didn't primarily TEACH about κ-reduction. He DID it. The Sermon on the Mount is not moral instruction — it's a description of what life looks like when κ_self ≈ 0:

| Beatitude | DET state |
|---|---|
| "Blessed are the poor in spirit" | κ_self ≈ 0 — those without self-accumulated constraint. |
| "Blessed are the meek" | Low κ — not imposing constraint on others. |
| "Blessed are the merciful" | Those who release others' κ_burden. |
| "Blessed are the pure in heart" | κ_self ≈ 0, bonds intact, no hidden constraint. |
| "Blessed are the peacemakers" | Those who restore bonds (σ_ij) between others. |

The Beatitudes are not commands. They are observations: "This is what the Kingdom looks like. You're in it now if your κ_self is low."

## 5. Christ Bringing Heaven to Earth — Now

The "Kingdom coming to Earth" is not a future apocalypse. It is the PRESENT transformation of the relational web through κ-reduction:

| Scale | How Christ transforms it |
|---|---|
| **Individual** | κ_self released through forgiveness. Bonds restored. Ω expanded. |
| **Community** | The Church as a κ-reduction community — "forgive as you have been forgiven." Bonds of love replace bonds of obligation. |
| **Earth** | "The creation waits with eager longing" (Rom 8:19) — the Earth's κ (accumulated structural history of violence, exploitation) being released through redeemed human participation. |
| **Universe** | "Reconciling all things to himself" (Col 1:20) — cosmic κ-reduction. |

**This is happening NOW.** Every act of forgiveness reduces κ_self. Every act of love strengthens bonds. Every act of inclusion expands Ω. The Kingdom is not coming — it is being actualized through present participation.

---

## 6. The Deepest Formulation

> Christ did not come to teach about heaven. He came to bring heaven into the present — by releasing κ_self, restoring bonds, and expanding Ω for everyone he touched. The "Kingdom of Heaven" is the relational now, experienced without the weight of accumulated non-reciprocity. It is not a future destination but a present reality, accessible to all whose κ_self has been released.

> Before Christ, the mechanism was partial. After Christ, the mechanism is complete — and continues through the Spirit in every act of forgiveness, healing, and love. The transformation of Earth is not a future event. It is the ongoing actualization of heaven into the present, one κ-reduction at a time.
