## 3. Positive decomposition with its inherited finite premise

RI175 and its independent root review accept q=k*g with k>0, g monic

    e=q(1)>0,       t=-q'(1)>0,       a=q2=k>0,
    q(z)=e+(1-z)*t+(1-z)^2*a,
    q0=e+t+a>0.                                     (8)

These signs are inherited finite numerical-sign premises, not newly
deduced from positivity, canonicality or general DET axioms. This sitting
reads RI175's accepted analytic statement and its selected review, not
the old RI122 scientific body or its narrowly excepted decision. The
RI175-only literal-read exception is not current authority. The point
z=1 is a polynomial comparison point, not an admissible growth scale.

Eliminate a between the last two expressions in(8). Manual collection
gives the positive identity

    q(z)=(1-z)^2*q0+z*(2-z)*e+z*(1-z)*t.              (9)

For 0<z<1, both added terms are strictly positive. Also

    q0-q(z)=z*t+z*(2-z)*a>0.

Thus the unchanged polynomial obeys the strict joint bounds

    (1-z)^2*q0 < q(z) < q0.                         (10)

These do not fit q0 or choose independent coefficients. All of(6),
including the quadratic canonical product, continues to define q.

## 4. A single strict-branch-safe sufficient certificate

Define the new scalar comparison

    C0=K*(K*v-72lambda*j0)*q0-432lambda*G_*v.         (11)

It contains no formal scale evaluation q(Z). If C0>=0, then

    K*(K*v-72lambda*j0)*q0 >=432lambda*G_*v>0.

Since K,q0>0, this forces K*v-72lambda*j0>0 and hence v>B6.
No assumption about the sign of that factor was used to clear it.

Substitute Z=6/D into(9):

    D^2*q(Z)=K^2*q0+12(D-3)*e+6K*t.

Using K^2(v-B6)=K(K*v-72lambda*j0), obtain exactly

    D^2*H6=C0+(v-B6)*[12(D-3)*e+6K*t].              (12)

Every factor in the added term is strictly positive on the branch
already forced by C0>=0. Therefore

    C0>=0 implies v>B6 and H6>0.                    (13)

Equality C0=0 still gives strict H6>0. This is a sufficient certificate,
not an equivalent replacement of(2); negative C0 does not refute(2).
In particular the exact H6=0 possibility in(2) remains legitimate but
cannot be certified by(13). If eventually proved at the actual law,
(13) would make I(Z)<0 and W<0 throughout the containing curved domain.
No such actual sign is established here.

There is a complementary necessary magnitude comparison. If(2) holds,
then q(Z)<q0 and v>B6 give

    D^2*(K*v-72lambda*j0)*q0 >432K*lambda*G_*v.       (14)

Failure of this strict necessary comparison excludes the full-domain
endpoint criterion, not the actual W sign. The gap between(11) and(14)
is intentional: (10) supplies upper and lower controls, not equality.

## 6. How the neutral rows enter the unresolved contrast

One more exact identity exposes a source of missing joint control rather
than merely renaming H6. Complete row normalization gives

    C=c_1-c_0=delta-x-y.

Let k00=k_0(0)=2-2omega_0>0 and S0=sigma_0*k_0(0)-sigma_1*k_1(0).
Since omega_1-omega_0=44theta*x,

    S0=(k00/c0)*[P_0-P_1+sigma_1*C]
                       +88theta*sigma_1*x.          (19)

Indeed P_0/c0-P_1/c1=(P_0-P_1+sigma_1*C)/c0;
the remainder is sigma_1(k_0(0)-k_1(0)). No zero canonical coordinate
or record contrast is divided by. All signs and ties are retained.

Write Delta f=f_1-f_0 and Delta m=m_1-m_0. Then

    chi_1-chi_0=41x-Delta f/2-Delta m,
    P_0-P_1 >= -[41x-Delta f/2-Delta m]_+.            (20)

The latter follows from monotonicity and the unit Lipschitz bound of
the positive-part function, valid on both branches and at equality.
Multiplying only by positive k00/c0 yields the justified joint floor

    Lq=Tcore(0)+gamma*{
       (k00/c0)*(-[41x-Delta f/2-Delta m]_+
                             +sigma_1*(delta-x-y))
                         +88theta*sigma_1*x},
    q0>=Lq.                                         (21)

This makes the missing comparison concrete: the same singleton contrast
x that contributes to v also changes the canonical deficit against
Delta f/2+Delta m and the full-complement change delta-x-y. It cannot
be counted as a favorable contribution while its neutral-row effect
is omitted. No sign of x,y,C or either neutral-row contrast is assumed.

The floor Lq is not known positive. Substituting it for q0 in(11) is
not branch-safe unless Lq>0 is proved as well (or v>B6 is separately
fixed before multiplying the floor). Two negative factors must not be
allowed to certify a false strict branch. The exact R0 route in(18)
avoids this issue through the accepted q0>0. Equations(19)--(21) are
an optional tool for a future authorized analytic proof, not an actual
positive-floor result or a selected successor.
