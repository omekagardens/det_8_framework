## 2. Independent reconstruction algorithms

The only native structural parents are C3, C4, H5=(0,1,3,7,7), the lower
C3 ordinal-sum A3, and upper C3 ordinal-sum A4. Records are exactly 0 and
1. A direct downset predicate derives their individual ideals. Combinations
of omitted maximal vertices derive the complete nonempty deletion subsets;
descending mask order is applied only for the specified serialization.
Records, order masks and precursor masks are restricted through the same
explicit old-to-new label map. No ideal orbit is merged or reweighted.

Lower products accumulate separate exact numerators and denominators,
giving 20 individual slots and 80 retained factors across both records.
Their complete sums supply the upper full-complement factors, independently
of the closed-form sum. Upper products retain 36 slots and 220 factors.
In this fixed domain every negative-exponent factor is a strictly positive
held scalar: reaching the six-parent symbolic layer requires a singleton
deletion and exponent +1. The consumer enforces this stronger scoped fact
and separately accumulates constant denominators; it does not copy the
producer's Laurent-factor algorithm or admit a binomial inversion.

Every polynomial is an immutable dense 9-by-5 exact-rational rectangle,
with fixed global analytic degree caps. The closed route independently
forms the RI94 binomial formulas. A separate direct divided-binomial sum
reconstructs d without dividing the raw D. All lower sums, upper U,V,D,
and divided d must agree exactly between routes.

Prescribed epsilon*rho^2 division checks every forbidden position with
rho exponent below two or epsilon exponent zero. It retains the full
raw padded grid, every forbidden zero, the quotient and reconstruction.
D/A raw bounds are (4,4), C/G raw bounds (8,4); divided A has (2,3),
C/G have (6,3). Dynamic valuation and degree enlargement are forbidden.
Both D-based and V-based cross products are computed, and both raw and
divided full-complement s-endpoint numerators and zero residuals are kept.

The tensor forward transform is separable: epsilon conversion for each
ordinary rho coefficient, then rho conversion for each epsilon Bernstein
column. Reverse verification actually multiplies normalized Bernstein
basis polynomials by dense convolution and sums them, instead of reusing
the forward conversion. It then independently removes R^a*(1/4)^b and
compares the complete original grid. Four independently evaluated edge
transforms and the included corner are checked. All padded zeros remain.

There are 68 coordinates: 12 for degree-(2,3) A and 28 for each
degree-(6,3) Gi. The fixed sufficient criterion requires all nonnegative
and each Gi upper-right coefficient strictly positive. Weak A and excluded
axes are respected. The seventeen last-column entries must equal four
times the accepted RI91 entries at the same R and basis degrees. They
are strictly positive before native classification.

Only two native outcomes exist: certified-positive-amplitude-obstruction,
or unresolved-fixed-bivariate-bernstein with a nonempty ordered list of
negative coefficients. No native reverse-sign, blind, root or additional
search branch is present. rho, epsilon and s remain formal variables;
R and epsilon=1/4 are proof-domain endpoints, not actual global scales.
