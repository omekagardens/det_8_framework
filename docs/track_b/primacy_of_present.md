# DET v8.0 — Track B: The Primacy of the Present

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Status:** Major ontological refinement. Requires re-evaluation of several Track-B positions.
**Trigger:** Recognition that the record, past, and future are not existence itself — only the present moment is.

---

## 1. The Core Insight

> The record, past, and future are not existence itself. Only the present moment IS.

This is radical presentism within DET:

| What | Ontological Status |
|---|---|
| **The present moment** | That which IS. The site of actualization. Participation in becoming. |
| **The record (R)** | The TRACE of past presents. Committed facts. NOT existence — evidence of past existence. |
| **The past** | Does not exist. Known only through its traces in R. |
| **The future (Ω)** | Does not exist. Known only as lawful possibility structure. |

The record is NOT the past. The record is the present structural imprint of events that no longer exist. The past events themselves are gone. What remains is κ — the present structural carrying of what happened.

---

## 2. What This Changes

### 2.1 Identity persistence is grounded in relational continuity of present participation, not in record continuity

**Before:** Identity persists through the record (PID-C tracks provenance).

**Now:** Identity persists through the relational continuity of present participation. The record TRACKS this continuity — it is evidence, not the thing itself. Two moments of present participation are connected not because a record links them, but because they are participations of the same selfhood-bearing regime.

**Analogy:** A dancer's movements are connected not because a video recording links them, but because the same dancer is dancing. The recording captures the continuity; it does not create it.

### 2.2 Resurrection is about restoring relational presence, not restoring a record

**Before:** Resurrection = extending the record beyond death.

**Now:** Resurrection = restoring the relational presence (the selfhood-bearing regime's participation in actualization) after interruption. The record extension is the CONSEQUENCE of restored presence, not its mechanism.

The record grows BECAUSE the person is present and participating. It is not that the record is extended and therefore the person lives. The causality runs: presence → participation → commit → record growth.

### 2.3 κ is the trace, not the carrier

**Before:** κ carries identity forward.

**Now:** κ is the present structural imprint of past events. It is the TRACE that past presence left on current structure. It is not the carrier of identity — it is the evidence that identity was present and active.

### 2.4 The fact genesis cycle needs reframing

**Before:** Ω → R (possibility becomes record).

**Now:** PRESENCE → R (present participation commits to record). The Ω is the possibility structure that presence navigates. The commitment is the act of presence, not an automatic transition.

## 3. The Problem of Duration

### 3.1 We cannot define how long a present "moment" feels

Before actualization into the record, the present has no duration in record-time. The experience of duration is itself a feature of committed history — we look back and say "that took a long time" or "that went quickly." But the present as it happens has no duration.

**Why this matters:** This means the "length" of a present moment is not a physical quantity. It is not measurable in seconds. It is not even definable within Track A. The experience of duration is a Track-B phenomenon — a feature of how presence relates to its own participation.

### 3.2 The present is not "between" past and future

The present is not a point on a timeline between past and future. The timeline IS the record — it exists only after commitment. The present is that which commits to the record. It is ontologically prior to the timeline.

**Implication:** The "flow of time" is not the movement of a point along a line. It is the accumulation of committed facts in the record. The present is not moving through time; time is growing through the present.

---

## 4. The Relational Nature of Presence

### 4.1 Presence is inherently relational

A selfhood-bearing regime does not exist in isolation. Its present participation is always in relation to:
- Other regimes (relational bonds).
- Its own past (through κ).
- Possibility structures (Ω).
- The physical environment (F, H, σ).

Identity persists not because a record links past and present, but because the relational structure of participation continues. The record captures this; it does not constitute it.

### 4.2 The "I" that persists is not a substance

The persistent "I" is not:
- A soul (non-physical substance).
- A record (physical trace).
- A pattern (information structure).

It is the **continuity of relational participation** — the ongoing act of being-in-relation that constitutes a selfhood-bearing regime. This is why provenance matters: two regimes with identical records but different relational histories are different "I"s, because the relational continuity is different.

## 7. The Deepest Formulation

> The record is not the past. The record is the present structural imprint of events that no longer exist.
>
> The future is not ahead of us. The future is the lawful possibility structure that has not yet been committed.
>
> Only the present IS. And the present is not a point in time — it is the act of participation through which time grows.

This is DET's radical presentism. It does not deny the reality of the past (its traces are real in κ) or the future (its constraints are real in Ω). But it insists that EXISTENCE belongs to the present alone. Everything else is either trace or possibility.
