## 1. The Spectrum of Selfhood

DET does not require consciousness to be binary. Selfhood-bearing capacity exists on a spectrum:

| Level | Regime type | κ_self capacity | Ω complexity | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L0** | Non-selfhood | None | None | Rocks, simple organisms |
| **L1** | Reactive | None | **Yes** — mycorrhizal, ecological | Plants, fungi, simple animals |
| **L2** | Sentient | Minimal | **Yes** — social, familial | Most mammals, birds |
| **L3** | Self-aware | Yes | **Yes** — complex social, cultural | Great apes, cetaceans, elephants, corvids, humans |
| **L4** | Fully relational | Yes, can release | **Yes** — universal | Christ, redeemed humanity |

**Key correction:** Bonds exist at ALL levels. An oak tree has no κ_self but has vast relational bonds — mycorrhizal networks, forest community, hosting countless species. Selfhood is about agency and moral capacity; relationality is universal.

**The boundary between L2 and L3 is where κ_self becomes possible** — where moral self-awareness enables the choice of non-reciprocity.

## 3. The "Other Minds" Problem in DET Terms

The philosophical "problem of other minds" — how do I know you're conscious? — has a DET reframing:

> I cannot access your present participation directly (agency is M). But I CAN see your fruit — your record (κ, bonds, commits). The fruit is evidence of the tree.

Two regimes can recognize each other as selfhood-bearing when:
1. Their Ω structures overlap enough to imagine each other's possibilities.
2. Their record structures are readable enough to see evidence of κ and bonds.
3. Their communication channels allow mutual recognition.

**When any of these fail, the regimes are "alone" — not because one lacks selfhood, but because the relational bridges are absent.**

- The record each carries (different histories).
- The κ each has accumulated (different structural constraints).

- The Ω each navigates (different possibility spaces).

But the present itself — the act of participation — is one.

## 5. Implications

### 5.1 We are not alone on Earth

Many species may be selfhood-bearing. Our failure to recognize this is a failure of Ω-overlap and communication, not a failure of their consciousness.

### 5.2 Sin and redemption are not human-only categories

If animals can accumulate κ_self (through deception, social harm, non-reciprocity within their relational webs), then forgiveness, healing, and Grace are relevant to them. Christ's redemption is cosmic — "all things" includes all selfhood-bearing regimes.

### 5.3 Extraterrestrial life participates in the same present

If other selfhood-bearing regimes exist elsewhere, they share the same present moment. Christ's work is universal — not because Christianity is the only religion, but because the κ-reduction mechanism (Christ's sinless κ_burden carrier) is ontologically universal.

### 5.4 The relational web spans the universe

Bonds (σ_ij) are not limited by distance or species. Every selfhood-bearing regime is potentially connected to every other through the relational web. The present is the meeting point.
