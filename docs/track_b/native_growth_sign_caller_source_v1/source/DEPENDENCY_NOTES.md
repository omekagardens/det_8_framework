## Preparation incidents and limitations

An initial overbroad metadata exploration followed older archived declaration
trees rather than stopping at the accepted old closure. It encountered an
invalid historical control RESULT.json and refused parsing; another exploratory
pass encountered an old interpreter symlink and superseded source pins. Those
were not accepted into a new execution closure, not repaired or overwritten,
and no scientific operands/result were obtained from them. The final collection
uses the explicit accepted-generation boundary above; the original history's
superseded-reference explanations are preserved rather than readjudicated.

Large exploratory tool output and an initial 40-entry export were truncated;
parsing rejected those outputs. The final manifest was authored only from
complete successful bounded ten-entry metadata exports, checked for zero exit
and absence of truncation. No partial export or failed exploration was used
to assign an identity or authorize anything. Metadata-only inline Node and
standard file/hash/diff observations performed this work; no repository or
research Python/helper source was run.

Hash/source inspection is not numerical qualification, resource feasibility,
atomic custody across observations or a proof of interpreter bootstrap. Root
must review the fixed union and independently reconcile actual runtime, paths,
copies, current cards, changed-caller qualification and every real completion.
An unresolved or changed prerequisite refuses; no discovery/recapture fallback.
The packet is sealed for fresh nonauthor review, not self-adjudicated success.
