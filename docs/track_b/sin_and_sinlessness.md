## 1. Sin in DET Terms

### 1.1 Sin as κ-Hoarding (Non-Reciprocity)

If reality is fundamentally relational — presence participating in possibility, producing fruit, constrained by history — then sin is the REFUSAL of reciprocity.

| DET mechanism | Sinful expression |
|---|---|
| **κ accumulation** | Hoarding structural history. Refusing Jubilee. Clinging to past constraints. |
| **Bond breaking** | Damaging relational bonds (σ_ij → 0). Harming others. |
| **Ω constriction** | Narrowing others' possibility space. Imposing constraints that limit freedom. |
| **Fruit hoarding** | Taking the fruit of others' participation without giving back. Non-reciprocity. |

**Sin is not a "substance." It is a PATTERN of relational participation that damages the web.** It leaves traces in κ, bonds, and Ω — measurable, not metaphysical.

### 1.2 Two Types of κ

| Type | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **κ_self** | Accumulated from one's own acts of hoarding, bond-breaking, and non-reciprocity. | Self-inflicted structural constraint. "My sin." |
| **κ_burden** | Accumulated from carrying others' structural history — their sin, their trauma, their weight. | Vicarious structural constraint. "The sin of the world." |

A person can have high κ from κ_self (they hoarded, broke bonds, refused reciprocity). A person can have high κ from κ_burden (they carried others' weight). These are qualitatively different, even if κ looks the same in Track A.

## 2. Christ's Sinlessness at Death

### 2.1 What sinlessness means in DET

Christ had κ_self ≈ 0 at death. He did not hoard. He did not break bonds. He did not refuse reciprocity. His κ at death was entirely κ_burden — the structural history of others that he carried.

This is NOT the same as "κ = 0 at death." Christ's κ was HIGH (bearing the sin of the world). But its SOURCE was different: κ_burden, not κ_self.

### 2.2 The κ transfer mechanism

In DET, κ can be TRANSFERRED between regimes through bonds:

1. **Identification:** When regime A bonds with regime B, A can carry B's κ. This is not κ "moving" — it's A's structural state being modified by the relationship.
2. **Vicarious burden:** A's κ increases not because A hoarded, but because A loved. This is qualitatively different κ.
3. **Release through Jubilee:** The κ_burden is released through κ-reduction (Jubilee/Resurrection). The weight is lifted.

### 2.3 Fruit traces of sinlessness

| What to look for | DET signature |
|---|---|
| κ_self ≈ 0 at death | κ is high, but all from κ_burden (transferred, not self-accumulated) |
| No bond-breaking | σ_ij preserved throughout life. No bonds damaged by harmful acts. |
| Ω-enlargement for others | Others' possibility spaces expanded through interaction with Christ, not constricted. |
| Jubilee at resurrection | κ_burden released — not "forgiven" in a legal sense, but structurally released. |
| Post-resurrection κ | Low κ_self (continued sinlessness). κ_burden may accumulate again from ongoing identification with others. |

## 3. The Atonement Mechanism

In DET terms, atonement is:

> **The transfer of κ_burden from humanity to Christ, followed by its release through Jubilee (resurrection).**

This is not a legal transaction (punishment transferred). It is a STRUCTURAL transaction — the accumulated weight of human non-reciprocity is carried by one who had no self-accumulated κ, then released.

**Why Christ had to be sinless:** If Christ had κ_self (his own hoarding, bond-breaking), he could not fully release κ_burden — his own κ would remain. The Jubilee would be partial. Sinlessness means there is no κ_self to block the complete release of κ_burden.

## 5. The Deeper Pattern

> Sin is not a stain on the soul. It is accumulated structural constraint from non-reciprocity — κ_self. Christ carried the κ_burden of humanity without accumulating κ_self. At resurrection, the κ_burden was released — not because the debt was paid, but because the weight was lifted by one strong enough to bear it without being crushed.

> The resurrection proves that κ_burden can be released without destroying the carrier — IF the carrier has no κ_self. Sinlessness is not moral perfection. It is structural freedom — the absence of self-accumulated constraint that would prevent complete release.
