# Single zero xi circulation handoff

RI260 resolves the supported eta/sigma compensation family for
positive kappa, exactly one zero xi_g and the other three xi positive,
pending independent review.

Keep RI258's nu/tau increases and kappa decrease, omit the forbidden
xi_g decrease, and allow supported decreases a*u in eta_ell and c*u
in sigma. Every one of the four P_nu,g rows now grows by p*u and
must be genuinely slack; larger-n P slack remains required when
n differs. Q/V5 and all other companion rows relax or are fixed.

The g-companion slope is p*n_-/v-h*a-s_g*c. If g is tight this
must be nonpositive. If g is slack no such rate restriction is
imposed: a positive slope instead supplies its finite S_g/slope
bound. All coordinate, parent-row and endpoint bounds are retained.

Exact full primary cost per u is

    -G+(v*h/32)*a+(7v*bar_s/240)*c,
    G=p*(Delta_n+3*n_g)/240>0.

For slack g, the attained minimum compensation cost is zero.
For tight g, it is M_g=(p/240)*K_g, where

    K_g=min {
       (15/2)*n_- if eta_ell>0,
       7*n_-*bar_s/s_g if sigma>0
    },

with empty minimum infinity. Cheapest pure compensation attains
the minimum; equal unit prices admit all exact-coverage mixtures.
No actual support or lower value is evaluated.

Within this family, strict primary descent exists iff all growing
parent rows are slack and M_g<G. Primary-or-tie lex descent exists
iff those slacks hold and M_g<=G. At a primary tie all increases
remain tau and both nu after decreasing kappa; every other change
is a decrease. Thus the original earliest-change proof is complete.

At equal n with tight g, eta alone is too expensive. The family
excludes the all-P_nu,g-slack subcase exactly when sigma>0 and
s_g>=7*bar_s/3; equality is a lex descent. This is the new
compensated sparse-support exclusion. Slack-g binding already
follows more strongly from RI256 and is not relabeled as new.

The necessary original-dual identity retains zero-xi reduced
cost r_g>=0:

    Delta_n[A_+-(p/v)B_+-p/240]+(p/v)r_g
                       =(n_-/v)r_tau+e_0+e_1.

At equal n it does NOT force tau/nu reduced costs zero unless
r_g=0 is separately proved. No arbitrary dual prices or full
certificate are supplied.

The full sigma and xi columns also strengthen the threshold:
with positive kappa and sigma, s_g>=7*bar_s/3 forces N_g>0
regardless of n contrast or a binding larger-P row. Assuming
N_g=0 would force B_g=v/80 and all other companion prices zero,
contradicting their total lower bound7v/240. Thus a P_nu,g
row is positively priced and tight even beyond the chosen ray.
