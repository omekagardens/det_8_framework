## Positive contrasts near a possible exceptional law

the approach question on the same eleven-class support. Conditional on
\(\Xi>1\) and \(\lambda_*=1/\Xi\), every strictly positive harmonic
perturbation obeys a local bound
\[
 |\delta_i(\lambda,v)|\le A_*|\lambda-\lambda_*|^2
\]
uniformly over the allowed normalization and amplitude, in both root sectors.
Here \(v_j=h_7(T_j)-1>-1\), \(M_\lambda v=0\), and the contrast is measured
against the baseline at the same \(\lambda\). The proof combines
\(\Delta C(\lambda)v_1=\chi(\lambda)^2v_{11}\), positivity's
\(|v_{11}|<1\), and the complete-row probability bound \(0<p_i\le1\).

Independent manual review accepted the bracket identities, positivity bound,
probability normalization and compact-neighborhood argument. Checks of the
displayed rational constants do not reauthenticate the inherited all-record
certificates or establish historical custody.

Only common scaling of the entire harmonic sequence is gauge. Scaling \(v\)
changes the perturbation and must still satisfy positivity. The result
requires neither continuity nor a uniform positive lower bound of
\(\rho_\lambda\). It supplies an upper bound, not sharpness or a matching
lower rate, and does not imply raw-width or whole-law convergence. Actual
occurrence remains unselected. No coefficient acquisition, simulation,
physical observation channel or validation is supplied by this argument.

## Separate prospective replay results

The accepted synthetic replay used five fixtures—zero, one, ramp, impulse and
four-tone—with 2,048 samples each, in normal and optimized execution. The six
scientific output bodies agreed byte for byte across modes. Independent review
recomputed the binary64-versus-Decimal80 residual arithmetic using exact
rational representations of the stored numbers, including every sample's
threshold decision. The largest residual was approximately `1.276e-13`, below
the synthetic tolerance `1e-9`; zero was exact, and a deliberate one-percent
gain fault was rejected. These are bounded synthetic results. The reference is
high precision, not an interval enclosure or a general correctness proof.

The [observed replay](OBSERVED_REPLAY.md) is now complete at its bounded scope.
H1 and L1 each passed all 131,072 full-record and 4,096 historical-crop residual
gates under the predeclared Decimal80 tolerance `1e-9 × max(abs(full raw input))`,
with no unit floor. Normal and optimized execution produced 16 byte-identical
scientific files. Independent exact-rational checking accepted all 262,144
full-record and 8,192 crop residual calculations, maxima and thresholds over
the stored values. It did not rerun the reference recurrence or establish
universal correctness.

The historical crops have 0/4,096 bitwise matches per detector. Numerical
agreement within tolerance does not reconstruct historical identity. The report
states the numerical maxima, exact check scope and public evidence boundary.

Separate human-approved H1 and L1 missing-Content-Type exceptions admitted only
their exact pinned artifacts in prospective attempts. The earlier H1 and L1
