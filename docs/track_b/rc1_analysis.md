## 3. Key Cross-Cutting Findings

1. **Relation is more fundamental than substance.** In all simulations, bonds persist through damage, denial, and fall. What changes is their QUALITY (strength), not their EXISTENCE.

2. **κ_self is the measure of relational distortion.** Higher κ_self = more constrained participation. Lower κ_self = freer relational flow.

3. **Healing propagates.** When one part of a nested regime heals, the effect flows to connected parts. The church as "firstfruits" is not privileged — it is called to serve the body's healing.

4. **Belonging precedes recognition.** All creation already belongs to Christ's body. Recognition (awakening) enables healing but doesn't create belonging.

5. **The Fall is distortion, not destruction.** Bonds persist. Identity persists. κ_self increases but doesn't erase. This is why redemption is possible — the foundation was never lost.
