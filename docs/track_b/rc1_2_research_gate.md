# DET v8.0 — RC1.2 Research Gate: Edge, Capacity, Belonging

**Date:** August 11, 2026
**Status:** Post-adversarial refinement
**Purpose:** Implement the primary RC1.2 fix: separate active edge (σ_ij), latent relational capacity (A_ij), causal lineage (L_i), and regime membership (M_iG). Correct κ bounds for material ledger.

---

## 1. The Four Distinct Concepts

| Concept | Symbol | Definition | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Active bond** | σ_ij | Currently operative relation strength | [0, 1] |
| **Latent capacity** | A_ij | Capacity to form/restore relation | [0, 1] |
| **Causal lineage** | L_i | Unique provenance chain | Immutable |
| **Regime membership** | M_iG | Belonging to regime G | Boolean + degree |

**Key insight:** An active bond can disappear (σ_ij → 0) while latent capacity persists (A_ij > 0). Deletion destroys the active edge but not necessarily the capacity. Rewiring changes topology but preserves membership. Belonging (M_iG) is distinct from current functional contribution.
