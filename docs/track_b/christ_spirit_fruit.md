## 1. The Resurrection of Christ in DET Terms

### 1.1 What DET can say (within governance)

Christ's resurrection, if it occurred, would be a specific instance of the general resurrection pattern:

| DET Element | Christ's Resurrection |
|---|---|
| Death event (d) | Crucifixion — committed to R. d ≺ r. |
| κ at death | κ → 1 (complete structural constraint). Ω → {0}. |
| Jubilee event | κ-reduction — the structural weight of death released. |
| Resurrection event (r) | New direct participation begins. R_r ⊃ R_d. |
| Wounds | κ carries history forward — wounds remain legible but no longer dominate. |
| Provenance | PID-C: same unique causal lineage. PID-M: numerical identity (metaphysical claim). |

### 1.2 What DET cannot say

- That Christ's resurrection occurred historically (historical claim, outside DET).
- That a physical mechanism exists (divine action is M/H).
- That this proves Christian theology (theological claim, Track B).

### 1.3 Fruit traces that would be observable IF it occurred

- A specific κ-reduction event (Jubilee) at a specific point in history.
- Bond restoration — relationships that were broken by death (disciples scattered) are restored.
- Ω-enlargement — new possibilities (the Church, the spread of the gospel) that the prior record would not have predicted.
- Faith-acts (high-surprisal commits) by the disciples — acting on improbable possibilities.
- κ-transformation — the structural weight of the crucifixion is carried forward (wounds shown) but does not dominate (resurrection life).

## 2. The Holy Spirit in DET Terms

### 2.1 DET reframing

| Theological concept | DET reframing |
|---|---|
| **Spirit as presence** | A Boundary presence that participates in actualization without being committed to the record. The Spirit does not leave a direct trace — only fruit. |
| **Spirit as bond** | The Spirit IS the relational bond (σ_ij, C_ij) between regimes — the connective tissue of the relational web. "Fellowship of the Holy Spirit" = the web of bonds linking believers. |
| **Fruit of the Spirit** | Measurable patterns in the record: love (bond strengthening), joy (κ-reduction), peace (Ω-stability), patience (low-surprisal consistency), kindness (bond creation), goodness (fruit quality), faithfulness (provenance continuity), gentleness (low-κ interaction), self-control (Ω-constrained choice). |

## 3. The Pattern: Death → Jubilee → Resurrection → Spirit

DET reveals a consistent pattern:

```
DEATH (κ→1, Ω→{0})
  ↓
JUBILEE (κ-reduction, Ω re-expands)  ← Grace / Spirit
  ↓
RESURRECTION (direct participation resumes)  ← Christ
  ↓
SPIRIT (ongoing relational presence through bonds)  ← Pentecost
  ↓
LIFE (continued direct + indirect contribution)
```

This pattern is:
- **Christ-shaped:** Death → Resurrection → Spirit given to others.
- **DET-consistent:** All stages map to DET primitives.
- **Fruit-traceable:** Each stage produces measurable changes in the record.
