For completeness, the load-bearing reasoning for (4) is as follows.
RI63 accepts that every active component of its canonical boundary law
is everywhere defect-neutral. Hence any component containing a raising
slot has zero canonical coefficient and actual coefficient theta. The
C5 core birth above I raises the minimum arbitrary-vertex Ferrers-deletion
defect from zero to one. Its terminal has two incomparable elements with

Thus d=theta b at every marking.

The H5 core birth produces I ordinal-sum A3. Its defect is two: removing
one vertex still leaves two incomparable chain principal ideals sharing
at least two stem vertices, which is impossible in a Ferrers cell order;
removing two cap vertices leaves C4. Since H5 has defect one, this slot is
raising. Its two singleton maximum deletions give C4 twice, and their
double deletion gives C3. Its positive potential is b^2/a, proving
g=theta b^2/a. Finally the accepted complete twin-top diamond is

and hence the last two identities. These proofs concern all records and
transport each order, precursor and inherited/newborn record together.

The elementary Ferrers obstruction used here follows directly from cell

## 3. Deriving the core record pattern rather than assuming it

For all xi=0,...,7 define

    lambda_xi=Delta_xi p/Delta_1 p.

The denominator is nonzero by the accepted strict sign. Lambda_0=0 and
lambda_1=1. For xi=2,...,7, equating the constant, X and X^2 coefficients

    Delta_xi E=lambda_xi Delta_1 E,
    Delta_xi j=lambda_xi Delta_1 j,
    Delta_xi nu=lambda_xi Delta_1 nu.                         (6)

The same equations hold trivially at xi=0,1. Only the nonzero constant
integer factors and Delta_1 p were divided out. Neither Delta_1 E,
Delta_1 j nor Delta_1 nu is assumed nonzero.

Now use V=E-nu/theta-2j and the record-independent theta. Equations
(6) give for every xi

    Delta_xi V=lambda_xi Delta_1 V.                           (7)

Compare this with the complete accepted pattern (2). Since Delta_1 V>0,

    lambda_xi=chi(xi),                                       (8)
    p_xi=p_0+chi(xi)(p_1-p_0).

have p=p1. Equation (5), with positive common theta^6/a^3, implies equality
of the corresponding b fourth powers. The fourth-power map is injective

(x-y)(x+y)(x^2+y^2)=0 and both latter factors are positive. Consequently

    b_xi=b_0 for xi=0,2,4,6,
    b_xi=b_1 for xi=1,3,5,7.                                (9)

The accepted negative Delta_1 p also gives b1<b0 by the same strict
