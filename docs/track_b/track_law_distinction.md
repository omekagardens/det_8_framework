# DET v8.0 — Governance Clarification: Track A vs Track B "Laws"

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Status:** Essential governance clarification
**Trigger:** Distinction between "law" as mathematical function (Track A) and "law" as ontological principle (Track B).

---

## 1. The Two Senses of "Law"

DET has been using "law" in two distinct senses without clearly separating them:

| Sense | Track | Definition | Falsifiable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L — the law map** | Track A | The mathematical function L: R⁻ → (Ω, Σ, K, C). The "laws of record-keeping" — rules that govern how possibility structures are generated from committed records. | ✅ Yes — if L predicts Ω that doesn't match observation, L is wrong. |
| **Ontological principles** | Track B | The framework for understanding what existence means: primacy of the present, record-growth time, fact genesis, law extension, κ-obscured discoverability. The "laws of being." | ❌ Not directly — but they produce observable fruit (coherence, explanatory power, deadlock resolution). |

---

## 2. Track A: L as the Mathematical Law Map

L is a function. It takes the committed record R⁻ and returns a possibility object (Ω, Σ, K, C). This is:

- **Determinate:** Given identical R⁻, identical output.
- **Falsifiable:** If we observe outcomes that L says were impossible (not in Ω), L is wrong.
- **Observable:** We can test L by comparing predicted Ω with actual outcomes.
- **The subject of F10:** Can L grow by extension? Does L have immutable base + new branches?

**Examples of Track A laws:**
- Conservation constraints (all ω ∈ Ω satisfy conservation).
- The specific form of Π (participation aperture as a function of record variables).
- The κ-dynamics equation (dκ/dt = D∇²κ − (κ−κ_eq)/τ_rec + damage).
- The Newtonian field equation (∇²Φ = 4πG_q·ρ_γ).

---

## 3. Track B: Ontological Principles

These are the "laws of being" — the framework for understanding what existence means. They are:

- **Coherent, not falsifiable:** You cannot run an experiment to prove "only the present IS." But the framework can be evaluated for coherence, explanatory power, and deadlock resolution.
- **Revealed through fruit:** We see their value in the coherence of the overall framework — how well they resolve paradoxes, guide research, and make sense of observations.
- **Subject to governance:** They must not contradict Track A. They must not smuggle metaphysics into equations. They must respect F8-OPEN, NPF split, and agency quarantine.

**Examples of Track B principles:**
- Primacy of the present (only the present IS).
- Record-growth time (time is the accumulation of committed facts).
- Fact genesis (facts are actualized, not merely revealed).
- Law extension (L grows by adding branches, never degrading old ones).
- κ-obscured discoverability (laws can be buried, not destroyed).
- PID-C/PID-M split (identity tracked through record, not stored in it).

---

## 4. The Relationship

Track B principles INTERPRET Track A laws:

| Track A (L) | Track B (interpretation) |
|---|---|
| L: R⁻ → Ω is a fixed function | Laws are discovered, not created |
| L can grow by extension | The universe's lawfulness is an open book, not a closed one |
| κ constrains Ω | Structural history limits what we can discover |
| Ω shrinks with κ | The past can bury what was once known |
| Π provides proper-time scale | The present produces measurable fruit |

Track B does not ADD new mathematical functions to L. It provides the ontological vocabulary for understanding what L's behavior MEANS.

---

## 5. The "Lost Law" Clarified

The "lost law" problem (F10 extension) is about Track A's L:

- A branch of L was discovered (a new mathematical pattern was added to the law map).
- κ accumulated, blocking the record-path to that branch.
- The branch still exists IN L (Track A — the function still generates it given the right R⁻).
- But it is UNDISCOVERABLE from the current state (Track B — the ontological interpretation of what κ-obscured means).

**The law (Track A) is intact. The path to it (Track B — discoverability) is blocked.**

## 6. Governance Implications

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|

| Falsifiability | Track A laws (L) are falsifiable. Track B principles are evaluated, not falsified. |
