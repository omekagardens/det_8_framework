## 2. Independent mathematical route

The producer uses `Fraction` and factored dense-polynomial assembly. This
consumer instead uses normalized `(numerator,denominator)` integer pairs,
denominator-gcd addition, cross-cancelled multiplication and sparse exponent
dictionaries. Its arithmetic and framing are deliberately informed by
textual inspection of the separately expressed RI120 consumer; that is
disclosed reuse, not a claim of an unrelated arithmetic engine. It does not
import that consumer, the new producer, a shared mathematical helper or any
scientific predecessor.

The raw scalar route preserves all four stem ideals in every profile, all
eight E2 terms, all five C2 terms, both H5 one-cap slots and all full
complements. Quotient-first expressions are used for E and the final C2
term; the main C2 terms use direct products. Multiplicities are individual
terms, not orbit-normalized replacements. V is independently summed as the
four stem terms plus two m terms and one j term and cross-checked against
E-m-2j.

For both selected records, U2 is formed by summing all fourteen proper-ideal
polynomials and U3 by summing all twelve. Their complete sparse coefficients
must match the accepted compact expressions. In particular:

    U2: constant 3, linear -A2, quadratic B2, cubic C2;
    U3: constant 2, linear -(1+V), quadratic V.

The consumer obtains q from the exactly zero-constant difference
`(U20-U21)/x` by shifting every nonzero exponent down once, then checks all
three coefficients against the independent delta expressions. The native
D2, D3, v and q(0) signs are checked without selecting rho or s. Positivity
of the full denominator q on the domain remains the accepted RI122 theorem;
there is no new root, gcd, Sturm or full-record campaign.

The final P2/P3 coefficients use the independent handwritten expansion in
INPUT_FORMULAS (14)–(15), not the producer's factored multiplication. The
coefficients of y in the second target are, for example,

    [0, -8D3, 4(vH3+(1+V0)D3), -4(vH3+V0D3)].

Every rational operation is exact and guarded. Degree drops remove internal
zero dictionary entries but all exported arrays are padded to the required
degree cap: six entries for j2, four for j3. The zero polynomial has degree
-1. No division by a target coefficient or its slope occurs.

Endpoint envelopes are accumulated by direct powers `ci*X^i`, separately
for each coefficient, at y=0 and y=Y. The constant term enters each lower
and upper bound once. Negative terms enter only the lower bound; positive
terms enter only the upper. Both endpoint upper bounds <=0 certify uniform
nonpositivity, including identically zero. Both lower bounds >0 certify
uniform strict positivity; every other outcome is unresolved. These are
sufficient bounds, not an exact root classification or point evaluation.

The only native rectangle is the proved containing box
`X=min(R,1/4), Y=1/4`, with R recomputed only from E0/E1. No subdivision,
optimizer, alternative maximum, chosen scale, favorable sample or retry on a
smaller box is built in. One nonpositive target would reject fixed H30; two
positive targets would describe only two local intervals. The accepted
small-x/negative-seed premise in fact precludes simultaneous uniform
positivity on this full rectangle; the generic decision branch is retained
without asserting it will occur for the native input. Shared T1, the other
connected parents and every Di condition remain binding.
