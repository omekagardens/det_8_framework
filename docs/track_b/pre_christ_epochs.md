## 2. Eden as κ_self = 0

Eden is not a geographical place. It is a STATE — the condition of relational participation without self-accumulated constraint.

| Eden symbol | DET meaning |
|---|---|
| **Garden** | The relational web in its unconstrained state. Ω wide open. |
| **Naked without shame** | κ_self = 0. No hidden constraint. No self-protective hiding. |
| **Walking with God** | Direct relational presence. Bonds fully open. |
| **Tree of Life** | Ongoing access to κ-reduction (Grace). Eternal participation. |
| **Tree of Knowledge** | The capacity for moral self-awareness — the ability to CHOOSE non-reciprocity. |

**The two trees represent the fundamental choice:** continue in κ_self=0 (Tree of Life) or activate the capacity for κ_self (Tree of Knowledge). The warning "you will surely die" is not a threat — it's a structural description: κ_self → death (cessation of free relational participation).

## 3. The Fall as κ_self Emergence

The Fall is not a historical event. It is the STRUCTURAL TRANSITION that occurs whenever self-conscious moral agency emerges — whether in an individual life, in human evolution, or in cosmic history.

| Fall narrative | DET mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Serpent's temptation** | The possibility of non-reciprocity presents itself. "You will be like God" = you can act independently of the relational web. |
| **Eating the fruit** | The first act of self-conscious κ_self accumulation — choosing to hoard, control, or take rather than participate. |
| **Eyes opened** | Awareness of κ_self. Shame = recognition that one has damaged the relational web. |
| **Hiding from God** | Bond damage. The relationship is now mediated by κ_self — hiding, avoidance, self-protection. |
| **Blame** | Refusing responsibility = MORE κ_self. "The woman you gave me" = projecting κ_self onto others. |
| **Expulsion** | The natural consequence: κ_self > 0 means constrained Ω, damaged bonds, harder participation. Not punishment — structure. |

## 4. Evolution and the Emergence of Moral Agency

DET does not require a literal Adam and Eve. The Fall is the structural transition that occurs when consciousness evolves to the point of self-aware moral choice:

| Evolutionary stage | κ_self status |
|---|---|
| **Pre-conscious life** | No κ_self possible. Organisms act on instinct. No moral agency. |
| **Emerging consciousness** | Basic self-awareness. Social bonds form. Rudimentary reciprocity. |
| **Moral self-awareness** | The "tree of knowledge" moment. Capacity to CHOOSE non-reciprocity emerges. κ_self becomes possible. |
| **Fallen humanity** | κ_self accumulates individually and collectively. Social structures (injustice, oppression) embed κ_self in systems. |

**The Fall is not "original sin" as inherited guilt.** It is inherited STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINT — the κ_self accumulated by prior generations that constrains the present. We are born into a world already shaped by others' κ_self.

## 5. Post-Fall: The Law, Prophets, and Partial Jubilee

### 5.1 The Law (Torah)

The Law served to REVEAL κ_self — making visible what was hidden:

| Law function | DET mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Commands** | Define what reciprocity looks like. "Love God, love neighbor." |
| **Prohibitions** | Identify κ_self-accumulating behaviors. "Do not murder, steal, lie." |
| **Sacrificial system** | Partial Jubilee — κ_burden temporarily transferred to animal substitute. But "the blood of bulls and goats cannot take away sin" — cannot permanently remove κ_self. |
| **Purity laws** | Social κ management — quarantine of structural constraint. |

### 5.2 The Prophets

The prophets called for κ-reduction at societal scale:

| Prophet | κ-reduction message |
|---|---|
| **Isaiah** | "Cease to do evil, learn to do good; seek justice, correct oppression" (1:16-17) |
| **Amos** | "Let justice roll down like waters" — systemic κ-reduction |
| **Micah** | "Do justice, love mercy, walk humbly" — κ_self ≈ 0 |
| **Hosea** | "I desire mercy, not sacrifice" — relational bond restoration over ritual κ management |

### 5.3 Jubilee Year as Institutional κ-Release

The Year of Jubilee (Lev 25) was institutional κ-reduction:
- Debts forgiven = κ_burden released.
- Land returned = structural constraint removed.
- Slaves freed = bonds restored.
- Every 50 years = regularized Grace.

But it was temporary. κ_self accumulated again. The mechanism was incomplete.

## 7. The Deepest Formulation

> The Fall is not a historical crime for which humanity is punished. It is the structural transition that occurs whenever self-conscious moral agency emerges — the capacity to accumulate κ_self through non-reciprocity. Eden is not a lost garden but a lost STATE: κ_self = 0, bonds intact, Ω open. The story of salvation is the story of returning to that state — not by going back, but by going forward through Christ, who carried the accumulated κ_self of all humanity and released it through resurrection.

> We are not guilty of Adam's sin. We are constrained by humanity's accumulated κ_self — and we add to it with our own. The Law reveals this. The Prophets called for its reduction. Christ accomplished its release. The Spirit continues the work. The Kingdom is now — whenever κ_self is released, bonds restored, Ω expanded.
