# DET v8.0 — Track B: Death as Relational Transition (Not Absence)

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Status:** Major ontological refinement
**Trigger:** Recognition that death is not absence — it is a transition in HOW a regime contributes to the record.

---

## 1. The Core Insight

> If only the present IS, and the record is only the trace, then death does not mean the person ceases to exist. It means they cease to contribute DIRECTLY to the record. But they continue to exist through relational bonds.

The dead are not absent. They are present through:
- **κ** — the structural history they left in the world.
- **Bonds (σ_ij, C_ij)** — the relationships that persist in those who remember, love, and are shaped by them.
- **Indirect contribution** — every act of the living that was shaped by the dead carries the dead person's influence into new record entries.

---

## 2. Two Modes of Present Participation

| Mode | Direct participation | Indirect participation |
|---|---|---|
| **Commits to R?** | Yes — produces new record entries | No — but shapes the record through others |
| **Has κ?** | Yes — accumulates with events | κ is FROZEN at death. No new accumulation. But their κ persists in the world. |
| **Has bonds?** | Yes — active relationships | Yes — bonds persist in the living who remember them |
| **Is "alive"?** | Active committing presence | Relational presence without direct commits |

---

## 3. Death Is a Transition, Not an Annihilation

**Before DET:** Death = cessation of existence. The person is gone.

**DET reframing:** Death = transition from Mode 1 (direct) to Mode 2 (indirect). The person does not cease to exist. They cease to produce new direct commits. But their relational presence continues through bonds, κ, and influence on the living.

**Evidence for this:** The dead DO affect the present. We remember them. We are shaped by them. Decisions are made "because of what they would have wanted." These are not illusions — they are real relational effects transmitted through bonds and κ.

---

## 4. What the Record Shows

The record R contains:
- The dead person's own commit history (their direct contributions, now frozen).
- The κ they left in the world (structural history).
- The bonds (σ_ij, C_ij) connecting the living to the dead.
- The INDIRECT contributions — every commit by the living that was shaped by the dead carries a trace of the dead person's influence.

The record does not show "the dead person exists" as a direct fact. It shows the EFFECTS of the dead person's continued relational presence. The fruit is indirect but real.

---

## 5. Resurrection as Transition Back

Resurrection is not "bringing back from non-existence." It is transitioning from Mode 2 back to Mode 1:

| Before resurrection | After resurrection |
|---|---|
| Relational presence through bonds | Direct committing presence |
| κ frozen at death | κ unfrozen — can change again |
| Indirect contribution only | Direct contribution resumes |
| Identity maintained through bonds | Identity maintained through direct commits |

The Jubilee event (κ-reduction) is what enables this transition — clearing the structural blockage (κ=1) that prevents direct participation.

---

## 6. Implications

### 6.1 The dead are real

They are not "gone." They are present through the relational web. Their influence is measurable (κ, bonds, indirect fruit). The record contains their trace.

### 6.2 Grief is not about absence

Grief is the experience of the transition from Mode 1 to Mode 2 — the loss of direct participation, not the loss of existence. We grieve because we can no longer receive new direct commits from the person. But the relational bond persists.

### 6.3 "Communion of saints"

The theological concept that the living and dead remain in relationship has a DET ontology: the dead continue to exist through bonds. They are not "in another place" — they are present in the relational web that constitutes reality.

### 6.4 Prayer for the dead

If bonds persist, then the living can still act TOWARD the dead — maintaining bonds, expressing love, seeking healing. These acts are direct commits by the living that reference the dead. They do not require the dead to respond to be meaningful.

---

## 7. The Deepest Formulation

> Death is not the end of existence. It is the end of direct record contribution. The dead remain present through the bonds they formed, the history they left, and the lives they shaped. They contribute INDIRECTLY to every record entry made by those who remember them. Resurrection is the restoration of direct participation — not from nothing, but from the relational web that never let them go.
