## 1. Immediate theorem: the present law cannot fill a spacetime region

**Port-chain lemma.** In every L_XYZ history, any two events touching port a
are comparable, and the same holds for port b. Every event touches a, b or both.
Therefore the entire poset is the union of two chains and its width is at
most two.

**Proof.** A later event touching p includes the previous p-head and its
inclusive downset in its precursor ideal. Induction makes the p-events a
chain. In an antichain, no two events can share a port. There are only two
ports and every event has at least one, so an antichain has at most two
elements. This is independent of the quantum state and outcomes. ∎

**Qualified continuum obstruction.** Consider a causally convex neighborhood
of a smooth Lorentzian spacetime with at least one spatial dimension. Choose
three sufficiently small positive-volume open regions U_1,U_2,U_3 about
distinct points on a local spacelike slice, so that points in different
regions are causally unrelated. Any map preserving the event order into that
neighborhood and hitting all three regions requires three mutually incomparable
source events. The port-chain lemma forbids this.

Consequently width-two histories cannot form dense, causally faithful,
volume-filling approximations to such a neighborhood. This rules out that
particular role for the current L_XYZ. It does **not** rule out sparse
embeddings, a pair of worldline-like traces, selected-statistic agreement,
degenerate/time-only limits or representations that forget the event order.
Width two is not order dimension two and is not spacetime dimension two.

For comparison only, a Poisson sample of density ν in a *supplied* neighborhood
has probability at most Σ_i exp[−ν Vol(U_i)] of missing at least one of
the three regions. Thus its probability of width at most two tends to zero
as ν increases. This is an analytical comparison using a declared sampling
model, not a proposed generator, fixture run or new coverage contract.

**Decision:** retain L_XYZ as a finite quantum-record protocol. Additional
quantum verification, stronger purification axioms or a larger residual Hilbert
space cannot remove its fixed two-chain obstruction. No automatic port
enlargement or substitute growth law is designed in this sitting.

## 2. What order-and-volume mathematics can actually establish

In the standard smooth, dimension-greater-than-two setting, a chronological
isomorphism between suitable past-and-future distinguishing spacetimes fixes
their conformal geometry. The hypotheses already concern continuum
spacetimes; this is not a theorem that every finite poset generates a manifold.
See the HKMM formulation in [Surya, section 2](https://arxiv.org/html/1903.11544v2).

The remaining volume step has a simple conditional proof. If a smooth
conformal identification gives f* g′=α²g with α>0 in dimension d, then

\[
f^*dV_{g'}=\alpha^d dV_g.
\]

If the **local volume measures** also agree, α^d=1 almost everywhere;
smoothness and full support make α=1 everywhere. The identification is then
an isometry. Equality of one total count is insufficient to fix an arbitrary
position-dependent α. Neither cardinality alone nor the conversion of
detector counts to volume is established by this conditional argument.

Current reconstruction results sharpen this picture; it would be inaccurate
to say that no version of causal-set reconstruction has been proved.
[Braun, Theorem 1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01907v1) gives an isometry
criterion for pre-existing smooth causally continuous, future chronocomplete
spacetimes of common dimension d≥3 and common finite volume. It uses equality
of chronological adjacency distributions for **every** finite sample size
under i.i.d. normalized-volume sampling. One finite history or a bounded
collection of estimator checks does not supply those hypotheses.

[Müller's September 2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01719v4) also
distinguishes true and false formulations of the Hauptvermutung. These are
reasons to name the exact theorem and its assumptions, not to import a general
emergence conclusion into DET. No such theorem has been applied successfully
to L_XYZ here.

## 4. Relativity is more than schedule invariance

There are three distinct questions:

- **Record consistency:** commuting incomparable operations give the same
  complete record distribution across schedules. This has conditional finite
  examples already; it does not define a light cone.
- **Lorentzian kinematics:** the derived geometry has the appropriate causal
  and interval structure. Lorentz-frame relations can then be checked in its
  local flat description. A numerical invariant speed and physical units
  require a declared calibration; they are not inferred from graph relabeling.
- **Physical correspondence:** actual signal, timing and detector records can
  be mapped to the candidate event structure under documented instrument,
  localization and synchronization assumptions. This is separate from the
  present internal derivation task and is not initiated here.

At the eventual joint correspondence step, “independent ports” must be related
to the **recovered** causal structure; one cannot identify them with spatially
separated laboratories by notation alone. Selective quantum conditioning must
also be distinguished from controllable signalling in recorded marginals.
The four-level residual example does not constitute relativistic QFT or a
physical particle-dynamics model.

prove that the full joint object can never admit a geometric correspondence.
Any later summary must remain question-relative and retain what its claimed

## 5. Mass and gravity: the added research objectives

**Mass is a dynamical question.** Seek a justified description of persistent
excitations and propagation, with an energy/time and momentum/translation
interpretation where those quantities exist. In an appropriate relativistic
regime, a rest-mass or inertial-mass interpretation must follow from that
dynamics. Calling a graph count, residual rank, κ coordinate or arbitrary
eigenvalue “mass” does not establish it. A spectral gap can be a candidate
ingredient, but needs the physical correspondence. Initially distinguish
existence of massive behavior, selection of its scale, and prediction of an
actual mass spectrum; these are different achievements.

**Gravity is a coupled-dynamics question.** The research target includes how
the same matter dynamics supplies a source and how the event/geometry sector
responds, including feedback on matter. The comparison with GR must use
energy-momentum rather than treating rest-mass count as the complete source.
The relevant conventional benchmark includes matter coupling and Einstein
dynamics; see [Tong, chapter 4, especially section 4.5](https://davidtong.org/pdfs/teaching/general-relativity/gr4.pdf).
Whether an inertial mass and a gravitational response obey the appropriate
equivalence principle is a result to establish, not a notational identification.

Two approaches are explicitly permitted and must remain distinguishable:

- **Conditional coupling:** adopt conventional relativistic matter/GR
  structures openly and investigate whether the record calculus admits a
  consistent formulation. This can produce a theorem about an assumed model,
  but it is not native emergence.
- **Native derivation:** propose a primitive-level joint law and show that its
  limiting matter and geometric dynamics have the required mass and gravity
  behavior. Extra axioms, degrees of freedom, initial conditions and couplings
  must remain visible. A supplied Einstein action or mass term cannot serve as
  evidence that the same structure has been derived from weaker DET premises.

For either route, examine consistency before physical claims: preservation of
probability, causal compatibility, the applicable conservation/constraint
relations, stability in the claimed regime and the weak-field or other declared
comparison limit. Numerical values of masses and gravitational coupling are
not predicted merely by choosing units. Recovering GR, proposing a departure
from GR, and interpreting its metric as a record are three different claims.

This permits research now; it does not adopt a new source or resurrect the
specific retired κ-gravity model. No empirical discriminator is required merely
to formulate or prove an internal mathematical candidate. A claim that a new
physical model improves on the baseline would require an explicit empirical
comparison; ontology promotion retains its separate evidential burden.
