## 1. The Bridge Question

## 2. Three Models of Identity and History

### Model A — Material Identity

Identity depends on current material configuration:

\[
I = f(M_t)
\]

If the material configuration is destroyed (death), identity is destroyed. Resurrection would require recreating the exact material configuration — which is a copy, not the original (duplicate test).

### Model B — Pattern Identity

Identity depends on information pattern:

\[
I = f(\text{information}_t)
\]

If the pattern is preserved, identity persists. But two identical patterns produce two identical persons (duplicate test fails). Pattern alone is insufficient.

### Model C — DET Record Identity (PID-C)

Identity depends on unique causal-relational lineage:

\[
I = f(\mathcal R_{history}, \text{causal provenance})
\]

The record \(\mathcal R_{history}\) is the complete committed history of the regime. Causal provenance is the unique chain of commit events linking the earlier and later regimes. No duplicate can have the same provenance.

## 4. The Resurrection Question Reframed

**Original (theological):** Can a dead person be restored to life?

**DET-reframed:** Given the complete committed record \(\mathcal R_{history}\) of a selfhood-bearing regime, can a new commit event produce a living presence \(r\) such that:

1. \(d \prec r\) (death remains in causal past).
2. \(\mathcal R_r \supset \mathcal R_d\) (record extends, does not replace).
3. PID-C holds: unique causal lineage links the earlier and later regimes.
4. κ at \(r\) reflects the history without being dominated by death.
5. Agency (M) is present, not merely replayed.

This is the F9→O9-RID bridge: resurrection is the ultimate test of whether **a complete relational history can be reactualized into a new living presence** — the same question F9 asks about any fact, applied to the hardest case.

## 5. What DET Can and Cannot Say

### Can Say (within governance)
- Death remains a committed fact: \(d \prec r\).
- The record persists and can be extended.
- PID-C provides necessary conditions for identity (unique lineage).
- κ can change: structural carrying of death can be transformed without erasing death.
- The framework is coherent with local commit events (no global "now" needed).

### Cannot Say (without overclaiming)
- That resurrection has occurred historically (historical claim, outside DET).
- That PID-C is sufficient for numerical identity (PID-M is metaphysical).
- That a physical mechanism for resurrection exists (divine action is M/H).
- That agency is restored (agency is M throughout).
