## 4. Answer to the Central Question

\[
\boxed{
\text{After removing agency and metaphysical interpretation,}
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\text{does any distinctive, coherent, falsifiable DET physics remain?}
}
\]

### Honest answer (August 2026):

**Currently: No empirically validated distinctive DET physics survives.**

What survives is:

1. A **record-kernel calculus** — a formal grammar for transition models that is structurally equivalent to standard stochastic/quantum transition theories. Useful as a framework; not a physical theory.
2. A **participation aperture ansatz** — a proposed formula for proper-time accumulation that is DET-specific in form but not yet tested, not yet derived from first principles, and not yet mapped to relativistic energy-momentum.
3. A **mutable-\(q\) hypothesis** — a distinctive structural-history variable whose dynamics, measurement protocol, and identifiability are unresolved.
4. A **position on Bell** — the rejection of factorizability via nonfactorizable relational records, which is a proposal without a working model.
5. An **unvalidated DET 7 inheritance** — gravity, coherence, clock, and stability results whose survival without agency is unknown.

### What would change this answer:

1. **DET 7 regression passes** → DET inherits validated physical results (gravity, clocks, coherence) that are novel relative to standard physics. This alone could justify DET as a candidate physical theory.
2. **\(\Pi\) produces a risky clock prediction** → A specific, testable deviation from standard proper-time formulas that can be measured.
3. **\(q\) is given an operational definition and measurement protocol** → Makes the most distinctive DET variable empirically accessible.
4. **A nonfactorizable joint kernel reproduces CHSH without superdeterminism** → Solves the Bell blocker.

### Recommendation:

P0.5 should not claim that DET is a physical theory. It should produce:

1. The regression results (or a stub plan if code is unavailable).
2. The \(q\)-physics ledger (operationalizing the most distinctive surviving variable).
3. The Peres-Mermin and CHSH correspondence models (establishing quantum compatibility).
4. An honest classification: **interpretive framework** until at least one risky prediction survives testing.
