## Licensed-G snapshot contract — structural part only

Let C = (V, ≺, R) be a finite committed snapshot. Past-closed means no
committed predecessor of an included event is omitted; it is not restriction
of a pre-drawn future block. An allowed S must be an order ideal:
v ∈ S and u ≺ v imply u ∈ S. Candidate alternatives may describe S and a
possible record x without allocating any future event in V.

Only after sampling (S,x) from a specified joint K does append create a
fresh event e:

\[
V^+=V\cup\{e\},\qquad
\prec^+=\prec\;\cup\;\{(s,e):s\in S\},\qquad
R^+|_V=R,\quad R^+(e)=x.
\]

The slot x includes the setting/outcome and declared dependencies. The ideal
condition makes ≺⁺ transitive; freshness and the absence of outgoing edges
from e prevent cycles. Thus e is maximal, its past is exactly S, and all
old relations survive. # is the counting measure of V⁺. This structural
statement neither chooses S nor supplies probabilities or quantum updates.

V stores committed trace entries, not presently existing past events.
“Licensed growing-block G” is the owner's operational trace convention, not
a change to radical presentism. No global now or observer foliation is used;
R restricted to S is not identified with a geometric J⁻(e).

## Named gaps and rejected substitutions

**GAP — joint alternatives and commit law.** The declared chain
L(R⁻) → (Ω, event algebra, 𝔇, constraints) does not specify these assignments
for each committed parent, the allowed-ideal selector, or the partition
linking alternatives to (S,x). For normalized, strongly positive 𝔇,
K(S,x) = 𝔇(A_(S,x), A_(S,x)) gives normalized weights on a complete, exactly
decoherent partition; neither that partition nor its selector is provided.
Approximate decoherence is not exact
normalization. [Declared architecture](../../record_kernel_physics.md#4-revised-minimal-track-a-architecture),
[partition caution](../../QUANTUM_RECORD_STRUCTURE_RESEARCH.md#preserve-quantum-information-before-forming-an-ordinary-kernel).

**GAP — quantum update and composition.** A positive pair-kernel can admit a
Gram representation without specifying a full instrument, conditional update,
or between-birth identification/composition. The native outcome-resolved lift,
including zero branches and subsequent quantum information, is missing.
Supplying scalar weights, a Gram factorization or a preferred input state
does not supply this lift. Pair-kernels and instruments are explicitly
different data types in the [quantum charter](../../QUANTUM_RECORD_STRUCTURE_RESEARCH.md#preserve-quantum-information-before-forming-an-ordinary-kernel).
This does not require deriving 𝔇 from more primitive axioms this sitting;
𝔇 is allowed, but its joint generative use is not specified.

**GAP — stable native covariance.** The concrete QR-05A law uses supplied
ℂ², Kraus coefficients/phases and percolation p. Its positive diamond
identities cannot be transferred to an unspecified L/𝔇 law. Normalizing
weights alone does not ensure birth covariance; past-parity Z/X shows that
past-local reads alone fail quantum commutation.
[Existing counterexamples](../qr-05a-quantum-births-2026-09-05/RESULTS.md).
MAM-0's `LawMap.generate` supplies a conserving two-node classical transfer
rule, not an ideal-valued quantum birth map. The existing abstract-law and
change-point discussions likewise do not supply this F.

**Primitive-input disposition:** metric, Minkowski (t,x), Johnston K(z),
Rideout–Sorkin p, Hilbert space, amplitudes, ambient birth counter used as law,
future vertices and observer foliation are all excluded as construction
inputs. They may appear only as after-the-fact comparisons, never feed back
to generate the trace. In particular, relabeling percolation as L, importing
a mesh, using `stage_zx`, or preallocating future `CausalGraph` vertices is
rejected. The gaps do not prove that any forbidden input is necessary.
No replacement law or fixture is inserted to make a certificate pass.
