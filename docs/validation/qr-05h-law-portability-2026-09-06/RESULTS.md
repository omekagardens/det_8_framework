## Comparison and mathematical structure

Three globally known continuation policies are evaluated at each same history:

- iid_half: every eligible vertex is independently retained with probability 1/2.
- iid_color: odd original-ID eligible vertices use 1/3; even vertices use 2/3.
- block_coin: one shared fair coin for the odd eligible block and an independent
  shared fair coin for the even eligible block.

Parity is a supplied acquisition mark, not a geometric property inferred from
an ID. Fixed vertices—including fixed interior 3—are never randomly colored or
assigned a block coin. Hidden source-profile names remain excluded from keys.
The menu is a vector of counterfactual predictions, not a mixture or policy prior.

For color-graded chains D[q,o,e], fixed vertices have no random support degree:

    E[N_q] = sum_(o,e) D[q,o,e] eta(o,e).

The three inclusion factors are respectively 2^-(o+e), (1/3)^o(2/3)^e, and
2^-([o>0]+[e>0]). This supplies an explicit mean predictor, not a fitted equality.
For block_coin, D also determines all four count vectors from neither color,
odd only, even only and both. Their equal-weight mixture gives the entire count
law; absent blocks and repeated vectors coalesce. Different overlap structures
can nevertheless produce different IID laws at identical D.

## Fine records and coarse counts have different transport conditions

At supplied S, let q(U),p(U) be source/target labeled-record laws. Fine weights
p/q exist only where q>0; other entries stay null, including 0/0. The weighted
fine measure recovers only target mass on source-supported records. It is
not renormalized.

For joint count z, write Q(z),P(z) for the two count laws and A(z) for target
mass from source-supported fine records. On Q(z)>0:

    coarse_weight - E_source[fine_weight | z] = (P(z)-A(z))/Q(z).

Its Q-weighted sum is fine missing mass minus coarse missing mass. Full fine
support implies full coarse support, but the converse fails. Fine weight
measurability on source-positive count fibers is a separate condition: a coarse
ratio may be a conditional average without being the fine pointwise ratio.
Across fine-support holes, even that equality needs the displayed residual.

Transport diagnostics assume S and the laws are supplied. Predictive sufficiency
does not itself establish that a compressed provenance summary exposes weights.

Across all 5,472 directed state/policy transports, including identities and
diagnostic zero-first-mass states:

- 4,568 have full fine support.
- 4,720 have full coarse support.
- 152 have full coarse but incomplete fine support; 108 are at positive-P1 states.
- 1,905 have fine weights that are not count-measurable.

These are finite comparison counts, not real-world failure frequencies.

## Retained controls

1. **Identity-sensitive procedure.** Supported singleton S={1} versus S={2},
   final T empty (history IDs 1/3), has equal marked and anonymous-block orders.
   Colored-sampling means N1 are 1/3 versus 2/3.

2. **Correlations are not marginals.** Antichains S={1,3} versus S={1,2}
   (history IDs 11/5) have equal marked orders and IID-half laws. Under block
   sampling every vertex marginal is 1/2, but P(N1=1) is 0 versus 1/2.

3. **Means versus distributions.** G's star/path masks 57/27 (history IDs 521/155)
   have identical D and menu means. IID-half relation variances remain 15/16
   versus 13/16. Colored IID means are [1,2,5/9,0], but relation variances are
   68/81 versus 52/81. The full block_coin count laws are equal.

4. **Weight access is distinct.** On S={1,2}, half→color has full support.
   Singleton observations share a count vector but have weights 4/9 versus 16/9.
   Their coarse weight 10/9 is a conditional average, not either fine ratio.

5. **Reverse transport loses mass.** On S={1,3}, half→block weights are 2 on
   empty/full and 0 on singletons. Block→half misses target mass 1/2 and coarse
   support also fails at N1=1. The supported result retains mass 1/2.

6. **Coarse success despite fine failure.** On S={1,2,3}, block→half misses
   masks [1,3,4,6], target mass 1/2. All count atoms N1=0..3 remain source-positive:
   source masses [1,1,1,1]/4, target [1,3,3,1]/8. Coarse weights are
   [1/2,3/2,3/2,1/2]; all accessible fine weights are 1/2. The coarse target
   is fully recovered; the fine target is not.

These controls use case 0 with supported current histories. Zero histories stay
in the evidence with null class IDs, not as counterexamples to supported-domain
sufficiency.

## Applicable value, limits and next gate

The usable contract now includes the question, observed record, eligibility/
fixed marks, acquisition law and support domain. It provides a precise audit
of whether a changed measurement procedure invalidates a compressed summary,
and whether a proposed correction recovers labeled observations or only counts.

This finite model does not infer an unknown apparatus law or establish empirical
accuracy, geometry reconstruction, continuum convergence, Einstein dynamics,
DET ontology or recursive closure. RET/core, dependencies and the temporary
standalone model sheet remain unchanged. The change-of-measure principles are
standard mathematics; sources and the exact protocol are in [README.md](README.md).

**Proposed QR-05I: analytic portability contract.** Consolidate the definitions
and verify two-color IID count-law polynomials P_z(x,y) coefficient-by-coefficient,
instead of merely adding more chosen rates. Separate equality across a whole
parameter domain from equality at H's selected point. Preserve block_coin as
an average of the four deterministic parameter-corner laws, not as an interior
IID law. Justify any interpolation cross-check by the finite degree bound.
Preserve support-boundary caveats and counterexamples. This is proposed, not
executed; no Lean installation, RET adapter or physical coupling is introduced.
