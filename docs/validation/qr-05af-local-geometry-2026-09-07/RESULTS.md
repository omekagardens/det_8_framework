## Main result

We now have a generic, record-local interface for marked interval counts and
three scalar-kernel coefficients. It consumes retained IDs and their induced
order, fixed probes, supplied density and a complete selection law—not a
fixture name or hidden geometric truth. Both engines agree exactly with the
private literal oracle on all 1,280 fixed packets, including probability-zero
rows and separate wrong-channel examples.

The geometric limitation remains visible: correct observation-design weighting
does not remove finite-mesh discrepancy, supply local volume information, or
turn order into an identified metric. The base and active-warp models have
identical complete public packets and results under each matching design,
but different supplied local volumes. This is a successful diagnostic, not
successful reconstruction of the hidden geometry.

## What is newly operational

For each marked interval, the packet interface returns observed chain
inventories and raw, joint-inclusion, marginal-product and uniform-rate
estimates of the causal indicator, intervening-event count and ordered-pair
count. It converts those to the formal coefficients

    C/2, -Nhat/(4 rho), Rhat/(8 rho²).

Only the producer/auditor has the complete source order needed to turn the
supplied selection law into the full observation law. That layer computes
exact means, full cross-probe/cross-degree covariances,
support completeness, and geometric comparisons. The public API returns none
of those unavailable source quantities. Valid transitivity does not authenticate
an input as the correct induced observation of a hidden source.

The twenty cases comprise fourteen mesh/design combinations with seven marked
probes and six Ferrers/S3/chain controls with one probe. There are 312
case-specific questions, 997 full-source chain terms and 31,152 observed-chain
occurrences across all retained rows. Of 1,280 rows, 554 have positive
probability and 726 are structural zero rows. These are finite enumeration
counts, not independent physical experiments.

The constant, linear and quadratic scalar-kernel terms are a supplied
comparison calculus, not a new quantum operation or gravitational law.
The established chain-sum motivation and sampling attribution are recorded
in the protocol; neither is attributed as an original physical postulate.

## Sampling bias cannot be hidden in geometric discrepancy

For every method/question with geometry, the evidence preserves the signed
identity

    mean estimate - continuum comparison
      = design-estimation bias + finite-model geometric discrepancy.

Covariance is not used to explain away either term. For supported questions,
joint-inclusion correction removes design-estimation bias exactly, but the
finite target need not equal its continuum comparison.

The nonuniform design shows why local tests matter. A wrong uniform-rate
correction leaves the whole event-count mean at its correct value nine, yet
gives lower/upper central interval count means 8/3 and 10/3 instead of three
each. Its whole ordered-pair bias is -1/9; the local biases are different and
signed. Correct marginal-product and joint weighting agree under this
independent nonuniform design.

Under all-or-none observation, wrong marginal-product weighting gives a
whole ordered-pair mean of forty instead of twenty-seven. Joint weighting
recovers twenty-seven. Fixed interior probe events are never accidentally
treated as random vertices: their eligible support is empty where appropriate.

Exact joint covariance agrees both with enumeration of centered sample
vectors and with the ordered source-chain union-inclusion formula. Cross-probe
dependence comes from the same observation, not independent sampling of each
probe. A zero union inclusion contributes a negative covariance term rather
than a division by zero. Coefficient covariances retain their signed scale
factors; positive semidefiniteness does not mean every covariance entry is
positive.

For the same two disjoint central intervals, the corrected count covariance
changes with the joint sampling law:

| Design | Lower/upper count covariance | Whole count variance |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Independent half-retention | 0 | 6 |
| All-or-none | 9 | 36 |
| Fixed-size-two | -18/5 | 0 |

These are exact design quantities, not single-record error bars. The negative
cross-region covariance under fixed-size sampling is retained. Under independent
half-retention, the degree-one lower-center coefficient and degree-two
bottom-to-p3 coefficient have covariance -1/93312, illustrating the signed
kernel transformation rather than an erroneous absolute-value covariance.

## Missing support and wrong observation channels

Fixed-size-two sampling supports every degree-zero/one/two chain in this gate.
It is not reused as a missing-degree-three example: degree three is outside
AF's scope.

Singleton observation does lose pair support. The mesh whole-pair supported
target is fourteen rather than twenty-seven; thirteen two-eligible-vertex
pairs are missing. All seven mesh degree-two questions have unsupported terms.
The other full-source pair targets and their omissions remain explicit;
nothing divides by zero or inserts a hidden source contribution into the
observer estimate.

A separate, stronger obstruction is reproduced: Ferrers and chain singleton
observations have identical complete positive-probability public packet laws,
but full pair counts six and fifteen. No rule of those observations can
recover both hidden targets uniformly over those two sources. This is
channel-relative nonidentification, not a conclusion drawn merely from an
unsupported estimator term.

Retaining original Hasse links and taking reachability is another observation
channel, even when its output is a valid transitive order. On the mesh with
half retention, at least one marked probe comparison is lost with probability
63/64; under singleton or fixed-size-two retention the mismatch probability is
one. Separate first positive-probability wrong-channel packets are passed
through both engines without silently restoring missing relations. An API
schema check cannot certify observation origin.
