# GDM018 — a specific physical-readout correspondence rejected

Status: accepted scoped proposal assessment after distinct source review and coordinator disposition. This closes one proposed application; it is neither an experimental result nor a general impossibility theorem.

The proposed application used a Rydberg positronium electric interferometer to read the individual normalized-response distribution in [GDM017](../gdm017-phase-response-laws-2026-11-01/CALCULATION.md). The published architecture prepares quantum states, uses state-dependent electric forces and infers an interferometer phase from terminal state populations. It is a proposed gravity experiment, not a demonstrated measurement of the GDM017 statistic. [McCaul, Cassidy and Hogan, NJP 27 (2025) 095001](https://inspirehep.net/files/b342ebf50dc1060075b43d59ea9dc7c5).

The direct correspondence fails at identified clauses:

- The quantum preparation has not been mapped to the fixed-radius classical circular ensemble with uniformly unobserved orbital phase.
- Its state-dependent drive is not the stipulated ideal force intervention. Its apparatus flight time is not the fixed model readout time.
- A terminal detector outcome, an estimated population and an inferred interferometer phase are different from the individual matched gravity and force response derivatives. The interferometer phase is not the model's internal orbital phase.
- Destructive readout does not supply a second intervention on the same intact preparation. A newly prepared atom is not automatically a copy of the same hidden phase. A justified preparation or observation model could address this issue; repetition alone does not supply that model.

Accordingly, this arrangement is retired **as a direct implementation of the unchanged GDM017 event**. The accepted formal coefficient laws and their probabilities remain intact. Other positronium experiments and future readout mappings are not ruled out. No native DET prediction or physical mass identification follows.

The assessment used two further primary sources at limited scope. A demonstrated positronium excitation and field-ionisation experiment supplies a component example, not the required matched response pair. [Heiss et al., arXiv:1809.07854v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.07854v1). Circular wave-packet constructions provide preparation concepts; they do not establish a classical positronium response ensemble. [Yoshida et al., NIMB 279 (2012), 4–7](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1623709).

The next selected question is one explicit preparation-to-record mapping with declared states, controls, recorded outcomes and target correspondences. Conditional design hypotheses may be constructed before full experimental validation. Calibration, response-level omitted physics, disturbance, losses, finite-error threshold leakage and resolution at the model's shrinking response scale remain separate obligations. No experiment, simulator or scientific execution is admitted by this assessment.
