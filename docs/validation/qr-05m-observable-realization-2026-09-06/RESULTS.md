## Mean update and full-law closure are different results

each eligible counted support survives with probability x²y². Induced
restriction preserves its internal relation. Linearity gives

    E[M(U) | S] = x² y² M(S).

This argument does not require disjoint motifs or independence between
their survival indicators. A separate K2,3 toy has three overlapping
supports and expected count 3x²y²; all 32 induced outcomes are retained.
That toy is a recognizer/expectation control, not an added sampling-domain
state or a general closure certificate.

The mean identity is checked in all 257 certified states, across 4,112
coefficient cells. Full recursive prediction is established separately:
all members of each (frame,B,M) class have the same complete pushed
polynomial row. Its quotient obeys fresh independent-stage composition

    P_(x,y) P_(a,b) = P_(xa,yb).

The certificate checks all four-variable coefficient cells, including
independent stage rates and boundary behavior. Equal means alone would
not establish this; the retained B failure is the concrete warning.

Since the actual candidate partition equals Q*, it is also the coarsest
strongly closed B-refinement on this specified finite domain. That does
not prove that M is a uniquely necessary encoding, minimum storage, a
speedup, trace-minimality, or sufficient on unseen orders.
