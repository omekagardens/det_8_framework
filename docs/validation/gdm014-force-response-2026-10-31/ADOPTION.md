# Coordinator adoption

GDM014 is accepted only as the formal force-response result in CALCULATION.md, following the completed independent review. The stated canonical preparation, equal-mass conservative Coulomb model, ideal -f Qx drive, finite noncollision time and small-speed assumptions are essential. No laboratory realization or rigorous full-model response-error margin is established.

The result supplies a reusable error check: a coordinate correction that vanishes on an unforced baseline may survive differentiation with respect to the control. A force-reference response therefore cannot be transferred solely from equality of baseline centers. A consistent change of variables still preserves a consistently transformed observable.

The coordinator selected GDM015 to assess one explicit source gradient omission in a matched gravity-response calculation, comparing two declared truncated models. It is an unaccepted follow-up; restoring that one term would not establish a complete physical gravitational model. The earlier GDM013 applicability boundary and the conditional GDM011/012 toy theorem remain unchanged.
