## What is established analytically

- Full onto-both correspondences obey symmetry and triangle inequalities.
  Zero distance identifies twin quotients, not multiplicity. The audited
  fork pair has correspondence distance 1/10 but best-bijection error 1.
- A strict profile-gap bound, 2D_C<min(σ_X,σ_Y), makes every optimal
  correspondence a bijection. Equality is insufficient. This supplies a
  positive criterion for safely using the cheaper comparison class.
- Under supplied uniform ambient coverage and kernel-error bounds, finite
  comparison approximates the full bounded-kernel comparison within
  2ε_M+η_M+2ε_N+η_N. Finite sampled profiles cannot certify those premises.
- Diameter-normalized shape and calibrated absolute error have explicit
  comparison bounds. Global scale normalization does not prevent local
  point merging. One-sided fits are not generally symmetric distances.
- The declared DD error ℓU differs from U/meanL by ℓ meanL. Two exact
  families show why neither convergence implication holds without the
  corresponding noncollapse/boundedness assumptions. Trimmed discrepancies
  do not control a supremum over unreported pairs.
- All-pair max-plus closure is the least order-superadditive majorant and
  has fixed-order Lipschitz constant n−1. Cover-only recovery fails even
  for a superadditive input; pointwise domination does not compare errors.
  Zero-weight additive paths do not obey the prior multiplicative support
  theorem.
