local record law unchanged but changes the true continuous-profile integral.
This proves an observation-relative identifiability obstruction for the
expanded profile class. It does not invalidate the restricted bilinear
model. More shots at the same sites cannot remove this missing information.

Three independent conceptual reviews covered repository lineage, conventional
quantum/geometric comparators and adversarial observation controls. Their
findings were incorporated into the specification:

- Declare a local preparation/response map on the domain before discussing
  an unknown interior profile. Four isolated states alone are insufficient.
- Require continuity/point-localization separately from old almost-everywhere
  field-error assumptions; keep physical axis transport and drift unclaimed.
- Use fresh independent copies, labeled complete records and explicit access;
  repeated unreset measurements and missing records are not IID samples.
- Keep the weights independent of hidden targets. Do not mistake arithmetic
  receiver inputs, imposed phases or ideal labels for calibrated detectors.
- Separate finite-shot variation, readout calibration and interpolation
  uncertainty; the new conditional bound does not inherit BA's certificates.
- Require a classical probability comparator and independent geometric
  integration, along with the full-law bubble and interface failure controls.
