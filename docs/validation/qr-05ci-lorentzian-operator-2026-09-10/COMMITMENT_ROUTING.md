explicit: take a **local conservative kernel** and read the continuum operator's
coefficients **from discrete-kernel moments**. That is exactly the method CH
already used successfully for the diffusion (Laplace–Beltrami) case — it should
be extended to the **sign-indefinite / wave-like** case, not replaced by the BD
order-layer construction, which is a *causal-set field-theory tool*.

So:

- CI's operator is correctly implemented (its two forms agree exactly) and its
  numerical non-convergence stands, but it is an **imported correspondence**;
  its failure does not reflect on DET and its result should not be presented as
  a DET-native Lorentzian-operator benchmark.
- The DET-committed successor is a **T5 wave-kernel continuum benchmark**:
  a local conservative (sign-indefinite) kernel whose continuum limit is
  wave-like, with coefficients from kernel moments, verified on supplied
  geometry by the same two-route discipline as CH.
