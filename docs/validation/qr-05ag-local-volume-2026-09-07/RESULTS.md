# QR-05AG: supplied local-volume annotations

7 September 2026. Bounded geometry diagnostic after pushed AF commit
`39921dff4fc9e9bbc02817659473c87bda1bb430`.
The [prospective protocol](README.md) fixes the contract and all eighteen cases
before fixed AG calculations. The gate is complete; publication verification
and claim boundaries are recorded below.

## Main result

Supplied positive cell-volume marks make the declared finite volume sum
estimable from retained induced-order records. Inverse individual-inclusion
weighting recovers every finite target in expectation across the eighteen
cases, including singleton observation. Both independent engines match the
literal packet oracle on all 1,152 rows, including structural zeros.

This is useful additional measure information, not geometry derived from
order. Correct marks reproduce continuum volumes on the aligned intervals of
this supplied partition. On unaligned intervals, the finite representative
sum still differs from the clipped geometric volume. Stale but numerically
valid marks remain accepted and produce estimates of the stale finite target,
which can be wrong if interpreted geometrically. The interface cannot
authenticate annotation truth.

## What the interface knows

The public input is one retained induced order with fixed probe IDs, a full
eligible-ID selection law and marks for retained cells only. There are no
coordinates, cell boundaries, missing marks, source targets or covariances in
that input. Four fixed probe markers carry no volume, including markers lying
inside another probe. Marks are neither renormalized after selection nor
inferred for absent cells.

The output gives observed marked membership and raw, uniform-rate and
individual-inclusion volume estimates for each probe. The complete source-aware
audit separately computes expectation, covariance and geometric comparison.
The selection law is not the complete observation law without source order and
marks. The inverse-inclusion identity is established sampling mathematics,
not a new DET physical postulate.

The five sources are a six-cell 3-by-2 flat partition, its active coordinatewise
square warp, the same warp carrying stale base marks, a boost and a dilation.
The six designs are identity, independent half retention, independent unequal
retention, all-or-none, fixed-size-two and singleton. All six designs run on
base and correctly marked warp; the other sources use identity and half
retention. The identities and selection of representatives and probe markers
remain fixed while their coordinates are transformed: warped representatives
are not recentered in their new cells.

## Correct local marks add information

The whole supplied geometric volume stays 1/2 under the active warp. Its local
aligned volumes and finite marked targets change:

| Geometry | Whole | Lower aligned | Upper aligned |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Base, correct marks | 1/2 | 1/6 | 1/12 |
| Warp, correct marks | 1/2 | 1/18 | 5/24 |
| Warp, stale-mark finite targets | 1/2 | 1/6 | 1/12 |

For every matching base/warp design, erasing the mark field leaves identical
complete public packets across all mask rows. With correct marks retained,
the complete positive-probability public laws differ in every one of the six
designs. Erasure is an information projection, not a second accepted API schema.
The new information comes from supplied measure annotations, not from a new
order statistic.

The lower and upper aligned intervals do not partition the whole rectangle:
spatially incomparable regions remain outside them. Their volumes are not
expected to sum to the whole volume.

## Annotation truth is not self-authenticating

Base geometry with correct marks and warped geometry with stale base marks
produce identical full packets and analyses under identity and half retention.
Their true geometric finite targets nevertheless differ. The stale annotation
errors are +1/9 below the aligned marker and -1/8 above it, while the whole
error is zero. Checking only the total volume would miss this problem.

Under the declared fixed-mark observation model, the corrected estimator is
still unbiased for the stale supplied target. It is not thereby unbiased for
the true geometric target. This is a calibration/access boundary, not a theorem
that true local-volume annotations can never identify any geometric property.

## Boundary error remains separate

Membership means that a cell's representative lies strictly inside the probe.
It does not mean the cell lies wholly inside, and the observer cannot use
private cell-intersection fractions. Exact clipping is retained only as an
independent geometric comparison.

| Model and unaligned interval | Finite geometric sum | Continuum volume | Signed quadrature error |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Base lower | 1/6 | 3/25 | 7/150 |
| Base upper | 1/12 | 3/25 | -11/300 |
| Warp lower | 1/18 | 18/625 | 301/11250 |
| Warp upper | 5/24 | 168/625 | -907/15000 |

All whole/aligned finite geometric sums equal their continuum comparisons.
For every interval in every source, summing all clipped cell overlaps also
recovers its continuum volume exactly. That does not turn the observer's
representative-based target into the clipped target.

All methods retain the exact signed decomposition

    mean - continuum
      = sampling bias + annotation error + quadrature/boundary error.

For the stale warp under correct observation weighting, sampling bias is zero,
but annotation and quadrature errors both remain. Lower/upper unaligned total
errors are 517/3750 and -1391/7500. They are not experimental noise, variance
or evidence for different physical laws.

## Linear support and covariance

Positive singleton inclusion suffices for these linear marked sums.
Singleton selection has zero inclusion for distinct cell pairs, but no mean
term is unsupported. AF's missing higher-chain support is preserved in its
original evidence; it is not misapplied to this different target.

The same observation law drives every probe. Corrected whole-volume variance
and covariance between the disjoint aligned intervals are:

| Source/design | Whole variance | Lower/upper covariance |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Base, independent half | 1/24 | 0 |
| Base, all-or-none | 1/4 | 1/72 |
| Base, fixed-size-two | 0 | -1/180 |
| Base, singleton | 0 | -1/72 |
| Warp, independent half | 175/2592 | 0 |
| Warp, all-or-none | 1/4 | 5/432 |
| Warp, fixed-size-two | 67/1080 | -1/216 |
| Warp, singleton | 67/432 | -5/432 |

Zero whole variance for base fixed-size/singleton sampling is a consequence of
equal marks and fixed sample size, not general single-record volume recovery.
Unequal warped marks remove that coincidence. Negative cross-probe entries
are retained; covariance positivity is a matrix property, not an entrywise rule.

The centered sample covariance, second-moment route and weighted ordered-cell
inclusion formula agree exactly. The source formula includes diagonal/shared
cells and 228 zero-joint-inclusion pair occurrences across the suite. These
are source-aware design quantities, not error bars returned by one packet.

Unequal retention also exposes a wrong uniform-rate correction. On the base,
its whole bias is zero while each upper local bias is +1/36. On the correctly
marked warp, its whole bias is +5/108 and lower/upper local biases are +1/108
and +5/72. Individual-inclusion weighting removes these sampling biases without
altering the annotation or boundary terms.

## Transformation checks and applications

Boosted populations and complete packet/moment data agree with the base.
Correct dilation multiplies all volume estimates, targets and signed errors
by four, and all covariances by sixteen. Cell intersections scale with the
same factor. These exact finite checks are not a Lorentz-invariant ensemble
or a continuum-convergence theorem.

The operational value is a precise interface for estimating a declared weighted
region sum from incomplete records, with explicit assumptions about selection
and annotation provenance. A future application could use externally calibrated
cell volumes or exposure weights, but this gate includes no apparatus data,
sensor validation or RET integration. It supplies neither a quantum channel
nor gravitational dynamics.

## Next question: refinement and boundary-error certificates

Proposed QR-05AH: test a small, prespecified nested partition family with
additive supplied geometric marks and explicit representative rules. Compare
aligned additivity, unaligned interval sums, and source-aware inner/outer
cell-volume bounds with an explicit boundary-cell gap. Separate true geometric
marks from stale
annotations, and sampling bias from deterministic approximation.

A finer partition must not be assumed to improve every individual signed
error monotonically. Declare the refined observation access and selection law;
do not pass hidden cell bounds or overlaps into a supposedly order-only API.
The goal is a bounded refinement/error certificate, not unknown-metric recovery
or a claimed continuum limit. Freeze a detailed protocol before any AH
calculation. No AH computations have been performed; RET/Lean remain separate.
