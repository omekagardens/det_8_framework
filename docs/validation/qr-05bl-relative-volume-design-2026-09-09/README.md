# QR-05BL: relative-volume identification design

9 September 2026 (Pacific/Honolulu). **Design and analytical review only.**
No numerical study, simulation, unit-test suite or apparatus experiment is
executed here. The fractions below follow from the displayed integration
identities; they are not a numerical-results capture. No DET ontology,

## 1. Selected question and what is new

Can the accessible law of a short sequence of causal-membership records
identify **one relative spacetime volume**, provided the point-sampling law
is specified independently?

The target is a property of an unknown member of a declared geometric family,
not a response integral whose geometry is already supplied. This moves one
limited quantity from the known-input side to the inference side. It does
not reconstruct an arbitrary metric, discover the sampling measure from
counts, or infer gravity dynamics.

The positive result will be conditional: sampling uniformly in proper volume
makes membership probability equal relative volume by definition of that
sampling law. That premise carries geometric information into the records.
Calling the records quantum or relational does not supply the premise.

The negative controls ask whether changed sampling density can hide changed
relative volume, and whether normalized records can reveal absolute scale.
Keep both failures in the same model sheet as the positive correspondence.

## 2. Geometric family and marked query

Use auxiliary null coordinates u=t+x, v=t−x, signature (+,−), and a fixed
coordinate/unit convention. The chart describes candidate models to the
producer and mathematical verifier; individual sampled coordinates are not
observer data. No clock is measured or coupled to a field.

For η∈{0,1}, define the smooth positive metric family

```text
gη: ds²=(1+ηuv) du dv,
dμη=(1+ηuv) du dv/2.
```

In (u,v) coordinates the metric matrix has off-diagonal entries
(1+ηuv)/2 and zero diagonal entries, giving the displayed volume element.
The positive conformal factor does not change the future-cone condition
du≥0, dv≥0. All candidates therefore have the same strict timelike product
order: z≺z' when u<u' and v<v'. Null/boundary conventions have zero
probability under the smooth densities used here.

Fix the same three operationally identified probe events in every candidate:

```text
o=(0,0), m=(1/2,1/2), t=(1,1),
Q=I(o,t)=(0,1)²,
I=I(o,m)=(0,1/2)².
```

Use closures for integration if convenient; the integrals are unchanged.
The fixed probes are not random members of the sampling quota.
The label m does NOT assert a proper-time midpoint, a half-volume point,
or an observed chart coordinate. It identifies the same marked event across
the candidate worlds. Supplying those marks and the ability to ask a causal
comparison are explicit interface premises, not accomplishments of the study.

For a rectangle R=(a,b)×(c,d), direct integration gives

```text
Vη(R) = (b−a)(d−c)/2
        + η(b²−a²)(d²−c²)/8.
```

In particular,

```text
Vη(Q)=(4+η)/8,
Vη(I)=(16+η)/128,
τη=Vη(I)/Vη(Q)=(16+η)/[16(4+η)].

τ0=1/4,        τ1=17/80,        τ0−τ1=3/80.
```

The quantities called volumes are two-dimensional spacetime measures, not
spatial three-volumes. The target τ is dimensionless.

These are active alternatives for the metric with the marked query fixed,
not two passive coordinate descriptions of the same marked geometry.
A diffeomorphism that transports metric AND marked regions preserves their
volume ratio; the two unequal ratios therefore distinguish these marked
geometries. No curvature equation or gravitational field equation is assumed.

## 3. Sampling mechanism and observer access

For a world with geometric measure μ and positive sampling density ρ
*relative to μ*, define the normalized point law

```text
ν(A)=∫A ρ dμ / ∫Q ρ dμ,     A⊆Q.
```

The main positive family fixes ρ=1. Its actual η is fixed, hidden and common
to the whole run. Draw N=4 fresh independent points X1,...,X4 from ν.
N is a quota fixed externally, not a random count generated by a Poisson
process. The sample law is normalized on Q, so every attempted sample lies
inside Q in the main no-loss model.

Register only

```text
Yj = 1{Xj≺m} = 1{Xj∈I},     j=1,...,4.
```

The equivalence uses the guaranteed Xj∈Q and the fixed lower probe o.
The predicate is a declared ideal causal-comparison channel, not a lookup
that hands the observer a hidden coordinate or volume value. Implementing
and calibrating such a physical channel remains an apparatus problem.

The basic observer packet is the ordered word y=(y1,...,y4), with common
opaque run/attempt identifiers and the declared query/protocol identifiers.
The public model menu is allowed to contain both candidate metrics, their
predicted laws and predicted target values; that is model knowledge, not
disclosure of the actual hidden world.

| Information | Public or available? |
|---|---|
| Declared hypothesis menu, query labels o,m,t, quota four, channel specification | Yes, fixed across actual worlds |
| Four attempt-indexed membership bits | Yes |
| Optional inert Z outcomes and physical qubits in their normalized conditional state, defined below | Yes only in the explicitly named CQ variant |
| Actual world label, realized η, density/scale label when treated as unknown | No |
| Sample coordinates, true volumes/target, generator seeds or geometry-coded IDs | No |
| Inter-sample causal relations, reverse comparison m≺X, distinction between future and spacelike parts of Y=0 | No |
| Arrival times, signal delays, proper lengths, clock readings, other localization metadata | No |

Only the requested one-way membership predicate is returned. The complement
Y=0 aggregates different causal relations to m. If richer comparisons or
timings are available, their complete law must be modeled separately.

A common continuum causal order does NOT imply equal laws for randomly
sampled finite orders under different volume measures. The present law is
complete precisely for the restricted packet above. The stronger
density-compensation control below does preserve the entire latent point law.

Physical record collection occurs where the relevant reports can lawfully
be combined. Logical attempt order is not spacetime temporal order. Fresh
independent draws are an assumption about acquisition, not repeated use of
one spacetime event or a consequence of causal labels.

The sampler is postulated as a forward model, not implemented by this design.
Unknown geometry does not make the conditional experiment logically circular:
a generative law may depend on an unknown parameter. It DOES mean a later
apparatus must justify proper-volume uniformity independently. An algorithm
that reads the hidden target and writes it into a control or observer label
would not establish this bridge.

## 4. Complete record law and exact scope of identification

Let q=ν(I). Independence gives every ordered word y∈{0,1}^4 probability

```text
P(y)=q^k(1−q)^(4−k),     k=y1+y2+y3+y4.
```

For the uniform proper-volume family, q=τη. The histogram probability is
C(4,k)q^k(1−q)^(4−k); its multiplicity must not be attached to an individual
ordered word. All sixteen words belong to the prospective domain.

For any declared world menu W, the target is identifiable at the
population-law level exactly when

```text
P_w = P_w' on the entire accessible record space  ⇒  τ_w=τ_w'.
```

The positive pair {η=0,η=1,ρ=1} satisfies this criterion because its
one-record marginals differ by 3/80. This distinguishes the two stipulated
models at law level; it is not reconstruction of a general conformal factor.

The sample average obeys, under the same iid law,

```text
τhat=(Y1+Y2+Y3+Y4)/4,
E[τhat]=q,
Var(τhat)=q(1−q)/4.
```

It is unbiased for τ only when q=τ is justified. Four is a small exact-
enumeration quota, not a claim of useful experimental precision or power.

Both q values lie strictly between zero and one. Every finite ordered
membership word has positive probability in both positive hypotheses.
Consequently no rule can select the correct target with probability one
under both hypotheses from one four-record word. More generally, any fixed
finite quota retains common positive support. Distinct population laws,
unbiasedness and finite-shot certainty are different statements.

For this fixed iid Bernoulli experiment, k is sufficient for q: conditional
on k, all words are equally likely independently of q. Discarding attempt
order does not lose information about this target under the stated law.
Attempt labels are still needed to audit quotas, missing records and
dependencies. Do not transfer another experiment's histogram-loss claim
to this one.

No prior probabilities on worlds, posterior selection, confidence coverage,
thresholds, power target or fitted noise model are included.

## 5. Inert quantum extension: compatibility without added information

The geometry-identifying channel here is classical membership. To keep its
connection to the quantum-record calculus explicit without inventing a
geometry coupling, attach four fresh independent qubits in the same state
τq=I2/2, independent of all points, bits and world labels.

Before optional readout the complete classical–quantum state is

```text
Ω_w = Σ_y P_w(y) |y⟩⟨y| ⊗ τq^⊗4.
```

There are no hidden world-dependent quantum correlations. Equality of the
whole conditional payload state is required, not merely equal one-qubit
marginals. Ω_w describes the ensemble mathematically; a numerical matrix
selected by the actual hidden world is not an additional observer field.

For the explicitly named Z-record variant, set Z=diag(1,−1),
Πx=(I2+xZ)/2 for x∈{−1,+1}, and Jx(ρ)=ΠxρΠx.
For x=(x1,...,x4),

```text
Jx(τq^⊗4)=2^−4 Πx1⊗...⊗Πx4,
P_w(y,x)=2^−4 P_w(y).
```

The verifier retains the unnormalized branch P_w(y)Jx(τq^⊗4) for every
candidate world and both y and x labels. These candidate-indexed mathematical
predictions may be public model knowledge. Never supply an actual-world-
selected numerical branch matrix or P_w(y) as observer data: its trace would
disclose a hidden probability, not the outcome of one physical trial.
The observer receives (y,x) and, if retained, the physical conditional qubits;
their normalized post-readout state is Πx1⊗...⊗Πx4, identical across worlds.

There are sixteen membership words and sixteen Z words, giving 256 declared
joint branches per world. These are future coverage counts, not executed
tests. The ordinary projective instrument and classical fair-bit comparator
must agree on probabilities and branch traces.

Because τq^⊗4 is fixed and independent, any allowed world-independent
measurement/processing of the CQ packet is a common channel applied to y
plus an independent resource. Reading y already supplies all world information
present in this experiment. Adding the payload cannot split equal-law worlds
or improve statistical information over access to the complete y experiment.
No quantum sensing advantage is claimed. A world-dependent preparation,
correlation, phase, clock or interaction would be a different model.

The standard measurement notation is adopted, not derived from the geometric
measure or a DET ontology. This sheet introduces no quantum propagation law,
stress-energy source or gravitational feedback.

## 6. Fixed density and scale controls

Use exactly these five candidate worlds for the first proposed executable
study. Names are private verifier labels, never packet fields. The main
positive menu is {A,B}; the expanded nuisance menu is {A,B,C,D,E}.
Each world is fixed for the entire run.

| World | Metric | Sampling weight ρ relative to proper volume | V(Q) | V(I) | Geometric target τ | Membership probability q |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | g0 | 1 | 1/2 | 1/8 | 1/4 | 1/4 |
| B | g1 | 1 | 5/8 | 17/128 | 17/80 | 17/80 |
| C | g0 | 1+uv | 1/2 | 1/8 | 1/4 | 17/80 |
| D | 4g0 | 1 | 2 | 1/2 | 1/4 | 1/4 |
| E | 4g1 | 1 | 5/2 | 17/32 | 17/80 | 17/80 |

### Density compensation

In B and C the unnormalized point measures agree pointwise:

```text
ρB dμB = (1+uv) du dv/2 = ρC dμC.
```

Their normalizers agree too. This is equality of the entire latent point
law, not just one moment. It persists for every fixed number of iid draws
and every common observation channel of those points. Even a richer sampled-
order record, if later introduced, cannot distinguish B and C under these
equal point laws. An identical independent quantum extension cannot help.

Nevertheless τB=17/80 and τC=1/4. No statistic of these accessible records
can have expectation equal to the geometric target in both worlds.
The sample mean still estimates q correctly in C; q is then a density-
weighted membership probability, not relative geometric volume.

The compensation weight lies between one and two. It is an explicit fixed
nuisance alternative, not permission to fit a density after a disagreement
and call the model confirmed. Independent calibration could exclude C;
the same observations cannot do so when the expanded menu allows it.

### Absolute-scale ambiguity

In two spacetime dimensions, scaling the metric tensor g to s g scales
proper volume by s and proper lengths by √s. D and E therefore use volume
scale four, not length scale four. The same marked events are retained.

For any fixed density weight ρ,

```text
∫A ρ dμ_(s g) / ∫Q ρ dμ_(s g)
 = ∫A ρ dμ_g / ∫Q ρ dμ_g.
```

All normalized point and record laws, and the target ratio, are unchanged.
Absolute volumes change. Thus target identification can coexist with metric-
scale ambiguity; it is not full metric recovery.

An unconditioned Poisson-count experiment would be different: with an
independently known uniform intensity κ, mean count κV(Q) could carry
absolute-volume information. Unknown κ permits compensating scale changes.
The present fixed quota supplies neither that count law nor calibrated κ.

### Complete-law classes and target sets

The five-world membership/CQ law classes are exactly

```text
{A,D}:     q=1/4,     target set {1/4};
{B,C,E}:   q=17/80,   target set {17/80,1/4}.
```

Preserve all world labels even when target values coincide. These classes
refer to equality of complete laws, not equality of a realized count k.
The positive menu identifies its target at law level. In the expanded menu,
the second class is target-ambiguous because of density compensation;
the first has an identified target and an unknown metric scale.

Every finite membership/Z word has positive probability in every world.
Support-only compatibility with one four-record packet therefore retains
all five candidates. Being closer to a predicted fraction is not certain
identification. A later decision protocol may compare likelihoods; this
design invents neither priors nor thresholds.

## 7. Acquisition and model boundaries

The first executable scope is the no-loss five-world experiment above.
The ideal packet has four distinct expected attempt IDs, one membership
bit per attempt and correct common query/protocol metadata. Its CQ variant
also has one valid Z outcome per corresponding fresh qubit.

Refuse missing or duplicate attempts, invalid outcomes, mixed queries and
undeclared extra observer fields. An actual nondetection needs a future
expanded observation law; it is not an outside-I bit or an omitted row.
Interface refusal does not diagnose an apparatus fault, refute a geometric
hypothesis or validate a physical detector's loss behavior.

Three analytical boundaries guide later acquisition work:

- Independent constant positive retention preserves the conditional
  membership law, but the retained quota becomes random and an empty
  retained packet has no sample average. Conditioning and normalization
  require their own declared law.
- If inside/outside keep probabilities are r1,r0, the retained-only success
  probability is r1 q/[r1 q+r0(1−q)] when defined, not generally q.
  That conditional number is not the complete attempted-record law.
  Retention flags may add information and must not be silently discarded.
- A common all-or-none retention coin and independent coins may share
  marginal keep rates while giving different complete packet laws.
  Zero retention for all attempts produces the same all-missing packet in
  every world and no target information; it is neither an observed zero
  membership count nor a division by zero.

These are analytical scope boundaries, not additional BM fixture rows or a
validated correction scheme. Correlated/nonuniform original sampling,
imperfect causal comparisons, localization errors, drifting marks, preparation
correlations and metadata leaks also require different forward models.
They are conventional hypotheses to specify and independently constrain,
not automatic exemptions from matching observations.
