## Applicable value and boundary

Within the certified domain, a trusted H class can replace the full observed
order when predicting the next H distribution under the specified thinning.
The repeated-observation law remains composable. This broadens the supplied
model family on which the chain-pair and motif summaries are useful; it does
not demonstrate a storage/runtime gain or establish a production SDK.

The existing P consumer is still pinned to its original domain/evidence.
Q does not silently authorize it for arbitrary new inputs, nor promote
matching summary values to a domain-membership test. No RET integration,
named-ID prediction, ontology, manifold, metric, physical observation or
gravitational dynamics is certified here.

## Proposed next gate: QR-05R

Keep Q's domain, marks, H and law unchanged. Develop a nonnegative
combinatorial interface using
A_s(h,o,e)=the number of induced eligible subsets with summary h and
retained odd/even sizes(o,e). These counts multiply the *unnormalized*
Bernstein monomials x^o(1-x)^(O-o)y^e(1-y)^(E-e).

First show H fixes the parent color sizes(O,E). Then prove and certify
that constancy of these profiles across each H fiber is equivalent to full
polynomial closure, using the finite tensor-basis degree bounds.
Verify the direct counts, coefficient conversion, normalization
sum_h A_s(h,o,e)=choose(O,o)choose(E,e), and the combinatorial composition
identity obtained by counting intermediate supersets of each final subset.

The benefit is an interpretable nonnegative interface and a structural
criterion for closure, not a claim that the current exact coefficient
arithmetic is inaccurate or that H is sufficient for every order. Expanded
minimality, new domains, new observables and physical dynamics remain outside
that proposed gate.
