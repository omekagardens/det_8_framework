# GDM008: a position-only interaction-energy allocation obstruction

Accepted conditional mathematics; see [adoption and candidate decision](ADOPTION.md)
and [independent review](REVIEW.md). This tests a specified algebraic mapping,
not physical relativity or a native law.

## Complete premises and question

Let e,c,ell>0 and canonical coordinates X1,X2,P1,P2 have bracket
{F,G}=sum_i[(partial_Xi F)(partial_Pi G)-(partial_Pi F)(partial_Xi G)].
Set d=4ell, k=e/ell^2, q=X2-X1-d, R=(X1+X2)/2,
K_i=sqrt(e^2+c^2 P_i^2), U(q)=kq^2/2, H=K1+K2+U, and P=P1+P2.
These are the supplied GDM007 dynamics, not a derived physical model.

For any real C1 function alpha(q) on (-ell,ell), define

B_alpha=[X1 K1+X2 K2+U(q)(alpha(q)X1+(1-alpha(q))X2)]/c^2.

Alpha can depend on the fixed parameters e,c,ell, but not on momenta, R,
time or history. It need not lie between zero and one. No additional
interaction-dependent boost term or field/stress degree of freedom is included.

Require both {B_alpha,P}=H/c^2 and {B_alpha,H}=P at every phase point with
|q|<ell, arbitrary R and arbitrary finite P1,P2. These identities are supplied
correspondence requirements, not a full spacetime/worldline representation.
The phase-space question is distinct from GDM007's four prepared trajectories.

**Theorem:** no member of this family satisfies both identities throughout
that domain. The first holds identically; the second has a common nonzero
witness independent of alpha.

## Complete residual and units


Retain the supplied constants, canonical bracket and Hamiltonian:

q=X2−X1−d, d=4ell, k=e/ell², U=kq²/2,
K_i=sqrt(e²+c²P_i²), H=K1+K2+U, P=P1+P2.

Write s=X2−X1=q+d and define

A=alpha X1+(1−alpha)X2=X2−alpha s,
beta=alpha+s alpha',
v_i=c²P_i/K_i.

Here alpha'=dalpha/dq and U'=dU/dq. Then A_X1=beta and A_X2=1−beta. For the prescribed B=(X1 K1+X2 K2+UA)/c², all derivatives entering the brackets are

c² B_X1=K1−U'A+U beta,
c² B_X2=K2+U'A+U(1−beta),
c² B_Pi=X_i v_i,

H_X1=−U', H_X2=U', H_Pi=v_i.

Thus, with exactly the stipulated bracket sign,

{B,P}−H/c²=0,

and

{B,H}−P=(C1 v1+C2 v2)/c²,

where the complete coefficients are

C1=U(alpha+s alpha')−sU'(1−alpha),
C2=U(1−alpha−s alpha')−sU'alpha.

The free terms reduce to P because K_i v_i=c²P_i. The interaction terms use X1−A=−(1−alpha)s and A−X2=−alpha s; hence the displayed residual includes both force terms and every alpha derivative.

B has mass-times-length units. The two brackets have respectively mass and momentum units, matching H/c² and P. Alpha is dimensionless, alpha' has inverse-length units, C_i has energy units, and C_i v_i/c² has momentum units. The identities are supplied algebraic requirements, not a derived physical boost interpretation.

## Universal-family obstruction and one common witness

At any fixed q,R, the finite momenta vary independently, and each v_i ranges over (−c,c). A phase-space identity therefore requires C1=C2=0. But their sum is independent of alpha:

C1+C2=U−sU'
       =−kq(q/2+d).

For the admitted d=4ell and |q|<ell this vanishes only at q=0; the other root −2d lies outside the domain. Consequently no allocation can make both coefficients vanish throughout the required open domain.

More directly, the following single phase point defeats every allowed alpha:

q=ell/2, R=0,
X1=−9ell/4, X2=9ell/4,
P1=P2=e/c.

All coordinates and momenta are finite, |q|<ell, and v1=v2=c/sqrt(2). Here

U=e/8, sU'=9e/4,

so

{B_alpha,H}−P=−17e/[8 sqrt(2)c] != 0.

The witness requires no selected value of alpha or alpha'; their contributions cancel. It is a point in the newly declared algebraic domain, not an extra GDM007 trajectory, fitted preparation or change of its response protocol.

## Control and accidental equalities

For the identically absent potential, U=U'=0 everywhere. B reduces to the free expression (X1K1+X2K2)/c², independently of alpha, and both identities hold for all admitted phase points.

For the interacting potential at q=0, both U and U' vanish, so both identities also hold at that separation for every finite momentum pair. This isolated separation does not establish the required open-domain identity. At P1=P2=0, the second residual vanishes for every q because both velocities vanish. Other selected momenta may cancel a particular residual, but cannot replace independent variation of both momenta. The first identity holds throughout even where the second fails.
