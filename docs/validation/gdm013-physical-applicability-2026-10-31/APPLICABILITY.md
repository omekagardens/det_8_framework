# GDM013 — the toy discriminator has no established physical gravity map

**Coordinator-adopted scoped finding after independent review:** reject the present interpretation of GDM012 as a physical comparison against conventional gravity; retain its accepted exact two-family result. This is a missing-correspondence decision, not a disproof of gravity, DET, or every possible completion of the toy dynamics.

## Retained mathematical result

The accepted models use H0=K(P1)+K(P2)+V(q), K(P)=sqrt(e²+c²P²), V=e q²/(2ell²), q=X2−X1−4ell and arithmetic center R=(X1+X2)/2:

H_I=H0−2FR−gR[2mu_I+beta V/c²],
H_E=H0−2FR−gR[2mu_E+alpha(H0−2e)/c²].

The equilibrium/outward preparations have q=R=0 and respectively zero or opposite momenta of magnitude e/(10c). With h=ell/(4c), S=partial_F R|0, T=partial_g R|0 and Z=T/S, GDM012 proves

Z_I=(mu_I,mu_I+beta w(h/2),mu_I+beta w(h)),
Z_E=(mu_E,mu_E+alpha epsilon,mu_E+alpha epsilon),

where 0<w(h/2)<w(h) and epsilon>0. The entries are equilibrium at h and outward at h/2,h. At the original equilibrium/outward h readouts, the families can match all responses; the triple intersects exactly at alpha=beta=0 with equal mu. No term in that theorem identifies either family with gravity.

## Why the tempting identification is insufficient

As a bookkeeping test only, choosing alpha=1, mu_E=e/c² and phi(R)=−gR turns Family E's field term into phi H0/c². The substitutions are additional physical identifications, not consequences of GDM011/012. In particular, a label involving total energy does not establish that the measured coordinate, inertia, internal energy and external drive agree with those in a physical composite model.

Nor does a list of extra Hamiltonian terms alone prove failure of a particular linear response. With the stipulated classical zero-total-momentum baseline and phi=−gR, the P⁴, phi P² or phi² terms can disappear from this first-variation problem. This does not remove the P² H_A kinetic coupling or field dependence of internal terms. Each omission needs a response-level order check. Equality of baseline coordinate values also does not remove derivatives of the coordinate transformation. Thus neither blanket omission nor blanket retention supplies the missing calculation.

The accepted toy kinetic energy is exact while its interaction and field coupling are stipulated. No single controlled approximation maps all three to a physical action. A formal comparison must specify how e/c², momenta, binding energy and field strength scale, including the fixed protocol P_i=±e/(10c), h=ell/(4c). It must transform the dynamics, preparations and measured observable at the same order. A Hamiltonian-level resemblance is insufficient.

For the claimed discrimination, the necessary error control is on the normalized response difference relative to |beta[w(h)−w(h/2)]|. Its exact nonzero value supplies no lower bound against neglected terms or finite measurement uncertainty, especially as beta approaches zero. No such response-error bound has been established.

The local inputs also supply no physical implementation of the common constituent drive −2FR. Turning off an external interaction does not reproduce a calibrated derivative with respect to that interaction. A laboratory correspondence must state which control realizes F and which realizes g, what is held fixed when differentiating, and how the two symmetric classical preparations represent physical states. Separate trials avoid an intermediate-measurement assumption but do not establish preparation or readout calibration. Internal V→V+C covariance does not specify the external gravitational potential reference or laboratory time.

GDM008 removes one previously tested shortcut: its position-only partition of potential energy cannot satisfy both stipulated boost brackets throughout the stated domain. That obstruction concerns exactly its allocation family. It neither establishes a universal no-go nor supplies a successful momentum/field-dependent completion.

## Decision and minimal reopening premise

**Stop the present physical-gravity interpretation. Continue to retain the exact toy theorem.** This does not choose between conventional gravity and DET, and does not predict that a completed conventional calculation will pass or fail either toy membership equation.

The minimal missing premise is a justified map from one specified physical composite system, its states and laboratory controls/readout to the GDM variables, at one declared approximation order, with errors controlled for the actual response derivatives. Establishing that map would decide whether the three-readout test survives as a physical candidate or is a coordinate/model artifact. A DET-native claim would additionally need that model and map derived from stated DET premises; these papers do not provide that derivation.

Recommend further physical work only after the coordinator selects a bounded question that establishes or rejects that premise. No new coupling family, coefficient extension, readout time, simulation or experiment is selected here.
