## Selected bridge

Earlier BP accepted externally supplied population-probability boxes and
computed exact compatible geometric targets. BQ now states one way for
finite paired records to construct such bounds, with honest statistical
provenance and explicit coverage assumptions. It does not relabel a sample
frequency as an exact population probability.

Fix the quota, four one-sided error allocations and a rational grid before
acquisition. Each marginal count has a binomial law under fresh iid paired
sampling. Inclusive-tail comparisons give closed endpoints rounded outward
to the grid. A threshold-in-count proof bounds each tail failure for every
continuous population probability; a four-event union bound gives joint
coverage without assuming that the two questions are independent.

The declared geometric family has affine region masses in the continuous
density parameter δ. Its inverse intersects both probability constraints
using one shared δ per geometry. If the true world and density satisfy the
declared premises, a covering population box includes that world's parameter
and therefore its target. Thus the target set has coverage at least 1−α.
Scale ambiguity and the whole-point geometry/density collision remain.

The result is an analytical, model-relative finite-record uncertainty bridge.
It is not a claim that the actual sampling apparatus meets the model, that
the displayed intervals are shortest possible, or that their true coverage
is exactly the nominal level. No Bayesian prior or posterior is introduced.

## Analytical controls and what they mean

The selected successor baseline is n=4, total α=1/20, four equal tail
allocations 1/80 and grid {j/256}. These settings are fixed for correctness
verification, not fitted to produce useful precision or singleton answers.

| Analytical result | Consequence |
|---|---|
| All00 has probability at least 625/4096>1/20 in every declared world | A deterministic uniformly 95%-covering rule must retain both targets on that word; the proposed rule also retains every allowed density value |
| On all11, the first lower endpoint is at least 5/16, above the entire family's q₁≤1/4 | The proposed confidence inverse is empty on a possible packet; its probability is positive and at most 1/256 |
| A different rule reports only the conformal target on all11 and both targets otherwise | It has more than 95% coverage for every world, yet every singleton is wrong when the true geometry is flat |
| Flat δ=1 and conformal δ=0 have the same complete record law | When both satisfy the bound, the common-box construction reports both targets with probability at least 1−α, for any quota |

The selective-reporting counterexample is deliberately NOT the proposed
interval rule: the latter returns empty on all11. It shows why unconditional
coverage cannot be advertised as 95% correctness conditional on obtaining
a singleton. Under the true model, empty sets and wrong singleton reports
together have probability at most α. Neither empty nor ambiguous outputs
diagnose a particular instrument failure.

The whole-point bound 1−α uses one common population-box coverage event.
An arbitrary uniformly covering target-set procedure has only the general
same-law lower bound 1−2α on retaining both targets. Both conclusions require
a density premise admitting the two compensated parameter values. Absolute
scale remains unidentifiable from these normalized records at any quota.

## Review decisions and interface boundary

Three independent read-only analytical reviews checked the proof, failure
examples and successor design. The retained contract makes explicit:

- Inclusive tails, ≤ grid comparisons and closed endpoints, with strict
  noncoverage events and the k=0/n defaults. The unknown probability is
  continuous; no extra grid-size failure penalty is needed.
- Outward rounding, nonreversed intervals and the distinction between exact
  rational computation, conservative coverage and optimal interval width.
- The multinomial joint count law and same-point dependence. Validate every
  paired attempt first: an impossible10 can hide behind legal aggregate
  counts. Sufficient counts do not authenticate the physical record stream.
- A new record-derived confidence interface, not BP's external-population
  provenance tag. Fixed attempt IDs detect structural packet defects, not
  cloned events, independence violations, loss or dishonest acquisition.
- A true model and independently justified density bound. False-bound
  summaries remain visible but are not covered by the target theorem;
  optional stopping, favorable selection and adaptive assumptions need
  separate arguments.

The main dyadic-grid tail values cannot equal the nondyadic allocation 1/80.
Two separate private-helper regressions are therefore prespecified at budget
1/16: T⁺(4;1/2)=1/16 and T⁻(0;1/2)=1/16. They exercise equality retention
without changing the main confidence budget or case census. They have not
been executed here.
