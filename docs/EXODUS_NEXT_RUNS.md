# Exodus/DET8 phase-2 discriminator runs

**Status:** Synthetic experimental-design study. These runs do not validate an
Exodus force and do not add a DET8 force prediction.

This phase follows the equation and conservation audit in
`docs/EXODUS_DET_TRANSLATION.md`. The executable implementation is
`det8/models/exodus_next_runs.py`.

Run:

```bash
python3 -m det8.models.exodus_next_runs
python3 run_tests.py
```

The first command emits all phase-2 results as JSON. The second runs the full
260-test suite.

---

## 1. Questions tested

The runs target four discriminators:

1. Can a conventional grounded-boundary interaction reproduce a nonzero
   apparatus force while preserving global momentum?
2. Can a practical distance sweep distinguish a boundary-attached signal from
   a constant or null signal?
3. Can simultaneous force measurements classify internal stress, external
   exchange, and an endpoint-free momentum residual?
4. Which history and waveform protocols are informative enough to justify a
   real experiment?

All synthetic force observations use a fixed random seed. The default force
noise is \(5\ \mu\mathrm N\) per measured channel.

---

## 2. Conventional grounded-wall surrogate

The first run replaces the earlier phenomenological boundary curve with a
standard method-of-images calculation. An infinite grounded plane lies at
\(x=0\). A neutral point dipole has charges \(+q\) and \(-q\), separated by
\(1\ \mathrm{cm}\), at a variable distance from that plane.

Each real charge interacts with the induced plane charge represented by image
charges. The internal force between the two real charges cancels exactly:

\[
F_{+-}+F_{-+}=0.
\]

The device force due to the grounded plane is nonzero, but the wall carries
the opposite reaction:

\[
F_{\rm device}+F_{\rm wall}=0.
\]

The effective charge is calibrated to reproduce
\(237\ \mu\mathrm N\) at a center distance of \(10\ \mathrm{cm}\):

\[
q=2.6463\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm C.
\]

This charge and point-dipole geometry are a transparent surrogate, not a fit
to the Exodus article.

| Wall distance | Device-force magnitude | Wall reaction | Global residual |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(3\ \mathrm{cm}\) | \(30.541\ \mathrm{mN}\) | \(30.541\ \mathrm{mN}\) opposite | 0 |
| \(5\ \mathrm{cm}\) | \(3.840\ \mathrm{mN}\) | \(3.840\ \mathrm{mN}\) opposite | 0 |
| \(10\ \mathrm{cm}\) | \(237.000\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(237.000\ \mu\mathrm N\) opposite | 0 |
| \(20\ \mathrm{cm}\) | \(14.766\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(14.766\ \mu\mathrm N\) opposite | 0 |
| \(50\ \mathrm{cm}\) | \(0.378\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(0.378\ \mu\mathrm N\) opposite | 0 |

The steep distance dependence is the useful result, not these particular
numbers. A neutral source near a conductor can have a much steeper boundary
signature than an inverse-square point-force model. A wall sweep should
therefore fit several candidate transfer functions rather than merely compare
"near" and "far" positions.

## 3. Noisy boundary model selection

Synthetic observations were generated at wall distances

\[
8,\ 10,\ 12,\ 15,\ 20\ \mathrm{cm},
\]

with five repeats at each distance and independent
\(5\ \mu\mathrm N\) Gaussian noise. Four models were compared using AIC:

- zero force;
- a fitted constant force;
- a fitted inverse-square curve;
- the image-dipole boundary curve.

| Model | \(\Delta\mathrm{AIC}\) | Akaike weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| Image-dipole boundary | 0.00 | approximately 1.0 |
| Inverse square | 147.57 | \(9.0\times10^{-33}\) |
| Constant | 191.16 | \(3.1\times10^{-42}\) |
| Null | 205.70 | \(2.2\times10^{-45}\) |

Because the synthetic data were generated by the image model, recovering that
model validates the discriminator and fitting code; it is not empirical
evidence for that model. The experimental-design result is that five distances
and five repeats are ample at the assumed signal and noise levels.

A real pre-registration should include at least:

\[
F(d)=0,\qquad F(d)=F_0,\qquad F(d)\propto d^{-2},
\qquad F(d)\propto d^{-4},
\]

plus a field-solver-derived curve for the actual chamber and electrode CAD.

## 4. Closed momentum inventory

The next Monte Carlo run measures four channels simultaneously:

\[
F_{\rm patch},\quad
F_{\rm apparatus\ remainder},\quad
F_{\rm environment},\quad
\frac{dP_{\rm EM}}{dt}.
\]

The closure residual is

\[
R=F_{\rm patch}+F_{\rm apparatus\ remainder}
+F_{\rm environment}+\frac{dP_{\rm EM}}{dt}.
\]

Twenty-five samples were simulated for each channel at
\(5\ \mu\mathrm N\) noise.

| Scenario | Apparatus signal | Closure residual | Classification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Internal stress | \(1.66\sigma\) | \(0.15\sigma\) | No apparatus thrust; closed |
| External boundary | \(168.31\sigma\) | \(0.09\sigma\) | Apparatus thrust with measured reaction |
| No endpoint | \(168.28\sigma\) | \(120.80\sigma\) | Conservation failure; DET-rejected |

This is the strongest phase-2 finding. A force sensor on the apparatus alone
cannot distinguish the last two cases: both show the same apparatus signal.
The environment and field-momentum channels make them categorically
different.

The practical apparatus should therefore place independent force readouts on:

- the thruster assembly;
- the grounded enclosure;
- high-voltage feedthroughs and supply return;
- any shield attached to an external support.

The sensor sum, not a single torsion or pendulum reading, is the primary DET
observable.

## 5. History-sensitivity detectability

The declared history ansatz was swept over

\[
\lambda_H=0,\ 0.02,\ 0.05,\ 0.10,\ 0.20
\]

and dwell times

\[
0.2,\ 1,\ 5,\ 20\ \mathrm s.
\]

The calculation estimates how many repeats of each voltage path are required
for a \(5\sigma\) difference at \(5\ \mu\mathrm N\) force noise.

| Dwell | \(\lambda_H\) | Path difference | Repeats per path |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(0.2\ \mathrm s\) | 0.20 | \(2.278\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 241 |
| \(1\ \mathrm s\) | 0.05 | \(2.243\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 249 |
| \(1\ \mathrm s\) | 0.10 | \(4.486\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 63 |
| \(1\ \mathrm s\) | 0.20 | \(8.973\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 16 |
| \(5\ \mathrm s\) | 0.05 | \(3.515\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 102 |
| \(5\ \mathrm s\) | 0.10 | \(7.030\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 26 |
| \(5\ \mathrm s\) | 0.20 | \(14.059\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 7 |
| \(20\ \mathrm s\) | 0.20 | \(1.087\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 1,058 |

The non-monotonic dwell dependence is informative: very short dwells do not
build enough state separation, while very long final dwells erase it. Under
this ansatz, the useful region is around the relaxation timescale.

This remains an experimental-design calculation only. Dielectric absorption,
charge trapping, temperature, creep, corona conditioning, power-supply lag,
and sensor zero drift must be recorded as nuisance histories and fitted first.

## 6. AC phase and frequency grid

The exact derivative of patent equation (12) and the patent's prose average
were compared at phases

\[
0^\circ,15^\circ,30^\circ,45^\circ,60^\circ,75^\circ,90^\circ
\]

and frequencies \(100\ \mathrm{Hz}\), \(1\ \mathrm{kHz}\), and
\(10\ \mathrm{kHz}\).

At \(1\ \mathrm{kHz}\):

| Phase | Exact equation (13) | Patent prose | Difference |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(0^\circ\) | approximately 0 | \(59.25\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(-59.25\ \mu\mathrm N\) |
| \(30^\circ\) | \(59.25\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(88.875\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(-29.625\ \mu\mathrm N\) |
| \(45^\circ\) | \(118.50\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(118.50\ \mu\mathrm N\) | 0 |
| \(60^\circ\) | \(177.75\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(148.125\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(+29.625\ \mu\mathrm N\) |
| \(90^\circ\) | \(237.00\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(177.75\ \mu\mathrm N\) | \(+59.25\ \mu\mathrm N\) |

The averages are frequency-independent to numerical precision, so a frequency
sweep does not resolve the patent's mathematical ambiguity. A three-phase
test at \(0^\circ\), \(45^\circ\), and \(90^\circ\) is more discriminating.

The absolute-time defect also persists unchanged across all three
frequencies. Comparing identical field states one period apart changes the
equation-(13) force by

\[
1.48911\ \mathrm{mN}
\]

at every tested frequency. This confirms analytically and numerically that the
problem is the explicit \(t\) in \(P(t)=\epsilon_0tD(t)\), not the chosen
frequency.

## 7. Recommended next physical run

The simulations prioritize one experiment:

1. Fix voltage, waveform, electrode geometry, orientation, and thermal state.
2. Move an independently suspended grounded enclosure through at least five
   distances spanning roughly a factor of 2.5.
3. Record force simultaneously on the apparatus, enclosure, feedthrough, and
   shield/support system.
4. Reverse the entire apparatus inside the unchanged enclosure.
5. Fit null, constant, inverse-square, dipole-like, and CAD field-solver curves
   blind to run labels.
6. Require global momentum closure before interpreting the apparatus channel.

Only after that boundary inventory should the matched-history protocol be
added. It is less decisive and carries many more conventional nuisance
mechanisms.
