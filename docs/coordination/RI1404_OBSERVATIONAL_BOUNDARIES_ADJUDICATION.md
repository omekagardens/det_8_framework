## 1. QR: the complete four-query law has a classical realization

Let \(\mu\) be a Borel probability measure on \([0,1]\) with positive mass in \((0,1)\), and put \(p=\int\theta\,d\mu\), \(r=\int\theta^2\,d\mu\). The declared true predicates C1, C2, A2 and C3 have probabilities \((1,p,1-p,r)\). A stable shared memoryless detector has false-positive rate \(f\), true-positive rate \(t\), and \(s=t-f>0\), with \(0\le f<t\le1\). Query order and positive finite batch counts are fixed before observations; every trial uses a fresh independent preparation and independent detector noise. Only the reported bit and its prescribed query label are recorded.

For each preparation, an ordinary classical construction draws \(\Theta\sim\mu\) and independent uniforms \(U_1,U_2,V\). Set

\[
X_k=\mathbf1\{U_k<\Theta\},\qquad
(B_{C1},B_{C2},B_{A2},B_{C3})=(1,X_1,1-X_1,X_1X_2),
\qquad Y=\mathbf1\{V<f+sB_j\}.
\]

Only the selected predicate is observed on a preparation. The two coins inside C3 share the same latent bias, so their product mean is \(r\); independently redrawing that bias between the two coins would instead give \(p^2\). The entire tuple is redrawn between preparations. Conditional integration gives the reported probabilities

\[
q=(t,\ f+sp,\ f+s(1-p),\ f+sr).
\]

For an ordered finite record \(y=(y_{j,i})\), define \(b_q(1)=q\), \(b_q(0)=1-q\). Both descriptions have exactly

\[
\Pr(Y=y)=\prod_{j=1}^{4}\prod_{i=1}^{n_j}b_{q_j}(y_{j,i}).
\]

This handles endpoint probabilities without a zero-power convention. Fixed labels carry the same metadata. Every deterministic summary and every common randomized postprocessing kernel therefore has the same law. Total variation is zero. If a forced binary test declares the native description with probability \(g(y)\), its two opposite errors are \(\mathbb E g\) and \(1-\mathbb E g\): their sum is one and their equal-prior average is one half. Abstention adds no distinguishing observation. This is a probability-space construction, not a simulation or physically implemented apparatus.

The true complement occurs before detection. Flipping a previously observed C2 bit produces response rates \((1-t,1-f)\) relative to A2, generally different from \((f,t)\), and \(q_{A2}-(1-q_{C2})=f+t-1\). Even if \(f+t=1\), the flipped existing record remains tied to the original and is not a fresh trial.

The inherited compatibility region has \(0<p<1\) and \(p^2\le r<p\). Every admitted moment pair has witness

\[
\mu=(1-p^2/r)\delta_0+(p^2/r)\delta_{r/p}.
\]

Using that measure in the classical construction realizes every compatible reported tuple, so the declared observable families coincide. A sound finite confidence-box rejection still tests a genuine restriction on the conjunction of law, populations, true-predicate access, shared response and sampling. Rejection does not isolate the failed premise or reject all classical origins. Compatibility or nonrejection does not prefer a native origin over the matching classical member. The inherited finite-budget boundary-power limitation remains.

This equality applies to the fixed four-query records, not complete native histories, full orders, joint access to unused predicates, arbitrary adaptive interventions or all quantum observables. A native discriminator would require a derived outcome law for a specified operational preparation/readout, its nuisance allowances, and an independently justified rival class on those same records. A uniform claim needs separation from all remaining rivals. Merely choosing a unique \(\mu\) leaves the classical model with that same \(\mu,f,t\) available. No new native value, readout or restrictive rival premise is supplied here.

## 2. E: endpoint margins imply intersample safety only with a real rate bound

Consider one true physical interval \([a,b]\) of length \(L>0\). Assume the true temperature \(T_i\) is absolutely continuous and \(|T_i'|\le M_i\) almost everywhere throughout the interval. Independently valid endpoint readings satisfy \(|y_{i,j}-T_i(t_j)|\le\epsilon_{i,j}\), for \(j=a,b\). If both endpoints obey

\[
B_i^-+\epsilon_{i,j}+M_iL/2\le y_{i,j}
\le B_i^+-\epsilon_{i,j}-M_iL/2,
\]

then \(T_i(t)\in[B_i^-,B_i^+]\) throughout the interval. For any \(t\), its nearer endpoint is at most \(L/2\) away; integrating the derivative bound and adding that endpoint's error gives the conclusion. The upper inequality alone certifies a safety ceiling. A triangular Lipschitz excursion with equal endpoints can reach height \(ML/2\), so endpoint compliance without the margin is insufficient. Missing readings, an empty tightened band or an unbounded rate cannot qualify service.

For the dwell, both zones use \(B_i^\pm=T_i^*\pm0.5\,\mathrm K\). The target+1 K ceiling and all other safety constraints apply on every attempt interval, before entry and after dwell as well. Every joined interval in the claimed uninterrupted dwell must be certified. This implication supplies no physical rate, reset or source-cost certificate.

With timestamp error bounds \(\kappa_a,\kappa_b\), safety uses an upper gap bound

\[
L\le\widehat t_b-\widehat t_a+\kappa_a+\kappa_b.
\]

Dwell instead uses the lower elapsed-time bound \(t_b-t_a\ge\widehat t_b-\widehat t_a-\kappa_a-\kappa_b\). Nominal twenty 60-second intervals do not certify the full dwell with positive clock error unless the required slack is provided. Sensor, lag, synchronization and clock uncertainty need independently justified coverage; synthetic error amplitudes do not provide it.

The root clarification of the author's rate notation is essential. For a separately certified continuous thermal law with \(C_i>0\), nonnegative applicable coefficients and independently established whole-interval bounds \(|T_i-T_a|\le A_i\), \(|T_1-T_2|\le A_{12}\), \(|b|\le B_b\), \(|w_i|\le B_{wi}\), \(g_z\le G_{\max}\), \(0\le P_i\le P_{\max}\), define

\[
B_i^{\mathrm{rate}}=
\frac{\eta_iP_{\max}+h_iA_i+G_{\max}A_{12}+B_b+B_{wi}}{C_i}.
\]

The triangle inequality supports \(\operatorname*{ess\,sup}|T_i'|\le B_i^{\mathrm{rate}}\). Choose \(M_i=B_i^{\mathrm{rate}}\), a larger bound, or another independently proved derivative upper bound. The author's displayed \(M_i\le B_i^{\mathrm{rate}}\) must not license an arbitrary smaller \(M_i\). Numerator terms are watts and division by J/K gives K/s. No numerical physical bound is selected.

The sampled Euler family gives no continuous interpolation or apparatus derivative certificate. A reachable enclosure cannot be assumed from the same safety band that the rate argument is meant to establish. An independently justified enclosure or complete exit-time/barrier proof must cover all relevant disturbances and boundaries. A local estimate inside an unproved box is insufficient.

Prospectively, a controller may use only available causal information and independently certified enclosures covering every admissible future continuation. Future endpoint enclosures must lie wholly in the tightened set; the controller cannot use a future evaluator reading. Retrospective qualification may use independently observed endpoints together with an already valid rate and clock certificate. That distinction preserves equal causal channels for both arms and retains failures and missing packets.

With zero certified clock error as a conditional simplification, a 60-second interval needs margin \(\epsilon_i+30\,\mathrm s\,M_i\). The condition \(\epsilon_i+30\,\mathrm s\,M_i\le0.5\,\mathrm K\) only makes this particular tightened-band test nonempty; it is not physical feasibility. The unchanged 64-step cap and 20-step nominal dwell give conservative full-window entry by step 44. The official entry deadline remains step 60. Entry at 50 supplies only 14 covered intervals by 64; a rate bound cannot create the missing six. The common continuation/viability input, mandatory physical reset, complete source-to-reset costs, baseline competence and held-out evaluation remain required. No energetic advantage is established.
