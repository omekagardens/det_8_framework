## Purpose and claim discipline

Build useful provisional models with explicit state, growth/update and readout
interfaces across multiple declared cases. Constructive integration complements
probes and criticism; it does not wait for a surviving novel prediction. The
immediate consumer is finite matter/source modelling in the gravity, mass,
classical-physics and resulting native-unit programme.

Components may be supplied, borrowed, conjectured or calibrated. Record their
provenance, admitted domain, normalization or physical units, chosen parameters,
known failures and unresolved premises. Calibration and evidence used to choose
a model must be separated from tests of empirical predictions. The
[governance achievement distinctions](../../GOVERNANCE.md#1-architecture) apply:
**runs**, **reproduces**, **derives from DET** and **agrees with observations**
are separate reports, never synonyms. Usefulness is a named result or interface
that advances a decision; it does not imply empirical novelty or advantage.

Finite exploration does not require an all-size, covariance or continuum proof.
Claims of those properties still do. Known covariance failure remains failure;
a sequential operational model must expose schedule dependence and cannot be
presented as an order-independent native law. The scheduler is an implementation
order, never a global physical present. Preserve DET ontology, Option B,
Status M, non-smuggling and all physical/empirical claim requirements. RG2's
replicated-failure rule governs residual discovery, not the start of this track.

## PIM001 — finite growth, register and field baseline

**Question and decision.** Can one runnable dimensionless operational model
combine the accepted components and reproduce their specified state, order and
readout results across the complete registered cases? A successful result gives
a reproducible baseline for a later finite matter/source question; it is not
admission of a physical theory.

**Fixed inputs.** Use the original accepted four-birth law and the complete
[prepared source/readout ledger](../observables/native_source_readout_response.md),
with its [interval/preparation table](../observables/finite_interval_action_and_source_preparation.md),
[optional incidence register](../observables/finite_incidence_register.md),
[Gaussian field attachment](../observables/gaussian_field_transport_on_native_histories.md)
and accepted [marked-diamond state failure](../observables/native_field_state_across_a_causal_diamond.md).
Reuse these results and exact versions; change no probabilities, support,
preparations, field maps or readouts to make tests pass.

Start from the two supplied prefixes $a\prec b$, marks $(i,0)$ and counters
$(1,0)$ for $i=0,1$. Append exactly two births with every actual ideal and all
four fair suffix-mark pairs: **60 positive terminal marked histories per
preparation**, with total conditional mass one. Preserve actual vertex
identities and immutable marks; do not divide by automorphisms, postselect
histories or extend the horizon. The optional counters are included in this
baseline under their supplied memory/addressing assumptions. The sequential
three-pair Gaussian attachment is an external mathematical component, not a
native-law or quantum-physical derivation.

The implementation must expose finite state, one-step updates and fixed
readouts. Check stagewise mass/ideal eligibility, counters and marks across
empty, partial and full ideal cases. For terminal order indicator $D$ and
counter readout $Y$, reproduce the exact cells $(00,01,10,11)$:
$(246,295,0,779)/1320$ for $i=0$ and $(456,290,0,574)/1320$ for $i=1$,
including the accepted contrasts. Verify the target-first causal commutator
and preservation of the prepared $W_2$ restriction on every history, without
a kernel-as-zero quotient.

Reproduce the named $i=0$ $u/v$ swap with endpoint order, marks and counters
transported together: both structural weights are $19/1320$, but the full
Gaussian states differ. The fixed cover-link quadratic mean is **2 in both
histories**, for all four matched marks. This is one mean on one diamond,
not equality of diagnostic laws or a universal endpoint-state theorem. The
covariance counterexample must remain an explicit expected failure.

**Outputs and completion.** Produce one minimal runnable research model,
meaningful tests against the accepted results, a command/readme, an assumptions
record, a result report and exact input/output manifest. Run the registered
cases and report actual outcomes. Stop at the stable baseline and its distinct
integrated-output review, or a precise unresolved premise or failure; checkpoint
unfinished work rather than declaring completion. No replacement law or next
model is automatic. The intended public artifact location is
`research/provisional_integrated_model/PIM001`; it is not yet a published result.

**Evidence limits.** There is no empirical calibration, fitted constant or
empirical holdout in PIM001. Constants and Gaussian normalization are inherited
choices; known-answer fixtures are reproduction checks. No new derivation from
ontology, all-size/continuum law, physical clock, action, mass, energy, stress,
gravity coupling, physical preparation/readout or empirical agreement follows.
Future calibrated or conjectural extensions remain possible under separately
bounded questions and honest evidence labels.
