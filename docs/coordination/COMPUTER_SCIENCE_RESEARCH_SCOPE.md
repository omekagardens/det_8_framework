# Computer science and mathematics research scope

**Owner:** DET Comp-Sci Research. **Current remit:** general mathematics and
computing, including discrete structures, probability, optimization, algorithms,
complexity, information, numerical reasoning, representations and computation
under explicit resource constraints. AI applications are one possible consumer,
not the boundary of the lane. Useful supporting mathematics, finite proofs,
counterexamples and bounded research implementations are eligible.

Select and begin one useful finite question from current evidence and a small
relevant alternative set. Name the uncertainty, premises, prior work, output and
decision each candidate would resolve. An ordinary mathematical result or a
negative result is useful without a DET advantage claim. A general theorem may
stand on its stated assumptions; no artificial AI or physical interpretation is
required to justify it.

## Finite work and review

Before calculations or execution, record exact source versions and review
status, question, assumptions, method, output files, resource boundary, stopping
condition and review owner in the lane's control and current queue. State input
representation, arithmetic model and all relevant preprocessing, storage,
communication and output costs. Compare proposed algorithms with competent
conventional methods given the same information; do not hide an oracle or
weaken the required output. Distinguish a finite example from a universal or
asymptotic claim.

Maintain one active author package. Freeze completed work for a distinct reviewer
reserved by the coordinator; preserve original packets and negative outcomes.
A pending review blocks dependent adoption, not a compatible question using
accepted inputs. Each successor has a new finite control. Routine in-scope
selection and start do not require another human assignment.

Use the [four achievement distinctions](../../GOVERNANCE.md#1-architecture):
runs, reproduces, derives from DET and agrees with observations. Separately label
proved conditional results, provisional constructions and hypotheses, and
measured versus untested performance. Synthetic checks are not deployment or
physical evidence. Correctness, practical improvement, competitive improvement
and novelty require their own evidence.

CS-AI001's fixed-query exactness criterion and finite witness are accepted
mathematics; its evaluation design establishes no performance result. They do
not restrict future work to AI, KV selection or SlateOS. Historical closed
candidates and incomplete software checkpoints remain recorded; this scope
does not reopen their production backlog.

## Owned successors and continuity

Keep the owned current queue and handoff current: active work, frozen results,
review status, candidate next questions and blockers with owners and reopening
conditions. At completion, refresh relevant accepted inputs and reservations,
select and begin the next useful compatible question when capacity permits.
Do not treat packet completion or an unrelated pending review as a lane-wide
blocker, and do not generate repetitive extensions simply to remain busy.

The existing hourly coordinator heartbeat is the actual mechanism for resuming
an eligible idle lane after its turn ends. It resumes interrupted work first,
dispatches a continuation to the existing owner and verifies receiving activity,
without duplicating an active author. The lane owns scientific selection;
the coordinator owns context refresh, conflict resolution and scheduled
delivery. A prompt by itself does not wake a chat. Preserve the existing
schedule's cadence, target, model and notifications.

Follow the [current programme controls](FOUNDATIONAL_RESEARCH_SCOPE.md#current-programme-controls--16-november-2026):
fresh supported established-account capacity, ordinary service and strictly
more than 30% remaining in each applicable reported window before work and
substantial batches. Missing windows provide no capacity. Fail closed with a
minimal checkpoint for a failed or unreadable guard; no account, model, reset
credit, purchase or schedule workaround.

Minimal research code, finite execution and meaningful checks may support the
registered question. Unrelated production, deployment, maintenance, general
tooling, RET SDK work, dependency installation, paid API use, hardware purchases
and external outreach remain outside scope. Preserve other lanes' files,
protected evaluation and private material. Shared integration and publication
remain coordinator-owned.
