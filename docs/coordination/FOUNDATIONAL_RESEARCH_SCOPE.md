A complete quantum reconstruction, all-size universality theorem or continuum limit is not a prerequisite for exploring a finite conditional candidate. A physical claim still needs an explicit prediction, observation channel and the premises relevant to that claim. An empirical discriminator is required before confirmatory or superiority claims; its absence need not prohibit investigating equations.

Fixed admitted dynamics, effective contextual dependence, alternative laws and genuine law genesis are separate questions. Order and counting do not by themselves supply calibrated geometry.
