# Native geometry, gravity and measurement programme

23 September 2026 — RI-31. Planning scope: **simulations and existing public
data first**, as selected by the user. This plan turns the existing
[geometry work order](../track_b/QR_MAP_RELATIVITY_GEOMETRY_PLAN.md) into bounded
research packets. It does not announce a new native law, continuum theorem,
gravity derivation or measured result. Packet names below are coordination
labels, not replacements for the project's scientific or G1–G6 release gates.

**Recommended start:** QR specifies one native-input, record-responsive joint
growth law and proves its consistency or identifies a precise obstruction.
In parallel, the coordinator develops an observation-channel contract and
qualifies one public dataset. Both paths can make useful progress before a
continuum theorem exists. Matter/geometry coupling can be investigated alongside
the geometry work; a physical gravity claim still needs the later bridges.

| Existing result | What it supports | Missing result addressed here |
|---|---|---|
| The chosen two-port `L_XYZ` has width at most two. | A proved obstruction to its proposed dense, causally faithful, volume-filling role. | A native growth candidate that escapes that obstruction; increasing residual dimension alone does not do so. |

| Current gravity interpretation retains GR as Option B and metric-as-record as Status M. | A conventional comparison baseline and explicit interpretation boundaries. | A native matter/source law, controlled effective limit and testable readout. |

The width obstruction and native-input rules are in the published geometry
plan. The T8c description and `det8/models/joint_record_growth.py` are **local
reference evidence with unpublished source dependencies**; this plan neither
imports nor publishes them. Their useful boundary is summarized here so the
plan does not require those files as executable dependencies. Earlier accepted
models, proof inventories, source hashes and the original independent review
remain fixed.

The programme seeks three different outputs: an internally coherent native
candidate; a demonstrated geometric/gravitational regime; and an identifiable
prediction for actual records. Success in one does not certify the others.
Recovering GR from a native candidate would be a substantial internal result
even without a distinguishing empirical signature. A claim that observations
prefer DET would require such a signature and a fair comparison.

## 2. Native geometry: specify a law before estimating a metric

### NG-01 — one complete joint law, or a scoped obstruction

**Proposed owner:** QR; coordinator supplies independent proof/source review.
First perform a cheap primitive, operation-domain and chain/port-width audit.
A fixed finite-width family cannot serve the volume-filling target, even if
it has interesting record feedback. It may still merit a clearly narrower
finite-process theorem.

The first deliverable is a short candidate dossier containing:

1. **State and premises.** Define a finite order, append-only committed records
   and the complete live residual/type/controller/context payload. State the
   initial-state class, admissible domain, precursor ideals, settings, record
   alphabet, parameters and locality/read permissions. Identify every newly
   chosen restriction or transition function as an additional premise.
2. **Complete transition.** Specify exact joint weights and outcome-resolved
   payload maps, including zero branches, domain preservation and total mass.
   Include silent activity, stopping and never-commit possibilities if admitted;
   otherwise explicitly exclude them from this small model. A first-commit
   reduction needs a proof and its missing mass, not implicit conditioning.
3. **Covariance on the whole domain.** Transport order, precursors, complete
   records/settings/outcomes and residual coordinates together. Prove marked
   parent equivariance and equality of full unnormalized branch compositions
   for adjacent incomparable births after the canonical newborn swap. The
   ideals refer to the same old parent; the actual source/target types must be
   specified. Combine these identities with adjacent-swap connectivity to
   prove the claimed natural-label covariance. Preserve labeled multiplicities,
   including equal precursor sets, before aggregating unlabeled histories.
4. **Genuine feedback.** Exhibit admissible states with the same retained order
   and controlled remaining inputs but different records, producing different
   probabilities for an unlabeled next/future order question. Distinguish
   direct record dependence from residual-mediated dependence. Mark-only or
   label-only changes do not qualify. If matching residual inputs is impossible,
   disclose that limit; lawful input comparison does not authorize overwriting
   a committed record. A posterior mixture over a static hidden seed is not
   automatically a dynamical source-response law.
5. **Proof and adversarial checks.** Give a formula valid at every finite size
   and a full-domain theorem, or a counterexample/no-go for the precise proposed
   family. Specify exact small-parent checks, initially through four births,
   with independent row, payload, diamond and multiplicity oracles. Finite
   enumeration can refute a theorem or corroborate that horizon; it cannot
   establish the all-size result or a continuous-payload identity.

Use a joint Markov kernel or branch-map formulation appropriate to the actual
domain. Operational linearity, if used, is a stated premise; pair-kernel
biadditivity does not establish it. A whole positive-semidefinite cone with
total-entry mass and fixed positive linear complete branches already faces
the project's uninformative-record obstruction. Do not silently reuse that
domain to claim live-residual information transfer. Smaller domains or other
operations require explicit justification.

T8c's factorwise past-stability conditions were sufficient for its independent
order/record product. They are **not universal necessary conditions** for a
feedback law: valid joint factors may compensate. Impose the complete joint
identity and state locality separately. Global normalization may not read
forbidden records. Relabeling-invariant stage/parent-size dependence alone
does not guarantee path covariance. Uniform-over-ideals is an existing negative
control for that tempting inference.

**Native-input boundary:** target metrics, coordinates, meshes, target
sprinklings and borrowed growth kernels cannot generate the object then called
native. Supplied Hilbert spaces or amplitudes belong only to an explicitly
conditional comparison lane and do not satisfy the native-input test. A
claimed native representation must be constructed from its stated admitted
premises. Identify constants separately as derived, free law parameters,
calibrated or supplied; choosing units does not derive them. A coordinate-free
newly chosen formula is a *native-input candidate*, not yet a consequence of
existing DET principles. Borrowed models
remain useful separate comparisons. For example, the
[Rideout–Sorkin growth family](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9904062v3) follows its
own stated dynamical assumptions, not a derivation from DET's premises.

**Disposition:** accept one conditional existence/consistency theorem, reject
one precisely delimited family, or name the unresolved premise. Stop/reclassify
on a domain/mass failure, hidden global read, failed full-payload diamond or
feedback that disappears on unlabeled order questions. No automatic series of
replacement laws follows a negative result.

### NG-02 — histories and inexpensive regime screens

After NG-01, establish an all-size measurable state/kernel family and prefix
consistency: summing extensions recovers each prefix law, including absorbing
and never-commit outcomes. Construct the appropriate history measure and
measurable covariant event algebra; prove representation independence on that
algebra. A classical path-measure extension does not establish a quantum
decoherence-functional extension. These are different possible targets.

Finite simulations can begin from the accepted finite law while that extension
is studied. Predeclare sizes, initial states, parameter ranges, random sources,
statistics and computational limits. Check global and intrinsic interval-local
height/width, order density, interval sizes and small-order pattern laws across
scales. Preserve chain, antichain, layered and disconnected/thin failure cases.
An unbounded support for width is weak; an asymptotic target is
`Pr(width(C_N) <= k) -> 0` for every fixed k in a stated nonterminating/scaling
regime. Account for termination and the probability of any conditioned regime;
rare surviving examples cannot establish typical geometry.

These are rejection screens, not manifold certificates. Growing width can
coexist with an antichain and no useful spacetime. Reference sprinklings may
test diagnostic behavior but must retain their supplied-geometry provenance.
Do not tune a generator to target fixtures and report that fit as emergence.

### NG-03 — geometry, local measure and clocks

Select a precise limiting statement: scaling parameter, topology/notion of
convergence, dimension, order preservation, locality and error/probability
control. Begin with the simplest useful declared regime; success in a lower
dimensional toy regime does not establish four-dimensional spacetime.
Investigate stability across an explicit class of initial conditions and
parameters, rather than one selected seed.

The proof obligations are distinct:

- A manifoldlike Lorentzian order regime actually exists for the native law.
- Scaled native event counts converge to **local measures**, with a justified
  native counting law and normalization. One total count is insufficient.
  Replacing those counts by observed/retained records requires a separate
  formation/access channel; apparatus calibration is not a prerequisite for
  the internal native-count theorem.
- An applicable reconstruction result identifies geometry from that order and
  local measure, with every hypothesis supplied by the preceding results.
- Physical duration and signal records follow from clock/readout dynamics.
  Longest-chain length, birth index and coordinate time are not automatically
  measured proper time or seconds.

Order/number reconstruction results address identification once suitable
spacetimes and measure laws exist. They do not prove that a proposed generator
has such a limit; the distinction is central in
[Surya's causal-set review](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11544v2) and the recent
[spacetime reconstruction preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01907v1).
Recheck exact theorem hypotheses before applying either. Likewise, the
[corrected Benincasa–Dowker construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2725v4)
offers a conditional curvature/action comparison on suitably approximated
causal sets, not a theorem that a new growth law produces them.

## 3. Gravity: derive source response and a controlled limit

### GD-01 — matter and joint dynamics

This investigation can run alongside NG-02/03. Start with one explicit
excitation/source proposal coupled to the same admitted law. A dossier must
separate a chosen matter label from a persistent dynamical excitation, and
an input parameter from a predicted or calibrated mass scale.

| Required result | Evidence that would count | Insufficient substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent excitations | Existence, lifetime/stability and propagation in a stated regime. | A graph label, rank or unexplained spectral gap renamed mass. |
| Energy/momentum and inertial mass | Operational/time-translation and spatial-translation interpretation where applicable, dispersion or response law, units and controlled regime. | Importing an energy formula and calling it derived. |
| Source and backreaction | An explicit two-way matter/order law, response to controlled source changes and a justified continuum source interpretation. | Passive matter on supplied geometry or rest-event counts alone. |
| Consistency and stability | Domain preservation, balance identities, constraint propagation, well-posed evolution and perturbation control appropriate to the candidate. | Normalized probabilities alone. |

Relativistic source interpretation concerns energy, momentum, stresses and
their coupling. State local balance and boundary flux assumptions. Do not
require a globally conserved scalar energy on every curved spacetime without
the additional structure that defines it. Conventional
[GR dynamics](https://davidtong.org/pdfs/teaching/general-relativity/gr4.pdf)
and [Noether-charge methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9403028) provide
conditional comparison tools, not native derivations.

### GD-02 — weak-field response, then effective gravitational equations

The first gravity target is a **controlled source-dependent weak-field,
slow-motion limit**, with an explicit approximation parameter, error estimate
and matching of constants. Demonstrate a tidal/relative-free-fall response
and an independently specified clock or signal-propagation observable in the
same model. Redshift alone can arise in supplied kinematics or accelerated
frames; it does not establish native curvature dynamics. A single supported
accelerometer reading is not a curvature measurement.

The stronger target is GR, or a precisely bounded alternative, in a declared
effective regime. It needs derived equations, degrees of freedom, constraints,
conservation/balance, stability and initial-value behavior. An inverse-square
fit or action resemblance is insufficient. List dimensionless law parameters,
unit conversions, fitted/calibrated constants and genuine predictions
separately. Choosing units does not derive c, G, hbar or particle masses.

Complete this packet with a theorem and its domain, a scoped failure, or an
explicit unproved effective-law conjecture. An empirical discriminator is not
required to formulate these internal results; it is required before claiming
observations distinguish the native candidate from its conventional baseline.

## 4. Measurement: simulations and public data first

### ME-01 — observer channel and identifiability contract

**Proposed owner:** coordinator/application lane, with QR reviewing the
native/readout boundary. Make each link explicit:

`native history -> formed record -> emission/acquisition attempt -> detector
output -> retained/transmitted record -> analyst selection -> observable`.

The channel must account for exposure, false/null detections, loss, dead time,
timestamp resolution, latency, drift, retention and selection. Attempt logs
and actual receiver records have different evidential roles. Instrument
detections cannot be identified with fundamental births by assumption.

Two initial analytic counterexamples should accompany synthetic fixtures:

1. **Counts versus efficiency.** Under a supplied Poisson formation model with
   rate lambda and independent constant detection efficiency eta, counts
   identify only eta*lambda. Neither factor is separately identified. Unknown
   local volume normalization adds another ambiguity. History-dependent loss
   needs a richer model; this simple argument does not certify it.
2. **Clock offset versus path asymmetry.** Use
   `C_i(tau)=beta_i+integral_0^tau[1+s_i(u)]du+epsilon_i(tau)`.
   For the special noiseless transfer model, assume explicitly
   `C_A(t)=t+beta_A` and `C_B(t)=t+beta_B` with one shared transfer-time parameter
   t. Express d_AB, d_BA and B's turnaround in that same time unit. Unit rate
   relative to each observer's own proper time alone is insufficient. Let
   A1/B2 denote send/receive timestamps for A-to-B and B3/A4 for B-to-A. Then
   `(A4-A1)-(B3-B2)=d_AB+d_BA`, whereas
   `[(B2-A1)+(B3-A4)]/2=(beta_B-beta_A)+(d_AB-d_BA)/2`.
   Round-trip information does not separately identify one-way delay and
   offset without another premise. Unknown rates, turnaround/endpoint latency
   and varying paths require their own budgets.

Add adversarial simulations for drift, quantization, uncertain ordering,
finite pulse spacing versus instantaneous frequency, outages and selection.
Nonreceipt by a finite cutoff is not proof of nonemission, eternal nonarrival
or a horizon. Reuse the *lessons* of
[RI-26](../experiments/observer_signal_v1/EXPERIMENT.md), preserving its accepted
files. Export actual records separately from hidden simulator truth and retain
inconclusive outcomes. Proof of identifiability, finite coverage checks and
physical calibration remain separately labeled.

### ME-02 — one qualified public-data baseline

**Recommended first dataset:** a versioned GWOSC H1/L1 release around
[GW150914](https://gwosc.org/events/GW150914/). This is a practical test of a
conventional gravity measurement pipeline, selected for public access and
documented data products. It is not yet a dataset on which an unspecified
native law can be tested. No scientific data were downloaded in preparing
this plan, and no exact release/file set has yet been selected.

The first deliverable is a dataset card and frozen analysis design, before
fitting: release/version, DOI, exact files and hashes, detector/GPS intervals,
sample rate, units, calibration, quality/injection flags, missingness, prior
analyst access, conditioning, target statistic and uncertainty treatment.
Use a corrected release: GWOSC documents an approximately 1 ms offset in the
old version-1 4096 Hz downsampled product, corrected in version 2. Its
[technical documentation](https://gwosc.org/techdetails/) also describes
frequency-dependent calibration uncertainty, NaN gaps and the lack of
calibration below 10 Hz. Flag meanings and processing must match the chosen
release. Do not silently mix 4096/16384 Hz products or filter across gaps.

Then reproduce one conventional calibrated-strain/time-domain transient
analysis with predeclared surrounding comparison intervals and an independent
reference implementation or published calculation. Test the pipeline first
on synthetic signals/noise and record failures. The deliverable is a
reproducible calibrated-data analysis, not raw-detector reconstruction, a new
discovery, fundamental event counting or a DET-versus-GR residual claim.
GW150914 is already public and extensively analyzed; it is not a blind holdout.
Follow [GWOSC reuse/acknowledgment terms](https://gwosc.org/data/).

Keep alternative datasets conditional rather than starting several campaigns:

| Alternative | Qualification needed before selection |
|---|---|
| Existing public clock products/RI-11 path | Averaging windows, time scale, covariance, discontinuities and calibration; ingestion success does not make a clock product a direct proper-time observation. |
| [IGETS gravimeter data](https://isdc.gfz.de/igets-data-base) | Account/access arrangement and level selection: raw, instrument-corrected and geophysically corrected products answer different questions. Its [access service](https://isdc.gfz.de/igets-data-base/data-access) requires registration. |
| Laboratory clock redshift archives | A paper is not a qualified raw-data/calibration archive. The [NIST-listed millimetre-scale result](https://www.nist.gov/publications/resolving-gravitational-redshift-across-millimetre-scale-atomic-sample) is a comparison lead; suitable archived data availability has not been verified here. |

If GWOSC lacks metadata needed for the chosen statistic, narrow or reject that
analysis rather than substituting assumed precision. Dataset choice can change
once a native candidate supplies a different identifiable observable.

### ME-03 — a native prediction and a fair physical comparison

This packet waits for an explicit forward map from a fixed candidate law to
the same recorded observable analyzed under GR/conventional physics. Propagate
formation, channel, calibration, nuisance and approximation uncertainties.
Test whether a predicted difference survives nuisance freedom, calibration
floors and conventional alternatives before collecting more samples.

Freeze the law, parameter domains, initial/boundary conditions, coarse graining,
target/units/window, exclusions/missingness rules, stopping rule, calibration
validity, identifiable parameter combinations and comparison procedure.
Separate development/calibration/evaluation data with recorded prior access;
use genuinely unseen or prospective data for a claim that requires it.
Predeclare multiplicity, expected effect, error/power assumptions and resource
budget. Preserve actual source-freeze, custody and protected-validation
prerequisites for whichever workflow is selected; a hash does not supply
calibration, custody or independent evidence.

Stop or report inconclusive when the difference is absorbed by nuisance
parameters, lies below a justified floor, depends on unavailable metadata,
reuses evaluation data for tuning or lacks a native readout map. A rejected
prediction rejects its law-and-premise package; it does not uniquely decide
an ontology. Laboratory acquisition is a later option only after sensitivity
and identifiability justify a specific clock-transfer, differential-motion
or combined design.
