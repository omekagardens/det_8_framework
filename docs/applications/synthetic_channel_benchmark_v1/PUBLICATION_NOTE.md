project. It fixes C256, three references, invented three-category families,
core and stress grids, inference rules, costs and independently controlled

the candidate consumes at least 256 raw draws, while the standard sequential
reference has expected consumption at most 640/3 on every core cell and meets
the stated quality targets. This is a prospective proof, not observed
performance. A later pass could establish only the declared synthetic
operating-point conformance, not competitive practical value.
