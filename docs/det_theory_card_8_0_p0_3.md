## Executive Abstract (updated)

DET 8 proposes a foundational correction to DET 7’s treatment of agency and presence. The core cycle remains:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R^{-}_e
\xrightarrow[\text{determinate}]{\mathcal L}
\mathcal W_e
\xrightarrow[\text{strong presence}]{\mathfrak P_e}
\mathcal R^{+}_e
}
\]

The scientific obligation is to make the record‑possibility‑record mapping increasingly precise and falsifiable. Strong presence, agency, and the ontological openness of the future are *interpretations* that must earn additional physical status only if they produce distinctive measurable fruit.

**New in P0.3:**  
- An explicit **No Pre‑Existing Future Facts** invariant, forbidding any hidden variables or structures that pre‑fix unactualized outcomes.  
- A mandatory research challenge to identify an **imprint of becoming** in the committed record—a signature that could distinguish genuine openness from hidden determinism.  
- A new falsifier (F8‑OPEN) requiring the theory either to produce such a signature or to label “open becoming” as a metaphysical interpretation.  
- Integrated adversarial holdings reflecting the critique that if facts are created, the history must somehow show it, or else the distinction is empirically idle.

## 2. Scope Axiom (reaffirmed with open‑ontology emphasis)

DET 8 is a strictly causal‑local, relational, record‑determinate, future‑open theory. At every lawful local event:

1. The complete committed causal‑past record is determinate.
2. Local laws determinately generate the admissible successor structure; this structure includes propensities but no pre‑existing actualities.
3. All admissible successors obey locality, conservation, finite propagation, and declared structural constraints.
4. One lawful successor becomes actual in strong presence. Prior to this event, no fact of the matter exists as to which successor will be actualized.
5. The actualized consequence joins the record and thereafter constrains future possibility.
6. No unactualized alternative is treated as a second committed world or hidden completed future.
7. Agency, where present, belongs only to a selfhood‑bearing regime’s participation in actualization and is not a stored or externally writable state variable (M).
8. The transition from possibility to record must eventually be captured by a complete, locally normalized predictive kernel \(K(\mathcal R^{+}|\mathcal R^{-})\) that does not presuppose the existence of its own outcomes (A, O).

## 3. Non‑Negotiable Invariants (additions and modifications)

All invariants from P0.2 are retained. The following are added or sharpened:

### 3.4 Future Openness (clarified and extended)
The complete record need not uniquely determine the next committed actuality. Crucially, the future does not exist as a set of hidden facts waiting to be uncovered; the open possibilities are *real constraints* without yet being actual. This openness is currently an ontological claim. The roadmap must produce a predictive kernel with non‑singleton support and, additionally, seek evidence that the openness is not merely epistemic.

### 3.14 **(New) No Pre‑Existing Future Facts (Open‑Ontology Constraint)**
The physical record at any event contains no token, hidden variable, or structural element whose value uniquely pre‑fixes an unactualized future outcome. Admissible possibilities are real constraints, but the identity of the next committed actuality does not exist *in any form* before the actualization event. This prohibits:
- hidden completed futures disguised as records;
- many‑worlds branches treated as already‑existing parallel records;
- any claim that a future measurement outcome “is already determined but unknown.”
Predictions are expressed as lawful propensity kernels \(K(\mathcal R^{+}|\mathcal R^{-})\), not as pre‑existing values waiting to be uncovered. The kernel itself is part of the present record; its normalization does not require reference to unactualized alternatives as real entities.

### 3.15 **(New) Imprint of Becoming Challenge**
If genuine becoming creates facts rather than merely revealing them, the historical record must be capable of bearing some signature of that creativity—otherwise the distinction between creation and discovery is empirically empty. The theory must either identify such a signature or explicitly classify “open becoming” as a metaphysical interpretation that does not add predictive content. This is a standing, high‑priority research obligation (see F8‑OPEN).

### 3.16 Separation of Interpretational Layers (strengthened)
Any concept classified as metaphysical/interpretational (status M) shall not enter physical equations or be used to adjust physical predictions. Its fruit may be monitored, but it does not constitute a physical mechanism.

## 6. Determinate Record (with open‑ontology guardrails)

### 6.2 Record Types (A‑note added)
Calling a quantum superposition “an actual relational record” is permissible **only** if it is made clear that the superposition is a determinate relation that constrains future outcomes without containing those outcomes as pre‑existing facts. It is an open relation, not a list of hidden branches.

### 6.3 No Eternal Archive Requirement (A‑note)
The statement that the past event is fixed while present recoverable traces may dissipate is accepted. However, the relation between \(q\) reduction and lost information must be accounted for. The open‑ontology constraint does not require that all past openness be eternally inscribed; it requires that the present record be sufficient to generate the correct future propensities.

## 21. Mandatory Falsifiers and Consistency Gates (updated)

### New Falsifier: F8‑OPEN — Becoming Imprint Test (True Discriminator)
The theory must either:
(a) produce a well‑defined, operationally testable prediction that differs from a comparable deterministic hidden‑variable model with an equivalent predictive kernel, or
(b) admit that openness is empirically indistinguishable from hidden determinism and restrict “open becoming” to metaphysical status.
This gate must be addressed before any claim that the universe is ontologically open can be considered scientifically supported. Any proposed signature (e.g., growth of record, temporal asymmetry, violation of a specific inequality) must be explicitly falsifiable.

## 27. Provisional Final Principle (with ontological caution)

*The following is a statement of the desired ontology. Its physical content depends entirely on the completion of the research programme, including the Becoming Imprint Test. Until then, it is a regulative ideal.*

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R^{-}_e
\xrightarrow[\text{lawful and determinate}]{\mathcal L}
\mathcal W_e
\xrightarrow[\text{present and not pre-contained}]{\mathfrak P_e}
\mathcal R^{+}_e
}
\]

The existing record is definite.  
The laws definitively constrain what may become, without pre‑containing it.  
The actuality is not a hidden future dragged into the present; it is a new fact added to the record.  
Whether this addition leaves a detectable trace is **the open question that P0.3 places at the center of the programme**.
