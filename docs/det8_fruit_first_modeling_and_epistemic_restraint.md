DET 8 Propositional Supplement: Fruit-First Modeling and Epistemic Restraint
Status: Propositional steering guide Purpose: Guide DET 8 rewriting, simulation design, mathematical formalization, and falsifier development.
1. Core Epistemic Principle
DET 8 distinguishes between becoming and the record left by becoming.
[ \boxed{ \mathcal R^- \rightarrow \mathfrak P \rightarrow \mathcal R^+ } ]
where:
* (\mathcal R^-) is the determinate inherited record;
* (\mathfrak P) is present becoming;
* (\mathcal R^+) is the newly committed record.
DET proposes that the interior of present becoming may not be directly measurable. Any successful measurement of an event is itself already part of the resulting record.
Therefore:
[ \boxed{\text{DET measures the fruit of becoming, not becoming itself.}} ]
This is an epistemic limitation, not permission to make unfalsifiable physical claims.

2. Determinate Record, Open Becoming
Everything already actualized belongs to the record and is, in principle, available to deterministic modeling:
[ \mathcal R^-= {F,q,C,\theta,\pi,L,r,\sigma,H,\text{geometry, memory, structure,\ldots}}. ]
The record determinately constrains the lawful possibility structure:
[ \boxed{ \mathcal R^- \xrightarrow{\mathcal L} \mathcal W } ]
where (\mathcal W) specifies what may lawfully become and, where appropriate, its amplitudes or propensities.
DET 8 does not require that the record uniquely determine the next actuality:
[ \mathcal W \xrightarrow{\mathfrak P} \mathcal R^+. ]
Thus:
[ \boxed{ \text{law-determinate} \neq \text{future-determinate}. } ]

3. Agency Belongs Only to Presence
Agency must not be represented as:
* a persistent state variable;
* a numerical agency field;
* an externally supplied control input;
* random noise;
* a force or energy source;
* an exception to physical law.
There should be no canonical update such as
[ a^+=f(a,q,C,P,\ldots). ]
Such an equation converts agency into another consequence of the prior record.
Instead:
[ \boxed{\text{Agency is a selfhood-bearing regime's participation in present becoming.}} ]
Intentions, habits, memories, preferences, constraints, learned policies, and prior choices are records and may be modeled. They may strongly predict subsequent action.
They are not agency itself.
Disposition is record. Agency is present enactment.
After an agentic act becomes actual, its consequences become ordinary record and are again available to mathematical modeling.

4. Presence Must Not Become a Hidden Variable
DET 8 should resist attempts to mathematically fill the inaccessible center merely to make the theory appear complete.
The mathematical program should emphasize:
[ \mathcal R^- \rightarrow \mathcal W ]
and
[ \mathcal R^- \rightarrow P(\mathcal R^+|\mathcal R^-), ]
rather than inventing inaccessible variables inside (\mathfrak P).
Strong presence is an ontological proposition unless and until it produces a distinct measurable consequence.
DET therefore models:
* records rigorously;
* lawful possibilities structurally;
* probabilities quantitatively;
* becoming itself with epistemic restraint.

5. Participation Aperture Is Not Presence
The record-derived DET clock/participation function should remain mathematically distinct from strong presence.
A provisional participation aperture may be written:
[ \Pi_i= \sigma_i \frac{1}{1+F_i} \frac{1}{1+H_i} \frac{1}{\gamma_{v,i}} \frac{1}{1+\lambda_Pq_i}. ]
(\Pi_i) describes how the current structure constrains physical participation.
It is measurable/modelable record-side physics.
It is not agency and should not be identified with the ontological event of becoming.

6. Mutable (q) Remains Entirely Record-Side
Structural history (q) belongs to the committed record.
[ q\in\mathcal R. ]
Mutable (q) means that the present structural imprint of history may change.
It does not mean that an actualized event becomes unactualized.
Therefore:
\text{release of retained constraint}, \quad \text{not erasure of history}. } ]
Only actualized events may write (q). Unactualized possibilities must never accumulate structural history.

7. Grace, Healing, and Jubilee Should Primarily Modify Possibility
DET 8 should investigate moving Boundary operations away from direct compulsory state writes.
The preferred conceptual architecture is:
[ \mathcal W^0 \xrightarrow{\mathcal B} \mathcal W^B \xrightarrow{\mathfrak P} \mathcal R^+. ]
Boundary action changes the lawful possibility landscape rather than coercively selecting the resulting record.
Provisional interpretations:
* Grace: enlarges or strengthens lawful resource/recovery possibilities.
* Healing: restores or strengthens lawful relational/coherence possibilities.
* Jubilee: opens lawful release from retained structural constraint (q).
Thus:
[ \boxed{\text{Boundary action permits or favors; it does not compel.}} ]
Whether each operator should alter the support of possible futures, their propensities, or both remains an open research question.

8. Fruit-First Falsifiability Rule
No invisible DET mechanism may be defended solely by appealing to its invisibility.
For every proposed physical mechanism (X):
[ X \rightarrow \text{predicted record-level signature}. ]
DET must specify a null model:
[ H_0(\mathcal R^+|\mathcal R^-) ]
and the proposed DET model:
[ H_X(\mathcal R^+|\mathcal R^-,X). ]
A physical DET claim requires some declared domain in which:
[ H_X\neq H_0. ]
If no measurable distinction exists, (X) may remain an ontological or theological proposition, but it must not be presented as an experimentally established physical mechanism.
Unexpected fruit alone is never evidence of Grace, agency, Jubilee, or Boundary action.

9. Interpretation Must Be Separated From Measurement
The same empirical transition may permit multiple interpretations.
For example:
[ \mathcal R^- \rightarrow \mathcal R^+ ]
might be interpreted as:
* deterministic hidden dynamics;
* fundamental stochasticity;
* quantum actualization;
* DET becoming;
* agentic participation.
DET must not claim experimental discrimination where none exists.
A scientist should be able to reject DET's metaphysics of agency while still testing its record-level physical predictions.
This separation strengthens rather than weakens the research program.

10. Simulation Rule
DET 8 simulations do not simulate metaphysical presence.
They simulate:
1. the inherited record;
2. the lawful possibility structure;
3. predicted outcome distributions;
4. an explicit computational actualizer when an outcome is required;
5. the resulting committed record.
The computational actualizer is a simulation device, not a claim that random-number generation, optimization, or sampling is what ontological becoming actually is.
This distinction must remain explicit in code and documentation.

11. Regression Rule
DET 7 should remain a regression suite during DET 8 development.
First remove stored agency from deterministic matter-sector equations and determine which existing results survive.
Preserve wherever possible:
* locality;
* conservation;
* gravity;
* clock behavior;
* momentum;
* binding;
* mutable-(q) stability;
* coherence;
* pointer records;
* recovery behavior;
* established calibration readouts.
Any failure following removal of agency must be investigated rather than automatically patched.
A central migration question is:
[ \boxed{ \text{Was agency accidentally doing ordinary physical work in DET 7?} } ]
If so, that physical function must be derived from record-side variables rather than returned to agency.

12. Anti-Smuggling Rules
DET 8 must reject:
Hidden determinism: Do not claim open becoming while secretly calculating the unique future inside an inaccessible variable.
Hidden randomness: Do not rename arbitrary numerical noise "agency."
Hidden nonlocality: Do not use global reconciliation, normalization, or selection while describing the mechanism as strictly local.
Hidden intervention: Do not use Grace or Boundary action as an unexplained exception to conservation or causal propagation.
Hidden agency variables: Do not reintroduce agency under names such as intention strength, choice field, will coefficient, or actualization bias unless the quantity is explicitly a measurable record-side variable.
Fruit retrofitting: Do not label unexpected successful outcomes as evidence for DET after observing them. Signatures and thresholds must be declared prospectively.

13. Claim-Status Discipline
Every major DET 8 statement should be identifiable as one of:
* Observed: directly supported by established measurement.
* Derived: follows mathematically from declared DET assumptions.
* Modeled: demonstrated within a DET simulation.
* Predicted: produces a prospective empirical test.
* Propositional: candidate DET architecture not yet established.
* Ontological: interpretation not presently discriminated by experiment.
* Theological: relational or Christian interpretation not established by DET physics.
Movement between categories requires evidence.
Simulation success alone does not promote a claim to observation.

14. Steering Question for Every New Equation
Before adding an equation to DET 8, ask:
Does this equation describe the record, constrain lawful possibilities, or pretend to calculate becoming itself?
If it describes the record, it belongs naturally in the physical model.
If it constrains possibilities, it requires explicit locality, conservation, and falsifiability tests.
If it claims to calculate becoming, the burden of justification is substantially higher.

15. Steering Question for Every Falsifier
For every proposed falsifier, ask:
What observable fruit would differ if this DET claim were false?
If no answer can be given, the claim is not presently a physical falsifier.
It may still be philosophically important, but its status must remain explicit.

16. Working DET 8 Research Principle
[ \boxed{ \text{Closed in law.} \quad \text{Open in becoming.} \quad \text{Local in presence.} \quad \text{Determinate in record.} \quad \text{Tested by fruit.} } ]
DET 8 should seek the strongest possible mathematical description of what can be measured before and after becoming, while resisting the temptation to manufacture mathematical certainty about the present merely because the present is difficult or impossible to observe directly.
The scientific burden of DET lies in the imprint. The ontology of becoming should never be used to escape that burden.
