## 4. Conserved energy-momentum, virial quadrupole compensation, and multipole contrast

Because $\Sigma^{ab}_{\rm beat}(t,\cdot),\Phi^{\rm beat}_{mn}(t,\cdot)\in C_c^\infty(B_L(0))$, integrating $T^{00}_{\rm beat,tot}=\partial_1^2\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}$ and $T^{0b}_{\rm beat,tot}=-\delta^{b1}\partial_1\partial_0\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}$ over $\mathbb R^3$ yields zero beat energy and zero beat linear momentum at every $t\in\mathbb R$. Furthermore, integrating $y_aT^{00}_{\rm beat,tot}=y_a\partial_1^2\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}$ and $y_aT^{0b}_{\rm beat,tot}=-\delta^{b1}y_a\partial_1\partial_0\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}$ once by parts on $\mathbb R^3$ gives $\int_{\mathbb R^3}y_aT^{00}_{\rm beat,tot}\,d^3y=-\delta_{a1}\int_{\mathbb R^3}\partial_1\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}\,d^3y=0$ and $\int_{\mathbb R^3}y_aT^{0b}_{\rm beat,tot}\,d^3y=\delta_{a1}\delta^{b1}\int_{\mathbb R^3}\partial_0\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}\,d^3y$ (symmetric in $a,b$), so the beat mass dipole and total angular momentum $J^{ab}_{{\rm tot},z}=c^{-1}\int_{\mathbb R^3}(y_aT^{0b}_{{\rm tot},z}-y_bT^{0a}_{{\rm tot},z})\,d^3y=0$ vanish identically at all $t\in\mathbb R$. Hence $\psi_+$, $\psi_-$, and $\rho_{\rm inc}$ share the exact constant total energy $E_{\rm tot}:=\int_{\mathbb R^3}T^{00}_{\rm tot,inc}(y)\,d^3y=E_{\rm cav,inc}+E_{\rm support}^{\rm inc}+E_{\rm plateau}^{\rm dyn}>E_{\rm cav,inc}$ (where $E_{\rm support}^{\rm inc}:=\int_{\mathbb R^3\setminus\Omega}T^{00}_{\rm st,inc}(y)\,d^3y>0$ and $E_{\rm plateau}^{\rm dyn}:=U_{\rm col}^{\rm dyn,(12)}\int_{\mathbb R^3}(1-\chi_{\rm in}(y))\chi_{\rm out}(|y|/L)\,d^3y>0$), zero linear momentum $P^a_{{\rm tot},z}=0$, zero angular momentum $J^{ab}_{{\rm tot},z}=0$, and identical mass monopole and dipole moments.

Unlike the orthogonal two-mode profile of the [coherent two-mode cavity beat note](coherent_cavity_beat_and_retarded_tidal_readout.md) (which was odd in $y_1$ and $y_2$), here $G_{\rm diff}(y)=\chi_{\rm box}(y)\cos(k_{\rm beat}y_1)$ is even in each coordinate $y_1,y_2,y_3$. Integrating $y_ay_bT^{00}_{\rm beat,tot}(t,y)=y_ay_b\partial_1^2\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}(t,y)$ twice by parts on $\mathbb R^3$ (and comparing against $\int_{\mathbb R^3}T^{ab}_{\rm beat,tot}\,d^3y=\partial_0^2\int_{\mathbb R^3}\Sigma^{ab}_{\rm beat}\,d^3y$) yields the exact whole-space relativistic virial / collar compensation relation:
$$
\int_{\mathbb R^3}T^{00}_{\rm beat,tot}(t,y)\,d^3y=0,\qquad
\int_{\mathbb R^3}y_aT^{00}_{\rm beat,tot}(t,y)\,d^3y=0,\qquad
P^a_{{\rm tot},z}=0,\qquad J^{ab}_{{\rm tot},z}=0,
$$
$$
M^{\rm beat}_{ab}(t):=\int_{\mathbb R^3}y_ay_bT^{00}_{\rm beat,tot}(t,y)\,d^3y
=2\delta_{a1}\delta_{b1}\int_{\mathbb R^3}\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}(t,y)\,d^3y
=\frac{L^5}{2\pi^2}\rho_{\rm beat}\cos(\Omega_{\rm beat}t)\,\delta_{a1}\delta_{b1}\,\mathcal I_{\rm diff}\,\mathcal I_0^2,
$$
$$
\int_\Omega y_1^2T^{00}_{\rm beat}(t,y)\,d^3y=+\frac{L^5}{2\pi^2}\rho_{\rm beat}\cos(\Omega_{\rm beat}t),\qquad
\bigl|M^{\rm beat}_{11}(t)\bigr|\le 4\delta_1(1+4\delta_1)^2\,\frac{L^5}{2\pi^2}\rho_{\rm beat}<\frac{1}{148}\,\frac{L^5}{2\pi^2}\rho_{\rm beat},
\tag{18}
$$
where $\mathcal I_0:=\int_{\mathbb R}\vartheta(u)\,du\in\bigl[1,1+4\delta_1\bigr]=\bigl[1,\frac{151}{150}\bigr]$ and $\mathcal I_{\rm diff}:=\int_{\mathbb R}\vartheta(u)\cos(2\pi u)\,du$. To verify both formulas in (18), note first that on the uncollared interior $\Omega=[-L/2,L/2]^3$, differentiating $F(u):=\frac{u^2}{2\pi}\sin(2\pi u)+\frac{u}{2\pi^2}\cos(2\pi u)-\frac{1}{4\pi^3}\sin(2\pi u)$ gives $F'(u)=u^2\cos(2\pi u)$, so $\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}u^2\cos(2\pi u)\,du=F(1/2)-F(-1/2)=-\frac{1}{2\pi^2}$ and $\int_\Omega y_1^2T^{00}_{\rm beat}(t,y)\,d^3y=-\rho_{\rm beat}\cos(\Omega_{\rm beat}t)L^5\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}u^2\cos(2\pi u)\,du=+\frac{L^5}{2\pi^2}\rho_{\rm beat}\cos(\Omega_{\rm beat}t)$. On $\mathbb R^3$, however, $\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}\cos(2\pi u)\,du=0$ kills the entire interior contribution to $\mathcal I_{\rm diff}=\int_{[-s_2,-1/2]\cup[1/2,s_2]}\vartheta(u)\cos(2\pi u)\,du$, leaving only the thin collar endcaps of total measure $2(s_2-\frac12)=4\delta_1=\frac{1}{150}$ (where $\cos(2\pi u)<0$), so $|\mathcal I_{\rm diff}|\le 4\delta_1=\frac{1}{150}$ and $4\delta_1(1+4\delta_1)^2=\frac{1}{150}\cdot\frac{22801}{22500}=\frac{22801}{3{,}375{,}000}<\frac{1}{148}$ ($148\times 22801=3{,}374{,}548<3{,}375{,}000$). Confining the normal stress jump $T^{11}_{\rm beat}|_{x_1=\pm L/2}=+\rho_{\rm beat}\cos(\Omega_{\rm beat}t)$ across $[-s_2L,-L/2]\cup[L/2,s_2L]$ induces an opposing collar energy-density dipole layer that cancels the naive interior mass quadrupole up to $O(\delta_1)$.

## 5. Retarded harmonic-gauge potential, exact axial nullity, and asymptotic $1/r$ contrast

Supply the whole-space linearized Einstein equations in harmonic (de Donder) gauge with the no-incoming-wave retarded integral from Eq. (9) of the [retarded stress calculation](retarded_stress_at_fixed_density.md), for each preparation label $z\in\{+,-,\mathrm{inc}\}$:
$$
\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}^z=-\frac{16\pi G}{c^4}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{\mu\nu},\qquad
\Box=-\partial_0^2+\nabla^2,\qquad

$$
$$

\tag{19}

where $T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{00}=T^{00}_{{\rm tot},z}$, $T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{0b}=-T^{0b}_{{\rm tot},z}$, and $T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{ab}=T^{ab}_{{\rm tot},z}$. Changing variables to $w=x-y$ writes $\bar h_{\mu\nu}^z(t,x)=\frac{4G}{c^4}\int_{\mathbb R^3}|w|^{-1}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{\mu\nu}(t-|w|/c,x-w)\,d^3w$; for $|x|<R_0$, the integrand vanishes outside $|w|\le R_0+3L$ and all $(t,x)$-derivatives are dominated by integrable multiples of $|w|^{-1}\in L^1(B_{R_0+3L}(0))$. Thus $\bar h_{\mu\nu}^z\in C^\infty(\mathbb R^{1,3})$, differentiating under the $w$-integral gives $\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}^z(t,x)=\frac{4G}{c^4}\int_{\mathbb R^3}|w|^{-1}\bigl(-\partial_0T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{0\nu}+\partial_{y_a}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{a\nu}\bigr)(t-|w|/c,x-w)\,d^3w=0$ by (17), and applying the Gauss–Green theorem on $\epsilon<|w|<R_0+3L$ ($\epsilon\to 0^+$) to $V_a(w):=\bigl[-\frac{\hat w_a}{r_w^2}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{\mu\nu}+\frac{1}{r_w}\partial_{y_a}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{\mu\nu}-\frac{\hat w_a}{cr_w}\partial_tT^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{\mu\nu}\bigr](t-r_w/c,x-w)$ ($r_w=|w|$, $\hat w=w/r_w$, $\nabla_w\cdot V=-r_w^{-1}\Box T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{\mu\nu}$) proves $\Box_x\bar h_{\mu\nu}^z=-\frac{16\pi G}{c^4}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{\mu\nu}$ everywhere on $\mathbb R^{1,3}$. By linearity, $\bar h_{\mu\nu}^\pm(t,x)=\bar h_{\mu\nu}^{\rm inc}(x)\pm\bar h_{\mu\nu}^{\rm beat}(t,x)$ with static $\bar h_{\mu\nu}^{\rm inc}(x)$.

Adopt the linearized Riemann curvature convention $R^\rho{}_{\sigma\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\Gamma^\rho_{\nu\sigma}-\partial_\nu\Gamma^\rho_{\mu\sigma}+\Gamma^\rho_{\mu\alpha}\Gamma^\alpha_{\nu\sigma}-\Gamma^\rho_{\nu\alpha}\Gamma^\alpha_{\mu\sigma}$ of the [static stress-quadrupole construction](stress_quadrupole_at_equal_energy.md) and [retarded stress calculation](retarded_stress_at_fixed_density.md), giving the gauge-invariant electric curvature tensor $R^{(1),z}_{0a0b}=-\frac12\partial_0^2h_{ab}^z+\frac12\partial_0\bigl(\partial_ah_{0b}^z+\partial_bh_{0a}^z\bigr)-\frac12\partial_a\partial_bh_{00}^z$.

**Proposition 1 (Exact finite-radius axial nullity along $\pm e_1$).** *For every preparation $z\in\{+,-,\mathrm{inc}\}$, every finite radius $r\ge 0$ (and in particular every exterior radius $r>3L$ strictly outside $\overline{B_{3L}(0)}$), and every time $t\in\mathbb R$, both transverse tidal shear components in $(e_1)^\perp=\operatorname{span}\{e_2,e_3\}$ vanish identically along the longitudinal axis $x=\pm re_1$:*

\tag{20}

*Proof.* By (14) and (16), $\Phi_1^{\rm inc}\equiv 0$, $\Phi^{\rm beat}_{11}\equiv 0$, $\Sigma^{ab}_{\rm beat}\propto\delta^{a1}\delta^{b1}$, and $\Phi^{\rm beat}_{22}\equiv\Phi^{\rm beat}_{33}$. Let $R_2(y_1,y_2,y_3):=(y_1,-y_2,y_3)$ and $S_{23}(y_1,y_2,y_3):=(y_1,y_3,y_2)$ generate the transverse reflections and swap. Because $\chi_{\rm in}$, $\chi_{\rm box}$, and $|y|$ are invariant under $R_2$ and $S_{23}$, $\Phi_2^{\rm inc}(S_{23}y)=\Phi_3^{\rm inc}(y)$ gives $T^{\rm tot,inc}_{22}(S_{23}y)=T^{\rm tot,inc}_{33}(y)$, while $T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{00}(R_2y)=T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{00}(y)=T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{00}(S_{23}y)$ and $\delta^{cd}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{cd}(R_2y)=\delta^{cd}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{cd}(y)=\delta^{cd}T^{\mathrm{tot},z}_{cd}(S_{23}y)$. Since $|Ox-Oy|=|x-y|$ for $O\in\{R_2,S_{23}\}$, the retarded integral (19) obeys $h_{00}^z(t,R_2x)=h_{00}^z(t,x)=h_{00}^z(t,S_{23}x)$ (and static $h_{22}^{\rm inc}(S_{23}x)=h_{33}^{\rm inc}(x)$ with $\partial_0^2h_{ab}^{\rm inc}=0$). Differentiating at the fixed points $x=(\pm r,0,0)$ of $R_2$ and $S_{23}$ yields $\partial_2\partial_3h_{00}^z(t,\pm re_1)=-\partial_2\partial_3h_{00}^z(t,\pm re_1)=0$ and $\partial_2^2h_{00}^z(t,\pm re_1)=\partial_3^2h_{00}^z(t,\pm re_1)$, proving (20). $\square$

For $|x|>L$ (strictly outside $\operatorname{supp}G_{\rm diff},\operatorname{supp}G_{\rm sum}\subset B_L(0)$), define the two smooth outgoing scalar Helmholtz convolutions $I_{\rm diff}(x)$ and $I_{\rm sum}(x)$, and integrate $\partial_{y_k}$ and $\partial_{y_k}\partial_{y_l}$ by parts in (19) using $\partial_{y_k}\bigl(|x-y|^{-1}e^{ik_{\rm beat}|x-y|}\bigr)=-\partial_{x_k}\bigl(|x-y|^{-1}e^{ik_{\rm beat}|x-y|}\bigr)$ and $T^{\rm beat,tot}_{0b}=+\delta_{b1}\partial_0\partial_1\Sigma^{11}_{\rm beat}$:

## 6. Explicit finite-radius wave-zone remainder certificate along $n_*=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(e_1+e_2)$

We now replace the asymptotic $O(r^{-2})$ symbol in (22) by an explicit finite-radius wave-zone error bound valid at every exterior radius $r>3L$, certifying strictly nonzero curvature contrast at $r_*=250L=2000c\tau>3L$.

First express $\Delta\mathcal E(t,r)=2D^{ab}R^{(1),\mathrm{beat}}_{0a0b}(t,rn_*)$ exactly on $|x|>L$ in terms of the directional derivatives $\partial_\parallel:=n_*\cdot\nabla=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(\partial_1+\partial_2)$, $\partial_\vartheta:=e_\vartheta\cdot\nabla=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}(\partial_1-\partial_2)$, and $\partial_3=e_3\cdot\nabla$.
