## 2. Non-perturbative Heisenberg evolution and 15-poset causal signaling reconstruction

We now compute the exact non-perturbative Heisenberg evolution of $Z_r^{\rm det}$ and $Y_r^{\rm det}$ on any history $H$ and coupling vector $\boldsymbol\lambda\in\mathbb R^4$. First conjugate $|{\pm}\rangle\langle{\mp}|_r\otimes I_{\rm field}$ by the local kick unitary $U_r(H;\lambda_r)=P_{r,+1}^{(x)}\otimes\mathcal W_H(-\lambda_r e_r)+P_{r,-1}^{(x)}\otimes\mathcal W_H(+\lambda_r e_r)$ from (3). Because $P_{r,\pm 1}^{(x)}|{\pm}\rangle_r=|{\pm}\rangle_r$, $\langle{\mp}|_r P_{r,\mp 1}^{(x)}=\langle{\mp}|_r$, and $D_{rr}(H)=0\implies\mathcal W_H(\pm\lambda_r e_r)\mathcal W_H(\pm\lambda_r e_r)=\mathcal W_H(\pm 2\lambda_r e_r)$, we obtain on all of $\mathcal H_{\rm tot}$:
$$
U_r(H;\lambda_r)^\dagger\bigl(|{\pm}\rangle\langle{\mp}|_r\otimes I_{\rm field}\bigr)U_r(H;\lambda_r)
=|{\pm}\rangle\langle{\mp}|_r\otimes\mathcal W_H(\pm 2\lambda_r e_r),
$$
$$
U_r(H;\lambda_r)^\dagger\bigl(Z_r^{\rm det}\otimes I_{\rm field}\bigr)U_r(H;\lambda_r)
=|+\rangle\langle -|_r\otimes\mathcal W_H(+2\lambda_r e_r)+|-\rangle\langle +|_r\otimes\mathcal W_H(-2\lambda_r e_r)
=Z_r^{\rm det}\otimes\cos\bigl(2\lambda_r\overline{\phi_r(H)}\bigr)+Y_r^{\rm det}\otimes\sin\bigl(2\lambda_r\overline{\phi_r(H)}\bigr),
$$
$$
U_r(H;\lambda_r)^\dagger\bigl(Y_r^{\rm det}\otimes I_{\rm field}\bigr)U_r(H;\lambda_r)
=i|+\rangle\langle -|_r\otimes\mathcal W_H(+2\lambda_r e_r)-i|-\rangle\langle +|_r\otimes\mathcal W_H(-2\lambda_r e_r)
=Y_r^{\rm det}\otimes\cos\bigl(2\lambda_r\overline{\phi_r(H)}\bigr)-Z_r^{\rm det}\otimes\sin\bigl(2\lambda_r\overline{\phi_r(H)}\bigr),
\tag{6}
$$
using $Z_r^{\rm det}=|+\rangle\langle -|_r+|-\rangle\langle +|_r$, $Y_r^{\rm det}=i|+\rangle\langle -|_r-i|-\rangle\langle +|_r$, and $\mathcal W_H(\pm 2\lambda_r e_r)=\cos(2\lambda_r\overline{\phi_r(H)})\pm i\sin(2\lambda_r\overline{\phi_r(H)})$ (matching the first-order commutators $i[X_r^{\rm det},Z_r^{\rm det}]=+2Y_r^{\rm det}$ and $i[X_r^{\rm det},Y_r^{\rm det}]=-2Z_r^{\rm det}$).

By Corollary 1(1), it remains only to conjugate (6) sequentially by $U_s(H;\lambda_s)$ for each strict causal predecessor $s\in J^-(r;H)$ ($s\prec_H r$). For $s\prec_H r$, the target-first commutator (1) fixes $D_{rs}(H)=+\beta$ and $D_{sr}(H)=-\beta$. Applying the Weyl relation twice to $\mathcal W_H(+\sigma_s\lambda_s e_s)\mathcal W_H(\pm 2\lambda_r e_r)\mathcal W_H(-\sigma_s\lambda_s e_s)$ for $\sigma_s\in\{+1,-1\}$ gives
$$

=\exp\!\left(-\frac{i}{2}(\sigma_s\lambda_s)(\pm 2\lambda_r)D_{sr}(H)\right)\mathcal W_H\bigl(\sigma_s\lambda_s e_s\pm 2\lambda_r e_r\bigr)

$$
$$
\mathcal W_H(+\sigma_s\lambda_s e_s)\,\mathcal W_H(\pm 2\lambda_r e_r)\,\mathcal W_H(-\sigma_s\lambda_s e_s)
=e^{\pm i\beta\lambda_r\sigma_s\lambda_s}\exp\!\left(-\frac{i}{2}(\pm 2\lambda_r)(-\sigma_s\lambda_s)D_{rs}(H)\right)\mathcal W_H(\pm 2\lambda_r e_r)
=e^{\pm 2i\beta\lambda_r\sigma_s\lambda_s}\mathcal W_H(\pm 2\lambda_r e_r),
$$
$$
U_{\rm tot}(H;\boldsymbol\lambda)^\dagger\bigl(Z_r^{\rm det}\otimes I_{\rm field}\bigr)U_{\rm tot}(H;\boldsymbol\lambda)
=\bigl(|+\rangle\langle -|_r\,e^{+i\Theta_r(H;\boldsymbol\lambda)}\bigr)\otimes\mathcal W_H(+2\lambda_r e_r)
+\bigl(|-\rangle\langle +|_r\,e^{-i\Theta_r(H;\boldsymbol\lambda)}\bigr)\otimes\mathcal W_H(-2\lambda_r e_r),
$$

U_{\rm tot}(H;\boldsymbol\lambda)^\dagger\bigl(Y_r^{\rm det}\otimes I_{\rm field}\bigr)U_{\rm tot}(H;\boldsymbol\lambda)

\tag{7}
$$
where $\Theta_r(H;\boldsymbol\lambda):=2\beta\lambda_r\sum_{s\prec_H r}\lambda_s X_s^{\rm det}$ acts strictly on $\bigotimes_{s\in J^-(r;H)}\mathcal H_s$ (commuting with $|{\pm}\rangle\langle{\mp}|_r$ since $r\notin J^-(r;H)$) and uses $\sum_{\sigma_s\in\{+1,-1\}}e^{\pm 2i\beta\lambda_r\sigma_s\lambda_s}P_{s,\sigma_s}^{(x)}=e^{\pm 2i\beta\lambda_r\lambda_s X_s^{\rm det}}$.

## 3. Unselected 60-history source discrimination under an imperfect erasure detector and boundary-image witness

Adopt the shared faulty three-outcome readout channel convention of the [complete-trial source response](native_complete_trial_readout.md) on the receiver's ideal $Y_x^{\rm det}$-measurement. On $\mathcal H_x$, the ideal spectral projections of $Y_x^{\rm det}$ are $\Pi_{x,y_{\rm ideal}}^{(y)}:=\frac12(I_x+y_{\rm ideal}Y_x^{\rm det})$ for $y_{\rm ideal}\in\{-1,+1\}$. Conditional on $y_{\rm ideal}$, one common unknown detector parameter tuple $\xi=(f,n,e)$ in the nonempty compact uncertainty box
$$
C:=[f_-,f_+]\times[n_-,n_+]\times[e_-,e_+]\subset[0,1]^3,\qquad
0\le e_-\le e_+<1,\qquad
0\le f_-\le f_+,\quad 0\le n_-\le n_+,\quad f_++n_+<1,
$$
$$
\begin{array}{c|cc}
M_\xi(y_{\rm obs}\mid y_{\rm ideal}) & y_{\rm ideal}=-1 & y_{\rm ideal}=+1\\ \hline
y_{\rm obs}=-1 & (1-e)(1-f) & (1-e)n\\
y_{\rm obs}=+1 & (1-e)f & (1-e)(1-n)\\
y_{\rm obs}=\perp & e & e
\end{array}
\qquad\bigl(k:=1-f-n\ge 1-f_+-n_+>0\bigr),
$$
$$
E_x^{(-1)}(\xi):=(1-e)\left[\frac{1-f+n}{2}I_x-\frac{k}{2}Y_x^{\rm det}\right],\qquad
E_x^{(+1)}(\xi):=(1-e)\left[\frac{1+f-n}{2}I_x+\frac{k}{2}Y_x^{\rm det}\right],\qquad
E_x^{(\perp)}(\xi):=eI_x,
\tag{10}
$$
produces the observed outcome $Y_{\rm obs}\in\{-1,+1,\perp\}$, where $f$ is the $-1\to +1$ false-positive flip rate, $n$ is the $+1\to -1$ false-negative flip rate, and $e$ is the state-, history-, setting-, and source-independent erasure probability. Each column of $M_\xi$ is nonnegative and sums to $(1-e)+e=1$, and $\{E_x^{(-1)}(\xi),E_x^{(+1)}(\xi),E_x^{(\perp)}(\xi)\}$ is a positive operator-valued measure on $\mathcal H_x$ satisfying $E_x^{(-1)}(\xi)+E_x^{(+1)}(\xi)+E_x^{(\perp)}(\xi)=I_x$. As required in the [complete-trial source response](native_complete_trial_readout.md), the same unknown $\xi\in C$ governs both prepared root sources $i\in\{0,1\}$, and every erasure $\perp$ remains in the trial ledger and full probability denominator without retry or postselection.

Consider first the two-probe $b\to x$ protocol ($\lambda_a=\lambda_y=0$, $\lambda_b,\lambda_x>0$). Under prepared root bit $i\in\{0,1\}$, the native growth law generates the 60 marked histories $H=(I_x,I_y,b_3,b_4)$ with unchanged conditional probability $P_i(H)=\frac14P_i(I_x,I_y)$ from the [prepared source ledger](native_source_readout_response.md) and [finite interval and source specification](finite_interval_action_and_source_preparation.md); independently of $(i,H)$, transmitter $b$ selects a fair bit $\sigma_b\in\{+1,-1\}$ ($\Pr(\sigma_b=\pm 1)=\frac12$) preparing $|\sigma_b\rangle_{x,b}$, while receiver $x$ starts in $|0\rangle_x$ and the cavity starts in $\Omega_0$. By Table 1 and Eq. (14) of the [all-branch cavity collar source](all_branch_cavity_collar_source_for_four_birth_fields.md), $b\prec_H x\iff I_x=\{a,b\}$ holds exclusively on Rows 12–15 (16 marked histories), where $z_x(\{a,b\})=(2,0)$ lies at the quarter-cavity reference point $x_{\rm ref}=(-L/4,0,0)$ ($\xi_x=0$) and the Neumann wall-image variance vanishes identically ($W_{xx}(\{a,b\})=W_0$). On Rows 1–11 ($I_x\in\{\varnothing,\{a\}\}$, 44 marked histories), $b\not\prec_H x$ and $\sin(0)=0$ in (8). Hence across all 60 marked histories, $\langle Y_x^{\rm det}\rangle_{H,\sigma_b}=-\sigma_b A_{b\to x}\mathbf 1_{\{b\prec_H x\}}$ depends only on the single closed-form cosecant variance $W_0$:
$$
W_0=\frac{\beta}{12}\sum_{m=0}^{5}\csc\!\left(\frac{(2m+1)\pi}{24}\right)>0,\qquad
A_{b\to x}:=e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\sin(2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x)\in(-1,1),
$$
$$
\mathbb P_i(H,\sigma_b,y_{\rm obs};\xi):=\frac{1}{2}P_i(H)\operatorname{Tr}\bigl[\rho_x^{\rm out}(H,\sigma_b)\,E_x^{(y_{\rm obs})}(\xi)\bigr]
=\begin{cases}

\dfrac{1}{4}P_i(H)(1-e)\bigl(1+f-n-k\sigma_b A_{b\to x}\mathbf 1_{\{b\prec_H x\}}\bigr),& y_{\rm obs}=+1,\\[8pt]
\dfrac{1}{2}P_i(H)\,e,& y_{\rm obs}=\perp,
\end{cases}
$$
$$
\sum_{H=1}^{60}\sum_{\sigma_b\in\{+1,-1\}}\sum_{y_{\rm obs}\in\{-1,+1,\perp\}}\mathbb P_i(H,\sigma_b,y_{\rm obs};\xi)=1,\qquad
h_0:=\Pr_0(b\prec x)=\frac{836}{1320}=\frac{19}{30},\qquad

\mu_i(\sigma_b,+1;\xi)=\frac{1-e}{4}\bigl(1+f-n-k\sigma_b h_i A_{b\to x}\bigr),\quad
\mu_i(\sigma_b,\perp;\xi)=\frac{e}{2}.
\tag{11}
$$
Summing $\mathbb P_i(H,\sigma_b,y_{\rm obs};\xi)$ over $y_{\rm obs}\in\{-1,+1,\perp\}$ at each $(H,\sigma_b)$ gives $\frac12P_i(H)[(1-e)+e]=\frac12P_i(H)$, summing over $\sigma_b=\pm 1$ gives $P_i(H)$, and summing over all 60 marked histories gives $\frac{5280}{5280}=1$. Marginalizing over the unobserved history $H$ yields the observable 6-cell setting–outcome probability vector $\mu_i(\xi)$ on $\{+1,-1\}\times\{-1,+1,\perp\}$ in (11) (normalized by $\sum_{\sigma_b,y_{\rm obs}}\mu_i(\sigma_b,y_{\rm obs};\xi)=1$).

**Theorem 3 (Uniform $b\to x$ source discrimination over the faulty detector box and null controls).** *In the unselected 60-history $b\to x$ protocol (11), define the erasure-retaining numerical code $c(-1):=-1$, $c(+1):=+1$, $c(\perp):=0$ as in [supplied probe operator locality](native_probe_operator_locality.md) and the empirical correlator $\widehat{\mathcal S}_{b\to x}^{(i)}(\xi):=-\sum_{\sigma_b\in\{+1,-1\}}\sum_{y_{\rm obs}\in\{-1,+1,\perp\}}\sigma_b c(y_{\rm obs})\mu_i(\sigma_b,y_{\rm obs};\xi)$. Then for every $\xi=(f,n,e)\in C$,*
$$
\widehat{\mathcal S}_{b\to x}^{(i)}(\xi)=(1-e)(1-f-n)h_i\,e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\sin(2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x),\qquad
\widehat{\mathcal S}_{b\to x}^{(0)}(\xi)-\widehat{\mathcal S}_{b\to x}^{(1)}(\xi)=\frac{1}{6}(1-e)(1-f-n)e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\sin(2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x),
$$
$$
\bigl\|\mu_1(\xi)-\mu_0(\xi)\bigr\|_1
=\frac{1}{6}(1-e)(1-f-n)e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\bigl|\sin(2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x)\bigr|,
$$
$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{b\to x}:=\inf_{\xi\in C}\bigl\|\mu_1(\xi)-\mu_0(\xi)\bigr\|_1
=\frac{1}{6}(1-e_+)(1-f_+-n_+)e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\bigl|\sin(2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x)\bigr|>0}
\qquad\bigl(\sin(2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x)\ne 0\bigr),
\tag{12}
$$
*attained at the upper uncertainty corner $\xi_*=(f_+,n_+,e_+)\in C$ (and strictly positive throughout $0<2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x\le\frac{\pi}{2}$). Conversely, under the same complete 60-history denominator $5280$, the source contrast vanishes identically ($\mu_0(\xi)=\mu_1(\xi)$) in each of the explicit null controls: (i) transmitter decoupled ($\lambda_b=0$), (ii) receiver decoupled ($\lambda_x=0$), (iii) half-wave phase aliasing ($2\beta\lambda_b\lambda_x\in\pi\mathbb Z$), (iv) uninformative detector boundary ($f+n=1$, outside $C$), (v) total erasure ($e=1$, outside $C$), and (vi) equal-source preparation under both trial labels.*

*Proof.* From (11), at each $\sigma_b\in\{+1,-1\}$, $\sum_{y_{\rm obs}}c(y_{\rm obs})\mu_i(\sigma_b,y_{\rm obs};\xi)=\mu_i(\sigma_b,+1;\xi)-\mu_i(\sigma_b,-1;\xi)=\frac{1-e}{2}(f-n-k\sigma_b h_i A_{b\to x})$. Multiplying by $-\sigma_b$ and summing over $\sigma_b\in\{+1,-1\}$ cancels the asymmetric bias $(f-n)$ via $\sum_{\sigma_b=\pm 1}\sigma_b(f-n)=0$ and sums $\sigma_b^2=1$ twice to give $\widehat{\mathcal S}_{b\to x}^{(i)}(\xi)=(1-e)kh_i A_{b\to x}$; using $h_0-h_1=\frac{19}{30}-\frac{7}{15}=\frac16$ gives the second identity in (12). Subtracting $\mu_0(\xi)$ from $\mu_1(\xi)$ across all six cells of $\{+1,-1\}\times\{-1,+1,\perp\}$ cancels the erasure cells $\mu_1(\sigma_b,\perp;\xi)-\mu_0(\sigma_b,\perp;\xi)=0$ and leaves four cells $(\sigma_b,\pm 1)$, each of magnitude $\frac14(1-e)k|h_1-h_0|\cdot|A_{b\to x}|=\frac{1}{24}(1-e)(1-f-n)|A_{b\to x}|$, whose sum is $\frac{4}{24}=\frac16$ times $(1-e)(1-f-n)|A_{b\to x}|$. (Coarse-graining to the three-outcome parity observable $R_{\rm par}:=-\sigma_b y_{\rm obs}$ for $y_{\rm obs}=\pm 1$ and $R_{\rm par}:=\perp$ for $y_{\rm obs}=\perp$ yields $\nu_i^{\rm par}(\pm 1;\xi)=\frac{1-e}{2}(1\pm kh_i A_{b\to x})$ and $\nu_i^{\rm par}(\perp;\xi)=e$, preserving $\|\nu_1^{\rm par}(\xi)-\nu_0^{\rm par}(\xi)\|_1=\|\mu_1(\xi)-\mu_0(\xi)\|_1$ with zero $L^1$ loss.) Because $1-e\ge 1-e_+>0$ and $1-f-n\ge 1-f_+-n_+>0$ decrease monotonically in $e,f,n$ on the product box $C$, their product attains its strict positive minimum on $C$ at $\xi_*=(f_+,n_+,e_+)\in C$, proving (12). Direct substitution into (11) verifies $\mu_0(\xi)=\mu_1(\xi)$ in each null control (i)–(vi). $\square$

Compare (12) now with the two-probe $a\to x$ protocol ($\lambda_b=\lambda_y=0$, $\lambda_a,\lambda_x>0$, fair $\sigma_a\in\{+1,-1\}$, and the same shared detector box $C$). Across the three stage-3 blocks $I_x\in\{\varnothing,\{a\},\{a,b\}\}$, the native continuation weights $c=\frac{264}{1320}=\frac15$, $(f_0,h_0)=(\frac{220}{1320},\frac{836}{1320})=(\frac16,\frac{19}{30})$, and $(f_1,h_1)=(\frac{440}{1320},\frac{616}{1320})=(\frac13,\frac{7}{15})$ have **identical** causal-precedence probabilities $\Pr_0(a\prec x)=f_0+h_0=\frac45=\Pr_1(a\prec x)=f_1+h_1$. On a spatially homogeneous wavefield without boundaries (such as the $1+1$D cylinder $\mathbb R\times S_L^1$ of the [all-branch $1+1$D wavefield extension](spacelike_branch_extension_for_four_birth_fields.md), where $W_{xx}^{\rm cyl}$ is independent of $I_x$), $a\to x$ signaling is therefore strictly blind to the root preparation $P_0$ vs. $P_1$. **Theorem 4 (Strict negativity of the Neumann boundary-image variance shift and $a\to x$ source contrast).** *On the fork block $I_x=\{a\}$ (Rows 7–11, 20 marked histories), the receiver vacuum variance $W_{xx}(\{a\})=\omega_{\rm vac}(\phi_x(\{a\})^2)$ satisfies the exact paired cosecant identity and strict inequalities (proved purely from first-principles convexity without numerical quadrature)*
$$
\Delta W_x^{\rm img}:=W_{xx}(\{a\})-W_0
=-\frac{\beta}{2}\sum_{m=0}^{5}(-1)^m(c_m+c_{11-m})\sin\!\left(\frac{(2m+1)\pi}{6}\right)
=-\frac{\beta}{12}\sum_{m=0}^{5}(-1)^m\csc\!\left(\frac{(2m+1)\pi}{24}\right)\sin\!\left(\frac{(2m+1)\pi}{6}\right),
$$
$$
\boxed{0<W_{xx}(\{a\})<W_0=W_{xx}(\{a,b\}).}
\tag{13}
$$
*Consequently, in the unselected 60-history $a\to x$ protocol ($\lambda_b=\lambda_y=0$) under the shared detector box $C$, although $\Pr_0(a\prec x)=\Pr_1(a\prec x)=\frac45$, the prepared-source signaling contrast is strictly nonzero whenever $\lambda_x\ne 0$ and $\sin(2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x)\ne 0$, with reversed sign ($\widehat{\mathcal S}_{a\to x}^{(1)}(\xi)>\widehat{\mathcal S}_{a\to x}^{(0)}(\xi)$ for $0<2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x<\pi$) and uniform $L^1$ box infimum*
$$
\widehat{\mathcal S}_{a\to x}^{(1)}(\xi)-\widehat{\mathcal S}_{a\to x}^{(0)}(\xi)
=\frac{1}{6}(1-e)(1-f-n)\Bigl(e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_{xx}(\{a\})}-e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\Bigr)\sin(2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x),
$$
$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{a\to x}:=\inf_{\xi\in C}\bigl\|\mu_1^{(a\to x)}(\xi)-\mu_0^{(a\to x)}(\xi)\bigr\|_1
=\frac{1}{6}(1-e_+)(1-f_+-n_+)\Bigl(e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_{xx}(\{a\})}-e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\Bigr)\bigl|\sin(2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x)\bigr|>0.}
\tag{14}
$$

*Proof.* Strict positivity $W_{xx}(\{a\})>0$ holds by Theorem 2 of the [all-branch cavity collar source](all_branch_cavity_collar_source_for_four_birth_fields.md) (and directly since $W_{xx}(\{a\})=\beta\sum_{m=0}^{11}c_m\cos^2\alpha_{m,x}(\{a\})$ with $c_0>0$ and $\alpha_{0,x}(\{a\})=8\tilde\varphi_0\implies\cos^2(8\varphi_0)=\cos^2(\pi/3)=\frac14>0$). At $z_x(\{a\})=(1,2/3)$, $\alpha_{m,x}=(6+3\xi_x)\tilde\varphi_m=8(2\pi-\varphi_m)$, so $\cos^2\alpha_{m,x}=\cos^2(8\varphi_m)=\frac12(1+\cos(16\varphi_m))$. Since $12\varphi_m=m\pi+\frac{\pi}{2}$, we have $\cos(16\varphi_m)=\cos(m\pi+\frac{\pi}{2}+4\varphi_m)=-(-1)^m\sin(4\varphi_m)$, whence $W_{xx}(\{a\})=\frac{\beta}{2}\sum_{m=0}^{11}c_m-\frac{\beta}{2}\sum_{m=0}^{11}(-1)^m c_m\sin(4\varphi_m)=W_0-\frac{\beta}{2}\sum_{m=0}^{11}(-1)^m c_m\sin(4\varphi_m)$. Pairing $m\in\{0,\ldots,5\}$ with $11-m\in\{6,\ldots,11\}$ uses $\varphi_{11-m}=\pi-\varphi_m\implies\sin(4\varphi_{11-m})=\sin(4\pi-4\varphi_m)=-\sin(4\varphi_m)$ and $(-1)^{11-m}=-(-1)^m$, whose product is $+(-1)^m\sin(4\varphi_m)$ with $4\varphi_m=\frac{(2m+1)\pi}{6}$ and $c_m+c_{11-m}=\frac{1}{12}(\cot\frac{\varphi_m}{2}+\tan\frac{\varphi_m}{2})=\frac16\csc\varphi_m$, establishing the paired formula in (13).

To prove $\Delta W_x^{\rm img}<0$ analytically, evaluate $(-1)^m\sin\bigl(\frac{(2m+1)\pi}{6}\bigr)$ explicitly at $m=0,1,2,3,4,5$:
- For $m=0,1,2$: $(+1)\sin\frac{\pi}{6}=+\frac12$, $(-1)\sin\frac{3\pi}{6}=-1$, $(+1)\sin\frac{5\pi}{6}=+\frac12$.
- For $m=3,4,5$: $(-1)\sin\frac{7\pi}{6}=+\frac12$, $(+1)\sin\frac{9\pi}{6}=-1$, $(-1)\sin\frac{11\pi}{6}=+\frac12$.

Thus $\sum_{m=0}^{5}(-1)^m\csc\varphi_m\sin\bigl(\frac{(2m+1)\pi}{6}\bigr)=\Delta_2(\varphi_1)+\Delta_2(\varphi_4)$, where $\Delta_2(\theta):=\frac12\csc(\theta-h)-\csc\theta+\frac12\csc(\theta+h)$ with step $h:=\frac{\pi}{12}>0$ evaluated at $\varphi_1=\frac{3\pi}{24}$ ($\varphi_1\mp h=\frac{\pi}{24},\frac{5\pi}{24}$) and $\varphi_4=\frac{9\pi}{24}$ ($\varphi_4\mp h=\frac{7\pi}{24},\frac{11\pi}{24}$), all lying strictly inside $(0,\frac{\pi}{2})$. On $(0,\frac{\pi}{2})$, $f(\theta):=\csc\theta>0$ is smooth with $f'(\theta)=-\csc\theta\cot\theta$ and $f''(\theta)=\csc\theta(\cot^2\theta+\csc^2\theta)>0$. Integrating by parts twice on $[0,h]$ gives the exact second-difference integral identity $\Delta_2(\theta)=\frac12\int_0^h(h-t)\bigl[f''(\theta-t)+f''(\theta+t)\bigr]dt>0$ for both $\theta=\varphi_1$ and $\theta=\varphi_4$. Multiplying by $-\frac{\beta}{12}<0$ proves $\Delta W_x^{\rm img}=-\frac{\beta}{12}(\Delta_2(\varphi_1)+\Delta_2(\varphi_4))<0$ and hence (13).

Finally, in the $a\to x$ protocol ($\lambda_b=\lambda_y=0$), (8) gives $\langle Y_x^{\rm det}\rangle_{H,\sigma_a}=0$ on $I_x=\varnothing$, $-\sigma_a e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_{xx}(\{a\})}\sin(2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x)$ on $I_x=\{a\}$, and $-\sigma_a e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}\sin(2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x)$ on $I_x=\{a,b\}$. Replacing $h_i A_{b\to x}$ in (11) by $\overline{A}_{a\to x}^{(i)}:=[f_i e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_{xx}(\{a\})}+h_i e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}]\sin(2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x)$ and using $f_1-f_0=\frac13-\frac16=+\frac16$ and $h_1-h_0=\frac{7}{15}-\frac{19}{30}=-\frac16$ gives $\overline{A}_{a\to x}^{(1)}-\overline{A}_{a\to x}^{(0)}=\frac16(e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_{xx}(\{a\})}-e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0})\sin(2\beta\lambda_a\lambda_x)$, with $e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_{xx}(\{a\})}>e^{-2\lambda_x^2 W_0}$ for every $\lambda_x\ne 0$ by (13). Repeating the 6-cell $L^1$ sum and box minimization of Theorem 3 establishes (14). $\square$

## 4. Non-perturbative switching-work ledger and ground-state passivity

Because the impulsive probe–cavity couplings in (3) are switched on and off at prescribed times $t_r(H)$ by an external control schedule, total energy conservation requires accounting for the external switching work $W_{\rm switch}$ exchanged with the joint autonomous Hamiltonian $H_0$ in (2). We first establish global ground-state passivity of the unshifted cavity–probe ground state $\Psi_{\rm gs}$.

**Proposition 1 (Global ground-state passivity of $\Psi_{\rm gs}$).** *On $\operatorname{Dom}(H_0)=\mathcal H_{\rm probes}\otimes\operatorname{Dom}(\overline K)$, the unperturbed Hamiltonian $H_0$ satisfies $H_0-E_0 I_{\rm tot}\succeq 0$ with one-dimensional kernel $\ker(H_0-E_0 I_{\rm tot})=\mathbb C\Psi_{\rm gs}$. Consequently, for every unitary operator $U$ on $\mathcal H_{\rm tot}$ (setting $W_{\rm switch}(U):=+\infty$ if $U\Psi_{\rm gs}\notin\operatorname{Dom}((H_0-E_0 I_{\rm tot})^{1/2})$),*
$$
\boxed{W_{\rm switch}(U):=\bigl\langle U\Psi_{\rm gs},H_0 U\Psi_{\rm gs}\bigr\rangle-E_0
=\bigl\|(H_0-E_0 I_{\rm tot})^{1/2}U\Psi_{\rm gs}\bigr\|^2\ge 0,}
\qquad
W_{\rm switch}(U)=0\iff U\Psi_{\rm gs}=e^{i\gamma}\Psi_{\rm gs}\quad(\gamma\in\mathbb R).
\tag{15}
$$
*In particular, zero work can ever be extracted from the unshifted 12-mode cavity zero-point energy $E_0=\frac{27\pi A_*}{\tau}>0$ by any single- or multi-probe unitary operation.*

*Proof.* On $\operatorname{Dom}(\overline K)$, the 12-fold Hermite eigenbasis $\{\psi_{\boldsymbol\ell}:\boldsymbol\ell\in\mathbb Z_{\ge 0}^{12}\}$ diagonalizes $\overline{H_{\rm sc}}-E_0 I_{\rm field}=\frac{A_*}{\tau}\sum_{m=0}^{11}\theta_m\ell_m$ with strictly positive mode gaps $\frac{A_*}{\tau}\theta_m\ge\frac{25\pi A_*}{8\tau}>0$, so $\overline{H_{\rm sc}}-E_0 I_{\rm field}\succeq 0$ with kernel $\mathbb C\Omega_0$. On $\mathcal H_{\rm probes}$, $\sum_{r\in\{a,b,x,y\}}H_{{\rm det},r}=\varepsilon_{\rm det}\sum_{r}|1\rangle\langle 1|_r\succeq 0$ with $\varepsilon_{\rm det}>0$ has kernel $\mathbb C|0\rangle^{\otimes 4}$. Hence $H_0-E_0 I_{\rm tot}=I_{\rm probes}\otimes(\overline{H_{\rm sc}}-E_0 I_{\rm field})+\sum_r H_{{\rm det},r}\otimes I_{\rm field}\succeq 0$ has $\ker(H_0-E_0 I_{\rm tot})=\mathbb C\Psi_{\rm gs}$, and spectral functional calculus gives (15) for every unitary $U$ ($\|U\Psi_{\rm gs}\|=1$). $\square$

Next we compute the exact coherent-state cavity energy shift on $\mathcal S(\mathbb R^{12})$ and close the pathwise and ensemble switching-work balance. For any real vector $g=(g_a,g_b,g_x,g_y)^{\mathsf T}\in\mathbb R^4$ on history $H$, substituting $\phi_r(H)=\sum_{m=0}^{11}\sqrt{2\beta c_m}\,u_m(x_r(H))X_m(t_r(H))$ from (1) into $\Phi_H(g)=\sum_r g_r\phi_r(H)$ yields $\Phi_H(g)=\sum_{m=0}^{11}(\alpha_m(H;g)Q_m+\gamma_m(H;g)P_m)$ on $\mathcal S(\mathbb R^{12})$, where $\alpha_m(H;g)+i\gamma_m(H;g)=\sqrt{2\beta c_m}\sum_{r\in\{a,b,x,y\}}g_r\cos\alpha_{m,r}(H)\,e^{i\omega_m t_r(H)}$. By Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff on each canonical pair ($[-i\alpha_m Q_m,-i\gamma_m P_m]=-i\alpha_m\gamma_m I$), the Weyl unitary $\mathcal W_H(-g)=\bigotimes_{m=0}^{11}\exp(-i\alpha_m Q_m-i\gamma_m P_m)$ acts in Schrödinger coordinates $\mathsf q\in\mathbb R^{12}$ by $(\mathcal W_H(-g)\psi)(\mathsf q)=\bigl(\prod_{m=0}^{11}e^{\frac{i}{2}\alpha_m\gamma_m-i\alpha_m\mathsf q_m}\bigr)\psi(\mathsf q-\boldsymbol\gamma)$. Thus $\mathcal W_H(-g)$ maps $\mathcal S(\mathbb R^{12})$ bijectively onto $\mathcal S(\mathbb R^{12})\subset\operatorname{Dom}(\overline{H_{\rm sc}})$ for every $g\in\mathbb R^4$, shifting $\mathcal W_H(+g)Q_m\mathcal W_H(-g)=Q_m+\gamma_m I$ and $\mathcal W_H(+g)P_m\mathcal W_H(-g)=P_m-\alpha_m I$ on $\mathcal S(\mathbb R^{12})$.
