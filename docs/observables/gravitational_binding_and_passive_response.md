# What a compensated binding contrast identifies about passive response

**Result.** Two of the three carriers have equal nongravitational stored energy and different gravitational binding. Two have equal binding and different stored energy. Under a matched material and support ledger, those two comparisons isolate the passive responses to binding and storage, respectively. The complete lift data can give a joint set of coefficients or determine both exactly. A worked record fixes $(\gamma_U,\gamma_B)=(1,3/2)$. The identical record, including its independent binding calibration, admits $(1,1)$ when the compensated carrier stores its energy in an unregistered component with a different passive response.

Both profiles have the same exterior Newtonian field and diametral moment of the layers. Those two measurements alone allow an interval of $B$. The profile selection and $d=g_w\Delta>0$ are **independent preparation calibrations** in this study. No profile or calorimetry row is added to the fifteen settings.

**Change the internal material registration.** Keep precisely the same total internal energies $U=(0,\Delta,\Delta)$, shells, $d$, inertial law, baselines and apparatus. Let $V_i$ be the part of $U_i$ stored in a second component, with $0\le V_i\le U_i$. The passive numerator relative to baseline becomes
$$
 \gamma_U\delta U_i-\gamma_B\delta B_i+(\gamma_V-\gamma_U)(V_i-V_0).
$$
Set $\gamma_U=\gamma_B=1,\ \gamma_V=1/2,\ (V_0,V_1,V_2)=(0,\Delta,0)$. This split meets both bounds in every preparation. In carrier 1 the ordinary-store increment is zero and the second component gains $\Delta$, giving $c^2(P_1-P_0)=\Delta/2-\Delta=-\Delta/2$. Carrier 2 still gives $c^2(P_2-P_0)=\Delta$. The two worlds therefore have identical $M,P$, native histories, spring ratios, lift clocks, signed legs, blank readings, stroke/echo data, store levels and support zeros. Nothing changes in the independently calibrated $d$. Both nulls hold for the underlying ordinary-store and binding coefficients in this world.

For a general split the two values inferred under a homogeneous-store interpretation satisfy
$$
 \widehat\gamma_U=\gamma_U+(\gamma_V-\gamma_U)(V_2-V_0)/\Delta,\qquad
 \widehat\gamma_B=\gamma_B+(\gamma_V-\gamma_U)(V_2-V_1)/\Delta.
$$
The second equation specifies the premise for an exact numerical attribution to binding. The differential passive contribution of the unregistered material between carriers 2 and 1 must vanish or be independently quantified and corrected. Equal total $U_1=U_2$ alone cannot establish that. An independent bound on the differential contribution can still exclude a proposed binding coefficient if every allowed correction excludes it. Matching the component partitions or establishing the same passive response for those components suffices. Other support, fixture, stress, thermal and composition changes enter through the same requirement to match or account for their differential contributions at the retained order.
