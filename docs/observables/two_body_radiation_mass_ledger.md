# Rest masses during a finite radiation exchange

Classical result CL063 supplies a reference for checking mass accounting at two interaction events. In one spatial dimension, two bodies of rest mass $M>0$ begin at rest a distance $L>0$ apart. The left body emits a rightward null packet of energy $Mc^2/4$. The right body absorbs it at $t=L/c$. Assume special relativity, exact local four-momentum conservation, inertial motion between the vertices and available internal states of the required masses.

Express body records as $(E/(Mc^2),p/(Mc),\mu/M)$, where $\mu$ is the individual rest mass. The table uses the initial rest frame.

| Stage | Source | Receiver | Packet energy and momentum in the same units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before emission | $(1,0,1)$ | $(1,0,1)$ | Absent |
| In flight | $(3/4,-1/4,1/\sqrt{2})$ | $(1,0,1)$ | $(1/4,1/4)$ |
| After absorption | $(3/4,-1/4,1/\sqrt{2})$ | $(5/4,1/4,\sqrt{3/2})$ | Absent |

Local conservation fixes each outgoing energy and signed momentum. The mass shell $\mu^2c^4=E^2-c^2p^2$ then fixes the positive masses. Requiring either body to retain its initial rest mass fails at its own vertex. The source recoils at $-c/3$ and the receiver leaves the absorption event at $+c/5$.

Include the packet while it is in flight and assign its four-momentum to the receiver after absorption. The complete four-momentum is $(2Mc,0)$ on every cut. The system invariant mass is therefore $2M$, while the final individual rest masses sum to $M(1+\sqrt{3})/\sqrt{2}$. Their deficit from $2M$, multiplied by $c^2$, equals the final recoil kinetic energy in this frame.

The mass ratios follow from the specified packet energy and the supplied relativistic laws. The input mass $M$ sets the scale.
