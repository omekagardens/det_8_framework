## The precise stability input and finite-interval argument

Its following regularity remark supplies smooth persistence. Section 2.2, pp. 276–277, exposes the local constants and coefficient-dependence issue. We use $k=6$, far above the required threshold. For the compact-manifold formulation, the corroborating source is Gasperín and Valiente Kroon, *Spinorial Wave Equations and Stability of the Milne Spacetime*, [arXiv:1407.3317v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.3317v1), Appendix E, pp. 34–35, equation (68) and Theorem 2. ## A registered normal-launch clock reaches proper $4\tau$

Coordinate duration and proper duration require separate arguments. Register the same initial probe point, $y_0=0$, and launch a future-directed timelike geodesic in the initial unit-normal direction. Let $r=\sigma/\tau$, where $\sigma$ is its proper time in seconds. Its dimensionless position $z(r)=(s(r),y(r))$ and velocity $V=dz/dr$ satisfy
$$
 \bar g(V,V)=-1,\qquad z(0)=(0,0),\qquad V(0)=e_0,\qquad
 \frac{dV^a}{dr}=-\Gamma^a_{bc}V^bV^c .
 \tag{16}
$$
The normalization follows from $d\mathcal L^2=-c^2d\sigma^2$ and $\ell=c\tau$; there is no missing factor of $c$. This is an initially normal geodesic, not an assumption that it stays normal to the wave-map slices or fixed at a spatial coordinate.

Because of uniform $C^1$ metric convergence, shrink the same positive threshold until, on the entire coordinate slab,
$$
 |\Gamma(z)(W,W)|_{\mathrm E}\le\frac1{100}|W|_{\mathrm E}^2
 \quad\text{for every }W .
 \tag{17}
$$
This Euclidean component norm uses the global flat affine frame of the product torus. It is a convenient sufficient neighborhood condition, not a measured or numerically certified bound on $\eta$.

Bootstrap $|V-e_0|_{\mathrm E}\le1/8$ up to proper parameter $r=4$. Then $|V|_{\mathrm E}\le9/8$, and (16)–(17) give
$$
 |V(r)-e_0|_{\mathrm E}
 \le\frac4{100}\left(\frac98\right)^2
 =\frac{81}{1600}<\frac18.
 \tag{18}
$$
Also
$$
 \frac78r\le s(r)\le\frac98r,\qquad
 s(4)\le\frac92<5 .
 \tag{19}
$$
A first exit of the velocity bootstrap or the coordinate slab before $r=4$ is impossible. Compactness of the spatial torus, bounded velocity and smooth coefficients give ordinary differential-equation continuation to $r=4$. Metric compatibility preserves $\bar g(V,V)=-1$, and $V^0\ge7/8$ preserves future orientation. The entire proper duration $4\tau$ is therefore contained in the proved coordinate development, with a strict endpoint buffer.

This construction establishes the existence of the geometrically registered events with proper parameters $0,1,2,3,4$, when desired. It does not compute their observables. The initial point and normal direction are part of the registration; observers with arbitrary $\eta$-dependent boosts are not included in this clock estimate.

The same argument gives only approximate comparison. Equation (13) makes the connection norm tend to zero, so the geodesic position and velocity converge uniformly on $0\le r\le4$ to $z^0(r)=(r,0)$, $V^0=e_0$. Combining this with the field norm in (13) gives convergence of the four scalar coefficients sampled along these registered curves, and of fixed finite covariance expressions built from them.
