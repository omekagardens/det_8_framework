## 2. Promotion Criteria (General)

For any term to be promoted from M to P (or higher):

1. **Operational definition:** The term must be linked to a specific, measurable record-side quantity.
2. **Causal interface:** If the term describes a mechanism, it must specify how it couples to the record (what it changes, under what conditions).
3. **Pre-registered falsifier:** A null model, test statistic, threshold, and failure condition must be declared before data collection.
4. **Distinctiveness:** The term must produce predictions that differ from a model without it.
5. **No smuggling:** The term must not be a renamed version of an already-excluded concept (e.g., "intention field" for agency).

---

## 3. Anti-Inflation Rule

The metaphysics ledger grows only by explicit decision. Adding a new M-term requires:
- A written proposal.
- Demonstration that the term captures something not already in the ledger.
- A commitment to work toward promotion criteria or to accept permanent M status.

This prevents the ledger from becoming a dumping ground for unfalsifiable claims.
