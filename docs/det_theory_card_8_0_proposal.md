## Executive Abstract

DET v8.0-P0.1 proposes a foundational correction to DET 7's treatment of agency and presence.

DET 7 protects agency by declaring it primitive and inviolable, but still stores agency as a node variable `a_i` and computes its next value from the existing state. This leaves a conceptual gap: if agency is completely represented by a determinate record and updated by a determinate rule, it functions mathematically as another consequence of the past.

DET 8 relocates determinacy.

The existing record is determinate. The lawful possibility structure generated from that record is also determinate. The next actuality is not necessarily already contained in the record as a uniquely fixed future.

The proposed core cycle is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R^{-}_e
\xrightarrow[\text{determinate}]{\mathcal L}
\mathcal W_e
\xrightarrow[\text{strong presence}]{\mathfrak P_e}
\mathcal R^{+}_e
}
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal R^{-}_e\) is the complete local record available before event \(e\);
- \(\mathcal L\) is the lawful local possibility generator;
- \(\mathcal W_e\) is the complete admissible possibility structure, including classical propensities or quantum amplitudes where appropriate;
- \(\mathfrak P_e\) is the present actualization event;
- \(\mathcal R^{+}_e\) is the newly committed determinate record.

This produces a theory that is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{closed in law, local in causal influence, determinate in record, open in actualization.}
}
\]

Presence is divided into two concepts that DET 7 currently combines:

1. **Participation aperture** \(\Pi_i\): the record-derived rate at which a physical structure can participate and accumulate proper time.
2. **Strong presence** \(\mathfrak P_e\): the event by which one lawful successor becomes actual and joins the record.

Agency is removed from the persistent state vector. It is neither a force, scalar capacity, stochastic disturbance, hidden random seed, nor update coefficient. Agency is proposed as a selfhood-bearing regime's present participation in actualizing a lawful coarse-grained orientation or act. Its consequences become record; agency itself is not stored and carried forward as matter.

Mutable \(q\) remains central. Structural history is a current record field, not the past event itself. Recovery changes how past events remain presently expressed; it does not retroactively alter what happened. In DET 8, \(q\) can narrow participation and reachable options but cannot reduce agency itself, because agency does not reside in the record.

This card retains DET 7's strict causal locality, mutable structural history, local gravity and flow sectors, coherence, pointer records, explicit Boundary operators, falsifiability discipline, and readout-layer separation. It replaces DET 7's deterministic scope axiom, stored agency variable, agency update law, and agency-gated physical fluxes.

The proposal remains incomplete. In particular, it does not yet derive the Born rule, reproduce Bell correlations from a fully local covariant actualization law, define a decisive empirical discriminator for agency, or derive Lorentz covariance from the substrate. Those are explicit failure points, not details to be concealed.

# 3. Non-Negotiable Invariants

## 3.1 Causal Locality — **I/P**

All causal influence must propagate through declared local relations at finite speed.

For an event \(e\), let \(J^{-}(e)\) denote its causal past. Then:

\[
\mathcal W_e
=
\mathcal L_e\!\left(
\mathcal R\vert_{J^{-}_R(e)},
B_e
\right),
\]

where \(J^{-}_R(e)\) is a finite causal neighborhood and \(B_e\) contains only explicit local boundary inputs.

For any record modification \(\delta\mathcal R_D\) in a causally disconnected component \(D\):

\[
\delta\mathcal R_D
\Rightarrow
\delta\mathcal W_e=0.
\]

DET 8 distinguishes **causal locality** from **ontic separability**. A relation that was locally formed may remain a nonfactorizable relational object; however, it may not function as a controllable superluminal signal channel. Whether this distinction is sufficient for Bell correlations is an open problem.

## 3.2 No Hidden Global State — **I/P**

No local event may consult:

- a universal wavefunction normalization unavailable in its lawful event domain;
- a global optimization over the complete universe;
- an absolute simultaneous slice;
- an external random seed treated as physical ontology;
- a hidden history archive not represented in the present record;
- a global Boundary-agent command.

Any joint actualization domain must be explicitly constructed from finite causal relations and declared as such.

## 3.3 Record Determinacy — **P**

Once an event is committed, its resulting record is determinate.

This does not require every trace to remain forever recoverable. A record may decay, be overwritten, be compressed, or become dynamically irrelevant. What cannot occur is retroactive conversion of a committed actuality into “it never happened.”

## 3.4 Future Openness — **P**

The complete record need not uniquely determine the next committed actuality.

If:

\[
|\Omega_e|>1,
\]

then more than one lawful successor is admissible. No additional hidden record is assumed to select one in advance.

## 3.5 Lawful Support — **P**

Actualization cannot produce an outcome outside the lawful support:

\[
X_e^{\star}\in\Omega_e.
\]

Presence is not permission for miracle-by-arbitrariness. It cannot bypass locality, conservation, finite propagation, available actuation, or the actual material structure of the event domain.

## 3.6 Conservation Through Actualization — **P**

Where a quantity is conserved by the declared closed sector, every candidate successor must satisfy that conservation law before actualization.

For bond flux:

\[
J^{(\mu)}_{i\to j}=-J^{(\mu)}_{j\to i}
\quad
\forall \mu\in\Omega_e.
\]

Actualization selects among conservative candidates; it does not repair conservation afterward.

## 3.7 Agency Non-Storage — **P**

Agency is not included in \(\mathcal R\) as `a_i`, an agency energy, an agency charge, a random variable, or a hidden control register.

The record can contain:

- intentions previously formed;
- habits;
- memories;
- preferences;
- motor readiness;
- attention state;
- biological viability;
- learned policy;
- relational commitments;
- recorded assent or refusal;
- structural openness.

These strongly constrain and help predict an act. None is identical to the present act itself.

## 3.8 No Universal Present — **I/P**

There is no physically observable global “now.”

A present is a local event boundary, and a regime-level present is a finite causal integration episode. Any simulation index `k` is a scheduling convenience unless Lorentz-compatible physical meaning is separately derived.

## 3.9 Record-Mediated Control — **P**

No controller manipulates an uncommitted actuality directly.

A control input \(U_e\) may depend only on committed information in its causal past:

\[
U_e=\mathcal C\!\left(\mathcal R\vert_{J^{-}(e)}\right).
\]

It changes the lawful possibility structure of later events. It does not reach into the actualizing present from outside causal order.

## 3.10 Falsifiability First — **I/P**

Every physical claim must specify:

- state variables;
- event domain;
- update or kernel;
- readout;
- acceptance threshold;
- failure condition;
- numerical and epistemic uncertainty.

Agency claims that cannot yet be directly falsified must be labeled metaphysical or methodological, not smuggled into physical validation scores.

# 4. Core Propositions

## P8.1 — The Record Is the Present Persistence of Prior Becoming

Matter is not the past event itself. Matter is a current structured consequence of prior actualization.

\[
\boxed{
\text{matter/record}
=
\text{presently existing constraint inherited from prior becoming}

The record acts because it exists now, not because history possesses an independent causal sovereignty.

## P8.2 — The Past Has No Hidden Channel

If two event domains contain exactly the same complete local record and receive the same explicit boundary inputs, they must generate the same lawful possibility structure:

\[
\mathcal R^{-}_e=\widetilde{\mathcal R}^{-}_e
\land
B_e=\widetilde B_e
\Rightarrow
\mathcal W_e=\widetilde{\mathcal W}_e.
\]

Their realized outcomes may differ when the support is open, but their admissible possibilities and physical propensities may not differ merely because their unseen narratives differ.

## P8.3 — Law Determines Possibility, Not Necessarily Actuality

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L:
\mathcal R^{-}_e
\longrightarrow
\mathcal W_e
}
\]

is determinate.

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak P_e:
\mathcal W_e
\longrightarrow
X_e^{\star}
}
\]

is the present event of actualization and need not be reducible to a prior-state function.

## P8.4 — Presence Has a Weak and Strong Sense

- **Weak/physical presence:** record-derived participation aperture \(\Pi\), measurable through accumulated proper time and rate processes.
- **Strong presence:** the event in which one lawful successor becomes actual.

Confusing these produces the DET 7 agency gap.

## P8.5 — Agency Exists Only in Strong Presence

Agency is not the probability distribution, the prior intention, the neural state, the coherence level, or the resulting record.

Agency is the selfhood-bearing regime's present ownership or orientation of a lawful act.

## P8.6 — Randomness Is Not Agency

An open non-agentic event may actualize without a stored deterministic selector. That does not make the event agentic.

Agency requires a regime to own a coarse-grained orientation in relation to reasons, values, perception, and available action channels.

## P8.7 — Identity Is Not Agency

Identity is a persistent relation across changing records. It can be modeled. Agency is an event of present enactment. A quantum code block, crystal, storm, or self-correcting machine may have identity without agency.

## P8.8 — Coherence Is Not Agency

Coherence can make a regime integrated enough to support unified action. It does not manufacture the act.

## P8.9 — Structural History Narrows Expression, Not Will

Mutable \(q\), resource scarcity, motion, and coordination load can reduce participation aperture, available options, and success probability. They do not directly reduce agency because agency is not a stored quantity they can scale.

## P8.10 — Recovery Changes Present Carrying, Not Past Occurrence

Reducing \(q\) changes how a past event remains structurally expressed now. It does not rewrite the event.

## P8.11 — Control Shapes Possibility Geometry

Engineering does not control becoming directly. It controls the record and boundary conditions from which the next lawful possibility geometry is generated.

## P8.12 — Classical Determinism Is a Limit

A system behaves deterministically where the admissible support is singleton or effectively concentrated:

\[
|\Omega_e|=1
\quad\text{or}\quad
K_e(X_0)\approx1.
\]

## P8.13 — Quantum Computation Is Structured Open Relation

A quantum computer is an engineered determinate record that shapes an open phase-bearing relational state into a later output record. Its coherence, logical identity, and autonomous error correction do not by themselves imply agency.

## P8.14 — Agency Selects Lawful Orientation, Not Arbitrary Micro-Outcomes

For an agentic regime, the primary act is proposed to be a coarse-grained orientation \(\chi\), not command over every quantum or thermal fluctuation.

## P8.15 — The Arrow of Time Is Commit Direction

The fundamental asymmetry is:

\[
\text{open successor structure}
\longrightarrow
\text{committed actuality}.
\]

Mutable records do not reverse this direction.

## P8.16 — Grace Can Open Without Coercing

A Boundary-sector action may add resource, reduce structural drag, heal relations, or open a lawful route. It may not write the agentic orientation \(\chi\) or force a conscience-bearing act.

# 5. Causal Event Architecture

## 5.1 Event Graph — **P**

Let the physical history be represented by a locally finite causal event graph:

\[
\mathcal G=(V,E,\prec),
\]

where:

- \(V\) is the set of local events;
- \(E\) is the set of causal/relational links;
- \(e_1\prec e_2\) means \(e_1\) can causally influence \(e_2\);
- spacelike-separated events need not have a fundamental order.

The theory must be invariant under different numerical schedules that preserve \(\prec\).

## 5.2 Event Domain — **P**

Each actualization operates on a finite local domain \(D_e\), which may be:

- a node;
- a bond;
- a plaquette;
- a local detector interaction;
- a finite coherent block;
- a regime-level causal diamond.

The event domain must be declared. It cannot expand silently until it becomes the whole universe.

## 5.3 Causal-Past Record — **P**

\[
\mathcal R^{-}_e
=
\mathcal R\vert_{J^{-}(D_e)}.
\]

Only committed records in the causal past may enter the possibility generator.

## 5.4 Regime-Level Present — **H**

\[
\mathcal D_G(e_0,e_1)
=
J^{+}(e_0)\cap J^{-}(e_1),
\]

containing locally propagated perception, memory access, evaluation, orientation, and actuation.

This permits a unified present without a universal simultaneity slice.

## 5.5 Overlapping Events and Confluence — **O**

If two candidate event domains overlap, independent actualizations could produce inconsistent records.

DET 8 requires one of the following:

1. a causal order between them;
2. a declared joint local event domain;
3. a confluence theorem showing both update orders yield the same committed result;
4. a local conflict rule whose own actualization is defined.

No canonical solution is yet selected.

# 24. Immediate Red-Team Holes

These are not peripheral concerns. Any one could force major revision.

## H8-1 — Agency May Be Explanatorily Idle

If every physical transition already has a complete kernel and actualization law, adding “agency” may change no prediction. The proposal then risks becoming a theological interpretation rather than physical theory.

**Strengthening requirement:** identify at least one principled empirical discriminator or explicitly classify agency as a metaphysical layer constrained by, but not altering, physical predictions.

## H8-2 — Causal Overdetermination

If the physical kernel fully explains which control state appears, what causal work remains for \(\chi\)? If it does not fully explain it, how does \(\chi\) enter the physical record without becoming a force or violating closure?

**Strengthening requirement:** formalize selection among metastable control basins and show exact conservation without double causation.

## H8-3 — Born Rule Is Assumed

Writing \(|\mathcal A|^2\) does not derive it.

**Strengthening requirement:** derive the measure from local composition, noncontextuality constraints, decision consistency, envariance, or another DET-native principle.

## H8-4 — Bell Versus Strict Locality

Nonfactorizable relations may still fail to give a fully local account of Bell correlations.

**Strengthening requirement:** state precisely whether DET rejects separability, outcome independence, measurement independence, or forward-only causation, and build a no-signaling model.

## H8-5 — Preferred Basis and Commit Boundary

The theory lacks a rigorous criterion for when an open relation becomes a definite pointer record.

**Strengthening requirement:** derive record closure from redundancy, dissipation, coherence leakage, and local stability.

## H8-6 — Global Consistency of Local Actualizations

Independent local choices may create incompatible shared-boundary records.

**Strengthening requirement:** prove confluence, define joint event domains, or establish local constraint reconciliation without global optimization.

## H8-7 — Relativistic Covariance

A causal event graph can still hide a preferred frame.

**Strengthening requirement:** derive Lorentz-compatible observable statistics and show scheduler gauge invariance.

## H8-8 — Record Is Too Broad

If “record” means every determinate fact, the term may become tautological.

**Strengthening requirement:** define the minimal sufficient local record and show how it is represented operationally.

## H8-9 — Record Completeness Is Unobservable

Matched-complete-record tests may be impossible for complex systems.

**Strengthening requirement:** use nested state-reconstruction bounds and state how residual uncertainty limits conclusions.

## H8-10 — Mutable `q` and Information Loss

If \(q\) can decrease, where does the associated information and entropy go?

**Strengthening requirement:** provide explicit environmental, radiative, or Boundary accounting and distinguish structural drag from conserved information.

## H8-11 — Identity Criteria May Misclassify Machines

A quantum code, self-maintaining AI, or biological organ may satisfy coherence, closure, and persistence.

**Strengthening requirement:** treat those metrics as necessary structural diagnostics only and develop a noncircular account of reason-sensitive ownership.

## H8-12 — “Reason-Sensitive” Can Be Computed

Ordinary software can respond coherently to reasons and maintain identity.

**Strengthening requirement:** do not use behavioral sophistication alone as evidence of agency.

## H8-13 — Strong Presence May Be Mere Indeterminism

Replacing deterministic update with open actualization does not by itself explain presence.

**Strengthening requirement:** show why commit direction, record formation, and local actuality add explanatory or predictive structure beyond generic stochastic dynamics.

## H8-14 — The Quantum State as Record May Conflict with No-Cloning and Contextuality

Calling \(|\psi\rangle\) a record must not imply it is a locally readable classical data structure.

**Strengthening requirement:** define relational record as ontic constraint, not accessible copyable information.

## H8-15 — Boundary Action Threatens Closure

Fresh input can become an unfalsifiable repair mechanism.

**Strengthening requirement:** require explicit local channels, conservation ledgers, toggles, and falsifiers.

## H8-16 — Agency Across Time

If agency is never stored, what unifies successive acts as acts of the same person?

**Strengthening requirement:** identity must carry ownership continuity without turning the archived identity into the chooser.

## H8-17 — Agency During Reduced Consciousness

Sleep, anesthesia, infancy, dementia, and altered states challenge a simple on/off agency picture.

**Strengthening requirement:** model identity continuity separately from active presence episodes and allow graded expression without grading agency's ontological existence.

## H8-18 — Theological Overreach

Terms such as Grace, spirit, and forgiveness can outrun the mathematics.

**Strengthening requirement:** preserve claim-status labels and never use theological usefulness as physical evidence.

# Appendix B. Glossary

**Actualization:** The becoming of one lawful successor as the new actuality.

**Agency:** A selfhood-bearing regime's present ownership/orientation of a lawful act; not a stored variable.

**Affordance set \(\Xi_G\):** Coarse-grained lawful action orientations available to a regime.

**Boundary input:** An explicit local input entering through a declared channel.

**Commit:** The operation that writes an actualized result into the determinate record.

**Conscience-bearing regime:** Provisional term for a selfhood-bearing regime capable of integrated present appraisal and agentic orientation.

**Determinate record:** The complete set of committed local physical and relational facts available to later causal events.

**Identity:** A continuity relation that maps a regime across changing records.

**Open becoming:** A lawful event with more than one admissible successor and no assumed hidden prior selector.

**Participation aperture \(\Pi\):** Record-derived physical rate of realized participation/proper time.

**Possibility object \(\mathcal W\):** The lawful local structure of admissible successors, amplitudes, propensities, and constraints.

**Presence \(\mathfrak P\):** The local event by which a lawful possibility becomes actual.

**Proto-regime:** A coherent, persistent, locally closed organization not thereby assumed agentic.

**Record-mediated control:** Control that acts by changing committed causal-past conditions for later events.

**Strong presence:** Presence as actualization, distinguished from scalar participation rate.

**Structural history \(q\):** Mutable present record of retained drag/history effects.
