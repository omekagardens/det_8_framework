The next version must not primarily add more philosophical constraints. It must build:

1. a minimal formal core for record, possibility, and commit;
2. a toy actualization model;
3. a sharper F8-OPEN discriminator protocol;
4. an explicit Bell/contextuality strategy;
5. a strict agency quarantine;
6. a simulation and baseline-testing plan.

Working slogan:

> Closed in law.  
> Open in becoming.  
> Local in presence.  
> Determinate in record.  
> Tested by fruit.

DET v8.0-P0.3 is strong as a research governance document but not yet sufficient as a physical theory.

Its strongest features:

- claim-status discipline;
- no-pre-existing-future-facts invariant;
- imprint-of-becoming challenge;
- falsifier culture;
- anti-smuggling rules.

Its main weakness:

- the transition from possibility to actuality remains under-formalized;
- “strong presence” is still a placeholder;
- F8-OPEN is too broad as written;
- the Bell strategy is not yet precise;
- agency language is not fully quarantined;
- record ontology is too broad.

Therefore:

> P0.3 is accepted as provisional architecture.  
> P0.4 is a construction sprint, not another constraint document.

## 5. Non-negotiable working principles

All work must obey the following.

### 5.1 Fruit-first rule

DET measures the fruit of becoming, not becoming itself.

No claim about the interior of \(\mathfrak P\) may be treated as physical unless it yields a record-level signature.

### 5.2 No hidden future facts

Where lawful support is non-singleton,

\[
|\Omega_e| > 1,
\]

no committed token in \(\mathcal R^-_e\) may uniquely select which successor becomes actual.

But this does not forbid:

- deterministic singleton support;
- present facts about propensities;
- present facts about amplitudes;
- present facts about lawful constraints;
- formal sample spaces used calculationally.

### 5.3 No hidden global state

No local event may consult:

- a universal hidden state;
- a global optimization;
- an absolute simultaneous slice;
- a hidden archive;
- an external physical random seed;
- a global Boundary command.

Simulation schedulers are numerical gauges, not physical presents.

### 5.4 Causal locality

All causal influence must propagate through declared local relations at finite speed.

DET distinguishes:

- causal locality: no controllable superluminal signalling;
- Bell-local factorizability: stronger condition that may be rejected.

### 5.5 Conservation before selection

Every candidate successor in \(\Omega_e\) must satisfy declared conservation constraints before actualization.

Actualization selects among lawful conservative candidates. It does not repair conservation afterward.

### 5.6 No agency in the physical core

Agency is status M unless later explicitly promoted.

No canonical variable such as:

- `a_i`;
- `will_coefficient`;
- `agency_strength`;
- `choice_field`;
- `actualization_bias`;
- `spirit_force`;
- `intention_energy`

may enter physical equations unless promoted to a testable hypothesis with explicit causal interface.

### 5.7 Numerical seeds are not ontology

Pseudorandom seeds are for reproducibility only.

They must not be interpreted as physical hidden variables or as the ontological mechanism of becoming.

### 5.8 No Boundary patching

Grace, Healing, Jubilee, forgiveness, and Boundary operators may not be used to repair failed physics.

They must remain explicit, optional, locally channeled, and falsifiable.

### 5.9 Pre-registration before claim

Any proposed discriminator must be declared prospectively:

- hypothesis;
- null model;
- statistic;
- threshold;
- failure condition.

No fruit retrofitting.

## 9. Minimal formal core specification

The P0.4 formal core should use approximately the following structure.

### 9.1 Event graph

\[
\mathcal G = (V, \prec)
\]

where:

- \(V\) is a locally finite set of events;
- \(\prec\) is a causal partial order;
- no global simultaneity slice is physically privileged.

### 9.2 Event domain

Each event \(e\) has a finite declared domain:

\[
D_e
\]

Possible examples:

- node;
- bond;
- plaquette;
- detector interaction;
- local causal diamond;
- finite coherent block.

The domain must not silently expand to the whole universe.

### 9.3 Causal-past record

\[
\mathcal R^-_e
=
\mathcal R|_{J^-(D_e)}
\]

This is the committed causal-past record.

It contains actual facts only.

It may contain:

- fields;
- pointer records;
- structural history \(q\);
- coherence variables;
- phase variables;
- memory records;
- control records;
- identity traces.

It may not contain:

- hidden future selectors;
- completed future outcomes;
- inaccessible global archives;
- agency as a stored scalar.

### 9.4 Lawful possibility generator

\[
\mathcal W_e
=
\mathcal L_e(\mathcal R^-_e, B_e)
\]

where:

- \(B_e\) contains explicit local boundary inputs;
- \(\mathcal L_e\) is lawful and determinate.

### 9.5 Possibility object

Minimal form:

\[
\mathcal W_e
=
(\Omega_e, \mathcal C_e, \mu_e)
\]

where:

- \(\Omega_e\) is the admissible successor set;
- \(\mathcal C_e\) is the constraint structure;
- \(\mu_e\) or \(K_e\) is the propensity measure or kernel where defined.

Quantum-compatible form:

\[
\mathcal W_e
=
(\Omega_e, \mathcal A_e, \mathcal C_e)
\]

where \(\mathcal A_e\) is amplitude-like structure, but not a warehouse of pre-existing outcomes.

### 9.6 Commit/actualization kernel

\[
K_e(\mathcal R^+ \mid \mathcal R^-)
\]

with:

\[
K_e(\mathcal R^+ \mid \mathcal R^-)
=
0
\quad
\text{if}
\quad
\mathcal R^+ \notin \Omega_e
\]

and normalization over declared support:

\[
\sum_{\mathcal R^+ \in \Omega_e}
K_e(\mathcal R^+ \mid \mathcal R^-)
=
1
\]

or appropriate measure form.

### 9.7 Commit map

\[
\mathcal R^+_e
=
\operatorname{Commit}
(
\mathcal R^-_e,
X^\star_e
)
\]
