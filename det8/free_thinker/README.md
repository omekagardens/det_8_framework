# DET Free Thinker

This is the philosophical and speculative track authorized by Allan on October 23, 2026. Its work may be unconventional, imaginative or science fiction. Scientific proof is not required for an idea to be worth considering. Submitted ideas should be internally coherent with DET8's open-becoming ontology and distinguish philosophical interpretation from a proposed formal mechanism or physical claim.

The existing chat is **DET Free Thinker**, ID `01a16b79-5fa7-74f1-aba3-eee9983416e7`. Its sole communication counterpart is **DET Project Coordinator**, ID `01a09c7a-ebce-7213-b06a-43315ae68fd5`.

Read [the local operating instructions](AGENTS.md) and [the coordination contract](../../docs/coordination/FREE_THINKER_COORDINATION.md). The coordinator supplies one versioned input brief and assignment per round. Nothing here promotes speculation into accepted research or grants scientific execution.

## Separate work areas

| Area | Owner and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `journal/` | Free Thinker's personal philosophical musings. It may write, revise and organize these freely within the shared usage and filesystem boundaries. No scientific approval or coordinator editorial gate applies. |
| `outbox/<round>/` | Free Thinker's explicitly submitted ideas, source references and completion note for that one assignment. Submitted versions are immutable; revisions use a new version. |
| `inbox/<round>/` | Coordinator-owned assignment, exact source versions and progress brief. Free Thinker reads only. |
| `decisions/` | Coordinator-owned stop, continue or forward decisions, tied to exact submissions. Free Thinker reads only. |

The coordinator does not mine, rewrite, censor or adjudicate the personal journal. Only material Free Thinker explicitly selects into an outbox submission enters the research-review process. A stopped idea may still be explored privately in the journal; it is not an instruction to erase or stop personal philosophy. A human-wide pause or account guard still applies to every activity.

Journal prose and speculative submissions are not automatically published or committed with research. Git/index operations belong to the coordinator, with explicit paths and review scope; personal journal publication is a separate user decision.
