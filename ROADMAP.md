It includes identity channels and the ABSTAIN trade-off: conditional error tolerances summing below one force coverage to vanish along the rare-event limit. This limits uniform testing, not exact positive-scale law distinction, and supplies no physical validation or falsification.

**Separate synthetic application project.** The [diagnostic benchmark design](docs/applications/synthetic_channel_benchmark_v1/BENCHMARK_PLAN.md) is a frozen prospective specification using invented probabilities. Its candidate cannot beat the standard sequential reference on mean raw-observation cost in the core class; a future pass would establish only declared synthetic conformance. It authorizes no implementation, held-out generation or execution and supplies no practical-value or physical-validation success.

The [half-scale availability theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/HALF_SCALE_EVENT_AVAILABILITY.md) adds a conditional positive result for one actual eligible fixed prefix on its own fixed compact positive-lambda neighborhood under an optional whole-domain half-scale law. Its finite symbolic prospective raw cap requires exact iid access and retains error, abstention, coverage and power accounting. This establishes no numerical cap, actual eligible occurrence, cross-prefix or arbitrary-scale uniformity, or physical readout. The earlier fixed-budget limitation and original diagnostic design remain unchanged.

[The height-growth theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/HALF_SCALE_HEIGHT_GROWTH.md) adds almost-sure linear intrinsic height and cardinality exponent one for the selected all-layer half-scale baseline, conditional on the admitted prefix and infinite classical history measure. The proof uses conditional full-birth bounds without iid births. This optional actual-probability prescription is not a coordinate gauge; it gives no exact limiting speed, physical dimension or validation and does not settle the separate optimizer or infinite harmonic extension.

[The intrinsic target screen](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/INTRINSIC_GEOMETRY_TARGET_SCREEN.md) adds finite-time bounds and an almost-sure normalized deletion-distance lower bound for fixed sublinear-height targets under the selected all-layer half-scale baseline. It preserves the balanced thin-hook failure of aspect-only Ferrers control and leaves unrestricted Ferrers defect and optimizer asymptotics open. The law and target are optional; no physical readout, dimension or geometry bridge follows.

## Current State

**Record-Kernel Physics finite/computed program implemented** (T1–T7 + T2a + T6b + T6 residual + why-ℂ), with open assumptions and continuum results tracked explicitly. Key limits: T2a shows grade-2 is empirical rather than forced; T7 is estimator verification rather than manifoldlike emergence; the complex-structure result assumes one phase form and reversible dynamics.

**Conditional native mathematics is consolidated:** the [theorem package](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/THEOREM_PACKAGE.md) collects the exact admitted finite law, support, small-amplitude positive result, kernel alternatives and 30/33/38 exclusions. Its [law-dependence companion](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/LAW_DEPENDENCE.md) identifies which choices change the law. The stated premises and unresolved historical-coordinate/actual-branch questions remain.

**The bounded law-family classification is complete:** the [supplement](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/LAW_FAMILY_SUPPLEMENT.md) and [singular-law proof](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/SINGULAR_LAW_WIDTH.md) give rank ten and kernel dimension one throughout the declared eleven-column family. At any \(\chi=0\) law every harmonic direction is width-blind at \(P_1\); the admitted \(\theta\) bound excludes the formal compatible alternative. The [\(P_4\) witness](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/SINGULAR_OBSERVABLE_WITNESS.md), conditional on \(\Xi>1\), detects every nonzero kernel amplitude in its exact positive interval using all eight ideals and sixteen marked outcomes. This assumes complete child-order access and the parent embedding, and supplies no sampler, simulation result or physical detector. The [regular family](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/REGULAR_OBSERVABLE_FAMILY.md), conditional on \(\Xi>1\), continues that direction with \(v_{11}=1\), no division by \(\chi\), and a fixed positive amplitude on a neighborhood. The [full-layer half-scale theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/BASELINE_SCALE_THEOREM.md) proves continuity and a symbolic uniform absolute contrast bound on that neighborhood for the explicitly chosen half-scale continuation. This optional normalization is not forced by DET principles; an arbitrary positive scale need not have such a bound. No numerical effect or neighborhood radius is supplied. The [complete-row comparison](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/P4_OBSERVATION_SUFFICIENCY.md) shows that width preserves full-row total variation at and just below the conditional exception, but loses it just above on the admitted neighborhood. TV-optimality does not imply statistical sufficiency. A positive first-order absolute loss uses the selected half-scale rule. The [child-shape theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/P4_CHILD_SHAPE_OBSERVATION.md) removes the parent-embedding and newborn-identity requirements for this fixed regular pair: seven unmarked shapes retain full-row TV and pairwise statistical sufficiency. The minimal sufficient grouping has three classes at the conditional exception and four nearby. Exact complete-order access and the fixed parent class remain assumptions; no physical observation channel is supplied. The [nuisance-identifiability theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/P4_NUISANCE_IDENTIFIABILITY.md) shows that varying the baseline parameter and a positive common scale cannot reproduce the fixed regular perturbation when the original prefix, parent and full old marking are fixed. A ratio of two shape probabilities separates these model families. Their closures can meet as scale tends to zero, so exact identification supplies no uniform finite-sample guarantee; the earlier fixed-pair groupings are not sufficient for the whole nuisance family. The [hidden-marking mixture theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/P4_MARKING_MIXTURES.md) extends that separation to arbitrary, possibly different hidden old-mark distributions throughout the accepted neighborhood. It uses locality and the specific canonical held-law identities; the original prefix and parent remain fixed. Exact law separation still supplies neither a physical readout nor a uniform finite-sample guarantee. The [prefix-family theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/P4_PREFIX_FAMILY_SEPARATOR.md) gives a common score over a declared family of actual valid canonical prefixes, including measurable mixtures of admitted laws. The family may be a singleton, and each perturbed member needs its own justified regular construction. This extends the conditional scope without constructing another prefix or establishing an absolute gap, finite-sample guarantee or universal physical law. The [prospective event test](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/P4_DIAGNOSTIC_EVENT_TEST.md) adds explicit independent-draw and exact-classifier assumptions: collect 40,000 diagnostic events within 400,000 raw draws or abstain, with each conditional wrong-label probability below 5%. No event-availability or uniform raw-draw power guarantee follows; no implementation or experiment is admitted. The [observation-channel theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/P4_OBSERVATION_CHANNEL.md) gives explicit conditions for missed/confused labels to preserve a gap, while requiring a separate threshold certificate. Fixed false diagnostics from OTHER erase the uniform conditional gap in the rare-native-event limit; a justified relative-contamination bound can restore a restricted-class guarantee. No channel is calibrated and the original design is unchanged. Occurrence alone remains unselected: \(\Xi=\theta+360\mu_1-359\mu_0\ne1\) gives one exception iff \(\Xi>1\). This does not settle the distinct fixed-law 30/33/38 canonical branch. The [width diagnostic](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/WIDTH_DIAGNOSTIC.md) has a symbolic error guarantee but remains unexecuted without a numerical effect bound and sampler. A separate [optional approximate protocol](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/APPROXIMATE_WIDTH_DIAGNOSTIC.md) specifies sufficient error, dependence and abort certificates and their sample-cost penalty. It remains ABSTAIN / NOT RUN and does not alter the exact protocol or supply an implemented advantage. The [deterministic map component](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/CATEGORICAL_MAP.md) implements one rational-proxy partition with exact error certification and synthetic tests; actual-law inputs, source/channel certificates and benchmark execution remain outstanding. The [proxy-envelope design](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/PROXY_ENVELOPE.md) proves a conditional route from complete rational intervals to a normalized proxy and TV allowance; the separately reviewed [envelope implementation](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/ENVELOPE_IMPLEMENTATION.md) constructs exact rational proxies and error bounds from supplied intervals, with infeasibility, budget and resource dispositions. Actual-law interval validity, source/channel certificates and operational benchmark admission remain unestablished. The [complete-row admission design](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/ENVELOPE_ADMISSION.md) binds prospective interval evidence to the frozen diagnostic parent and distinguishes structural checks from trusted mathematical review. The separately reviewed [structural admission adapter](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/ENVELOPE_ADMISSION_IMPLEMENTATION.md) now checks a bounded two-arm profile against this design, with exact target/row/endpoint/reference binding and refusal tests. Scientific validity requires an explicit trusted-caller review premise; hashes do not supply it. No native interval data, sampler or operational admission is supplied.

**Positive contrast near a possible exception:** conditional on \(\Xi>1\), the [limit theorem](docs/track_b/native_theorem_package_v1/POSITIVE_CONTRAST_LIMIT.md) gives a local uniform quadratic upper bound on all strictly positive harmonic target-width contrasts on the same eleven-class support, regardless of normalization or amplitude.
